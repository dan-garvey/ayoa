Seraphel takes the chair beside yours, folding her wings clear of its back.

“Thank you. I’ll keep quiet for a while.  
Their card game’s become distinctly less than mild.”

Her slim book has a weathered blue cover: *The Winter Passage of Lord Halm*. A tiny painted ship lists beneath black waves; an inexplicable goat stands at its bow in a captain’s hat.

You return to Stone’s volume. The opening cases are painstakingly practical: disputed translations, altered treaty language, a river accord stalled for six months over the meaning of *customary*. Each record lists what was proposed, what was understood, and what each delegation actually consented to.

Professor Stone resumes his work at the central table. Beyond the library wall, a burst of laughter rises from the cards, followed by Dena’s unmistakable accusation that someone has “invented a rule halfway through losing.”

After several quiet minutes, Seraphel taps one finger against her book’s cover.

“Would you like a page once you’ve had enough  
of councils, consent, and procedural stuff?”
