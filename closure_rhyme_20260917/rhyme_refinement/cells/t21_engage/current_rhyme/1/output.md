Seraphel turns the slim book so you can see its faded green cover: *A Pilgrim’s Route by River and by Road*.

“ A merchant wrote it while avoiding what he owed.  
He calls each wrong turn wisdom from the skies;  
his mule kicks a priest, which improves it by surprise.”

She opens to a small ink illustration of a crowded ferry listing in the rain. The mule is visible near the rail, rendered with an expression of unmistakable discontent.
