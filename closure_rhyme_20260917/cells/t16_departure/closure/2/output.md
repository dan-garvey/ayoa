Ashara gives a short nod. “I intend to.”

The remaining plates arrive in a steadier rhythm, and the table’s attention disperses with them. Rashid finishes quickly, then rises when the servers begin clearing the roast.

“I owe people cards,” he says to Ashara. “Tomorrow, then.”

“Tomorrow,” she agrees.

He leaves through the common-room arch. Dena’s voice reaches the dining hall almost at once: “There he is. Deal him in before he invents another obligation.”

Ashara drinks the last of her water and pushes back her chair. “I’ll find someone at the octagon.”

Caelindra looks at the half-cleared table. “Or discover the remarkable restorative properties of one evening without drills.”

Ashara’s glance is cool. “You should try exertion sometime.”

“I’ve watched you do enough for both of us.”

Seraphel gathers her napkin neatly beside her plate.

“The meal has reached its end;  
the house may now unbend.”
