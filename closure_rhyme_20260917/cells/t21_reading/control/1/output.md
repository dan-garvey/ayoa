Seraphel takes the chair beside yours and lays her slim book on the table. Its cover is plain blue cloth, softened at the corners from use.

“Thank you. I thought their game was quiet at the start;  
Dena has a fiercely competitive heart.”

From the common room comes Dena’s triumphant, “That is not how turns work,” followed by Rashid’s patient reply, too low to make out.

Seraphel opens her book but glances at the brown volume Professor Stone left you.

“Records of the Human Seat,” she reads. “A sober kind of art.  
Has Stone assigned you all of it, or only where you start?”
