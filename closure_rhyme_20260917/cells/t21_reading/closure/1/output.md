Seraphel takes the chair, folding her wings close so their tips clear the table.

“Thank you. I’ll keep still.  
The game next door has grown shrill.”

Her book is a thin, cloth-bound collection called *Journeys Mislaid*. She opens it beside your borrowed history while Professor Stone returns to his brittle papers at the far table.

The human-seat record is drier than the title suggests. Its first pages describe an office built around precision: ensuring a treaty had been heard in a shared language, recording consent, and stopping a proceeding when a party claimed not to understand a term. There is no mention of a requirement for magic.

From the common room, Dena’s voice carries through the wall.

“Rashid, you cannot infer the rules from your cufflinks.”

“I wasn’t—”

Laughter cuts him off.

Seraphel turns a page in her own book and lets out a small laugh.

“The captain says a fog  
Stole every map, then blames the dog.”

She settles into reading, lamp-glow warming the edge of her feathers. The two open books wait beneath your hands while the card game continues noisily next door.
