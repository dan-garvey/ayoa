Professor Stone’s gaze rests on your face for a moment.

“Food and clothes are not small questions,” he says. “They’re the questions people arranging a grand future often forget to answer.”

He slides the brown volume across the table. “Garvey House’s trust covers your room, meals, and course materials. Your lawyers should handle the rest, though I wouldn’t assume thoroughness merely because their stationery is expensive.”

He taps the first heading. “Read through page eighteen. The notes in the margins are from an earlier student and frequently wrong. The book stays in the library.”

Beyond the wall, Dena announces that Rashid has played a card he cannot possibly defend. Stone closes his brittle folder and looks back at you.

“Did the lawyers give you an inventory of what you inherited, or only papers naming you heir?”
