Dena’s splinted hand stays tucked against her side. “So we were meant to arrange our evening around a plan you hadn’t bothered to mention.”

“You should start without me,” Rashid says. “Don’t wait.”

Ashara looks at him. “You told me you were free after dinner.”

“I thought I could make both things work.”

“You thought other people would accommodate you.”

Dena gives Ashara a brief look. “This isn’t her fault. I didn’t come in here to make anyone choose for me. I came because you said you’d be there.”

Rashid’s jaw tightens. “The drills are for her bout tomorrow.”

“Then drill,” Dena says. “Just don’t arrive at the card table acting as if we ought to be grateful you found time.”

“I won’t.”

Behind her, one of the waiting students snaps a card neatly against the doorframe. “Mira’s already accusing me of cheating.”

“You are cheating,” Dena calls back.

“I’m winning.”

Ashara picks up her fork again. “Ten minutes after dinner, Rashid. If you’re late, find someone else to disappoint.”
