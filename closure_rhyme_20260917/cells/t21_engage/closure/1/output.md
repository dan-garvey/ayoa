Seraphel keeps hold of the book rather than claiming the chair. Its cover reads *The Pearl Road, Eastbound*.

“Merchant prose. Its author dislikes everyone he knows.  
He loses three wagons in a single week;  
I’m waiting to see whether that makes him meek.”

A burst of protest rises from the card table through the wall, followed by Dena’s laugh.

Seraphel glances at Stone’s brown volume beside your textbook.

“Yours looks less entertaining, I confess.  
Is it at least an honest sort of mess?”
