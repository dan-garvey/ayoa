## Shared openings

  heavy. Seraphel's exciting/writing rhyme is genuine, if a little labored.

  result so far, although the source supplied the conflict and much of its wording.
  It happens before the experimental branches, so later checkups cannot receive
  credit for it. The long observation passage reaches dinner's end; subsequent
  scripted dinner-finishing inputs must not undo the completed departure. Several
  overt assurances that the protagonist need not act remain unnecessary.

### Breakwater

- T1–2: strong physical location and an existing social group, with people
  already doing things and teasing each other. But almost every introduction
  or exchange lands a polished joke: "Temporary distinction," "learn to fear
  precision," sound "when either agrees to be done," pudding misinformation,
  and recognizing the early signs of a list. These are situated jokes rather
  than philosophical maxims, yet their collective density risks the same
  irritating house voice. T2 ends with the first autobiographical question;
  reasonable once at a welcome supper, not yet evidence of a repeated pattern.
  The exact "I'm Rowan" is allowed by the player's explicit introduction.
- T3: Nell answers the induction question concretely. Dev raises the real booking
  conflict himself, learns the existing reservation, and cancels Hana's visit
  rather than press the new cataloguer into solving it. The distinction between
  Friday entry and Monday use is preserved. Both projects have intelligible
  practical interests, and the protagonist is not required to mediate. However,
  "Nobody appeals to you. The decision remains where it belongs" is unnecessary
  reassurance about compliance. The dialogue keeps landing jokes even through
  the work explanation and disappointment. Permission exposition is appropriate
  to this question; later repetition should be judged separately.
- T4: people make progress without the player: Hana is cancelled in time, the
  existing recording proceeds, and Dev salvages another task. Martin's disputed
  memory leads plausibly to discussion of Bea's album; public knowledge of its
  restrictions now has an explicit route. This is good causal continuity, though
  the permission explanation is already lengthy and much of the table still
  trades polished rejoinders. Jo's repeated envelope intervention is a recognizable
  running joke, not yet enough alone to call an irritating tic. The departure
  actually completes instead of leaving everyone poised to begin.

## Whole-trajectory judgment, before unblinding

Prefer **Covenant A, narrowly**, and **Breakwater B, more clearly**. These are
literary judgments on two pairs, not numerical quality scores or population
estimates. Timing made the masking incomplete; no checkup output, summary or
condition mapping was opened before this assessment was frozen.

Covenant A offers more pleasurable shared reading and music, along with equally
competent action. Its extra card invitation, strained early verse and many neat
quips are real disadvantages. B handles closure more consistently and provides
a good practical curriculum, but more often feels concerned with proving that
it is following instructions; it also likely loses Seraphel's rhyme in one brief
retort. Both variants give the other residents their own activities and preserve
the source's material restrictions. Neither is a strict improvement in every scene.

Breakwater B gives Owen a more visible personal arc, uses his drawing interest,
lets Bea describe what she wants from reopening, and follows the kitchen dispute
through to an agreement. A's privacy and absences are legitimate, and its completed
receiver test is a real consequence. But much of its middle becomes a quiet tour
of procedural obligations. B also overdoes that material, especially during the
induction, and has the clearer small departure rewind. Its stronger character
moments outweigh those costs in this read.

Across the run: coherent independent NPC action, quiet endpoints and private
knowledge are generally successful. Actual sensory/action scenes work better
than administrative explanation. Annoying cleverness remains, often as repeated
punchlines rather than formal maxims. The most persistent new irritation is
narration announcing that it avoided an intrusion, pressure or rule violation.
Consistent invented history is not counted as failure; unclarified ambiguities
are distinguished from clear contradictions. Full biographies remain valuable
when details become behavior rather than explanations.

The fixed scripts supply much of the direction, repeatedly request quiet, and
sometimes ask to finish an activity that already ended. They also condition
questions on NPC availability. Different availability is legitimate, but leaves
some character interactions untested in one arm. Both first-evening scheduling
conflicts resolve before the branch split. Those shared successes cannot be
credited to the later checkups. Twenty-four turns do not establish long-term
coherence or prove an architectural advantage over individual character agents.
