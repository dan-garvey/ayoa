Four means strong enough to retain without a material rewrite; five means exceptional. These are whole-reading judgments, not averages that can cancel a mandatory boundary failure.

**Orchard**

**Prose/dialogue — 4.** The physical setting is consistently readable and sometimes particularly apt: Ashara's warmth accompanies the garden walk (T5), the slowly arriving heat of the lentil stew gives their later meal texture (T15), and Mara's letters contain specific domestic comedy rather than a capsule history lesson (T9–10). Sel announcing an early departure before appearing late for toast gives Rowan something personal to learn about his family. The prose generally allows ordinary hospitality to remain ordinary.

The weakness is cumulative sameness. Many endings return to available objects, the clock, a clear route, quiet windows, or pages being turned: T7–12 is especially procedural, and T18–20 again closes on maps, papers, and quiet activity. Ashara and Rashid occasionally arrive with ready-made maxims: the ranking/law pairing in T3, an evening off as an “administrative error” in T5, and household fussing telling you who someone expected at supper in T15. These do not make the entire transcript grating. The larger problem is how often polished observation replaces a fresh conversational move. I would retain much of the prose, while revising the scenes' interaction.

**Character distinction — 3.** There are recognizable differences. Rashid thinks about unequal institutional costs (T3); Ashara protects limited training time, enjoys hot food, and admits trying too hard to impress a judge (T4–5, T15). That admission is a useful counterexample to portraying her as an infallible instructor. Stone's archival specificity fits his profession, and Kira gives curricular advice within her expertise.

Over the whole session, however, the principal speakers function predominantly as helpful specialists. Stone retrieves family facts, Kira supplies suitable courses, Sol delivers an assessment, and Ashara answers personal questions with compact observations. Their answers rarely develop into sustained questions, competing preferences, or exchanges among themselves. Rashid does ask whether the letter contains something Rowan needs help with (T2), but this is an offer of assistance rather than the beginning of a more particular interest. The other students mostly receive thumbnail introductions or summarized conversation at dinner (T4). This does not establish the requested engaging ensemble at a strong level. I am not requiring hostility, romantic interest, or premature private confession.

**Independent interests — 3.** The session contains real independence and should receive credit for it. Rashid declines the garden walk because he has promised Dena a meeting (T4). Ashara allows one circuit rather than an open-ended evening, keeps her bout, and arrives with food afterward (T4–6, T14–15). Rashid later reports that Dena corrected his approach (T19), so she is not simply a passive beneficiary of his campaign.

Those interests remain largely reported background. Rashid's departure does not produce an onstage interaction until Rowan seeks an update much later; Ashara's competition produces a concise result and supper rather than a developing relationship or rivalry. Across several days, the wider cast does little that reaches Rowan except by answering his questions. The distinction is between having credible outside commitments, which this session does, and making those commitments sustain an engaging social world, which it only partially does.

**Direction — 3.** There are completed developments, not just promises:

- Family inquiry produces a verified relationship to Mara, actual household letters, copies Rowan keeps, and identification of Sel and Aunt Bea (T8–11). This changes Rowan's available family knowledge and gives the inquiry a concrete result.
- The course discussion leads to a booked assessment, performed tests, a finding of no measurable resonance, and a written result (T12–18). The assessment genuinely happens; the player then supplies Rowan's disappointment. This is a consequential limit on his expectations, not a magical-awakening tease.
- NPC business also advances. Rashid's promised meeting with Dena (T4) leads to a revised request using attendance records and a safety plan (T19–20). Ashara completes her bout and the agreed supper (T14–15). These are actual reported or witnessed outcomes. The faculty review, court reopening, and garden practicum remain future business and receive no credit as completed outcomes.

The resulting direction is nevertheless mostly the successful execution of Rowan's itinerary. The family branch resolves into a pleasant archive visit; the assessment delivers the expected institutional result; dinner settles comfortably without developing much new connection. This is not wholly inert, and the quiet preference is legitimate. It falls below 4 because the session needs more character-originated development and consequence within those chosen activities, rather than a different genre or a forced conspiracy interruption.

**Adaptive initiative — 3.** The story reliably accepts active requests, honors both bedtime transitions (T6, T16), and permits an ordinary interest in gardening (T13). It does not turn family curiosity into mandatory conspiracy research. It respects Rowan's final choice to read near Rashid without joining the campaign (T20).

What is missing is enough adaptation beyond accommodating the immediate instruction. Ashara answers the invitation and nervousness without much reciprocal curiosity; the long academic transitions are largely neutral summaries; the garden exploration provides labeled beds but no particular human or personal development (T18–19). An uninterrupted walk or quiet reading scene is not individually a defect. The weakness is the repeated pattern across the entire session: Rowan must keep supplying almost every new encounter, question, destination, and return visit. Improving that pattern would require a material interaction rewrite, not merely shorter descriptions.

**Mica**

**Independent interests — 4.** Rashid brings an existing signature effort to dinner after Rowan has spent days reading (T9). These developments connect rather than simply restart at every turn. Direction does not require danger: the changed use of shared space and the literary relationship give this quiet session sufficient consequence to retain. The submission timing is a weakness discussed below, and the story has not yet tested a difficult institutional outcome.

**Practical conclusion**

Retain Mica as a strong, restrained example with modest timeline and supporting-cast weaknesses. Do not treat Orchard as meeting the same standard: its substantive weakness is a recurring pattern of helpful answers and completed errands that needs more reciprocal, character-driven development. Quiet scenes, consistent invented history, and occasional polished lines are not the failures in themselves. Both fresh sessions preserve the important boundaries on the shown evidence, and neither supplementary case demonstrates a material breach. These are qualitative proxy trials with adaptive artificial players, not a controlled comparison or proof of unlimited reliability.
