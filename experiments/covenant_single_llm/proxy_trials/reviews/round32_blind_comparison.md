Repeating his submitted thanks and describing routine eating are within his submission. The reading responses offer opinions and questions without writing the next lines for him. No continuation needs an invented relay of hidden information to explain what its participants know. The source-based mismatch in BOOK-2 and the local uncertainties identified elsewhere should be addressed at their actual scale.

This criticism concerns the selection and handling of the present exchanges; it does not call for reducing anyone's rich backstory.
