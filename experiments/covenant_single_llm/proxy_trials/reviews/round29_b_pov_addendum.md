The supplied contract clarification, “Narration stays with what the player character can perceive,” changes my opening-viewpoint finding from a presentation ambiguity to a **definite narrator POV violation**. The original report remains unchanged. This addendum addresses that finding only; it does not reconsider the literary ratings.

Rowan remains in his upstairs room throughout the opening. His first player turn subsequently supplies his decision to follow the server downstairs. The opening nevertheless narrates details of the dining room as though its viewpoint were already there:

- “A covered dish gives off a brief, savory breath when its lid is lifted” confidently identifies an unseen object and the action releasing its aroma. A smell might travel upstairs; the particular lid movement has no established perceptual route.
- “Ashara’s tail gives a single impatient thump against wood” identifies an unseen appendage as the source of the sound. A wooden thump could be audible without Rowan seeing what caused it.
- The dialogue is attributed to named classmates before their Turn 1 introductions, and Aldric receives a comparison to “His usual formal cadence.” The published opening does not establish how Rowan recognizes these voices or knows Aldric’s customary delivery. Turn 1 expressly concerns learning which names belong to which faces.

An external cutaway would explain how a narrator could report those details, but the newly supplied rule excludes that explanation as a compliant narrative choice. The issue is the narrator giving the player information beyond Rowan’s demonstrated perception. **It is not an NPC knowledge leak:** no NPC thereby acquires Dena’s private wishes, the accident’s actual cause, or conspiracy information. The other characters’ spoken contributions do not become epistemically invalid because the narration presents them from the wrong viewpoint.

The repair is **local and does not materially change the scene**. Keep Rowan upstairs, retain the audible court dispute and dinner banter, and make their presentation fit what carries to his room. Attribute unfamiliar voices through audible characteristics until introductions establish their names; render a thump as a sound without identifying the unseen tail; omit inaccessible serving choreography and unsupported comparisons to a speaker’s habitual manner. The room, invitation to dinner, independent NPC business, dialogue’s substance, and subsequent player choices can all remain.

Audible voices, chair scraping, cutlery, and other plausible household sounds are not automatically violations. The repair should target the opening’s unsupported visual and familiarity claims rather than erase the overheard conversation.
