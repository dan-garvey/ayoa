You turn the frame. The tiny date in the upper corner has browned almost to the paper; Stone lowers a pair of reading lenses and leans close enough to read without touching the glass.

“His ankle. Tomas climbed a garden wall in Veyr to steal apples from a tree already dropping them into the lane. His mother cancelled the train. Miriam received the note, cursed the wasted cake, and went to him herself the next month with what was left in a tin. She said he slept through half the visit.”

Stone’s mouth pulls briefly at one corner. He moves a stack of catalog slips from the chair opposite his desk. “She would have hated seeing this framed. Sit, if you have nowhere else to be. I can give you a better account of her than a solicitor can.”
