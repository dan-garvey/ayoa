"Thank you, Ashara. And thank you for coming to get me."

I go back to my room and read the whole letter carefully, including the signature, before carrying the frame down to the library. At the upper gallery I pause where the person at the lit desk can see me, without leaning over his work.

"Professor Stone? I'm Rowan Garvey. Ashara thought you might be able to tell me about the person who wrote this. Have you a little time?"
