Thessaly leans over the board and touches the two small circles beside its center.

“A lantern moves up to two marked spaces on a turn. It may stop sooner, but it cannot pass through another lantern. If you take one from your hand, it enters on either of these circles and then makes its move from there.”

She traces the route she used with one narrow finger: center, first mark, second mark. “My amber lantern began here. I placed a tile that continued its road, then moved it two spaces. You may start a blue lantern now, or move one you have already started once there is one on the board.”

Her hand withdraws from the map. “The card ought to say that more clearly. It was written by someone who already knew the game.”
