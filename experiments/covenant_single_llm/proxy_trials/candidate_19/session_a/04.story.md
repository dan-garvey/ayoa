The server places a shallow dish of roasted vegetables and dark bread in the space left by your bowl. Ashara waits until he has moved on before answering.

“You may watch,” she says. “My booking begins at seventh bell. Use the north gallery; it has a clear view of the court and keeps you out of anyone's path. I will be preparing, not explaining what I am doing as I do it.”

She tears a piece of bread in half. “When I am finished, I can spare ten minutes to show you how to hold a practice blade without injuring your wrist. After that, you can decide whether you want lessons badly enough to make time for them.”

Rashid looks across the table at her. “Ten minutes is almost a welcome ceremony.”

“It is ten minutes,” Ashara says. “Do not turn it into one.”

Thessaly reaches for the serving spoon. “Is the north gallery a place where visitors may sit without agreeing to anything?”

“Yes,” Ashara says.

“Useful.” Thessaly serves herself two roasted carrots. “I have sat through several events where the answer was less clear.”

Ashara's attention returns to you. “Seventh bell, then, if you mean to come.”
