Thessaly closes the lacquered box with both hands. “Of course. Good night, Rowan.”

The other goodnights follow in their different voices. Rashid answers from behind his fresh page without looking up; Seraphel gives a small lift of her fingers and says, “Rest well. / The house is quieter / after midnight.” Ashara leaves by the opposite door with a brief nod, while the common room begins to settle around Rashid's scratching pen and the soft click of Thessaly arranging game pieces for herself.

Upstairs, the frame comes down from its hook more easily than it looked as though it would. The paper beneath the glass has browned at the edges, but the handwriting remains neat and forceful.

The Academy primer is thinner than its embossed cover suggests. Its opening pages reduce the Sundering War and the Covenant's founding to clean dates, seven seats, and a list of duties for future representatives. The section on conduct gives Article Nineteen a single severe paragraph: procreation between signatory races is forbidden, and its penalties are set by Covenant law. Nothing in the careful print offers a reason beyond the article number and its citation.

For the next half hour, the primer moves through the Academy's tracks, the bridges' travel permissions, the dining customs, and the schedule for the coming week. When the time has passed, the lamp goes out. The room dims to the pale rectangle of the window, then to the distant white of the bridge beyond the gardens, and finally to dark.
