Stone accepts your thanks with a small nod and turns back to the papers on his desk. The brass frame and copied index make a careful, light burden on the way upstairs. Back in your room, Lenora's letter returns to its place beside the primer, while the copy remains close enough to read without taking it down.

Ysolde is in the common room when you come back down. She sits near the garden-facing window with a thin volume open across her lap, though her attention is on the storm beyond the glass. Firelight does little to warm the pale line of her profile.

At your words, she turns from the window and considers the request without making it smaller through politeness.

“Yes,” she says. “But not tonight. At night, the terrace is wind and lamps. The green requires the sun low behind you and clouds low enough to catch the garden light from beneath.” She closes the volume around one finger to keep her place. “Tomorrow should work if the upper wind does not clear the sky. I go shortly before sunset. If you are at the west stair then, I will show you.”

Her gaze returns to the storm. “Wear a coat you do not mind fastening tightly. The wind finds the gaps in them.”
