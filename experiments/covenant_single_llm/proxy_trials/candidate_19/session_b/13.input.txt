“I'll be there. The coat and I have survived two winters together; hopefully it won't desert me now.” I leave Ysolde to her book and get an early night. The next day I attend the meetings with Professors Windwhisper and Kira, keeping my course sheet tucked into the primer. Shortly before sunset, I button my coat all the way up and go to the west stair to meet Ysolde. I arrive a little early so she won't have to wait for me.
