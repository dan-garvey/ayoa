I thank the others for dinner, then tell Rashid, “I'm going to try the library while the professor's still up. I'll find you about the petition afterward.” I go upstairs to fetch the letter in its brass frame. At the library door, I knock lightly. “Professor Stone? I'm Rowan Garvey. Seraphel thought you might recognise this.” I hold the frame by its edges. “My parents died when I was small. I haven't much family history to bring with me, and I'd like to know who Willan was.”
