“I'd still like to try.” I use the blank sheet to write:

Dear Willan,
My name is Rowan Garvey. I'm twenty-three, and I've just arrived at the Academy. A letter from Lenora was waiting in my room; Professor Stone helped me learn that we're related. I lost my parents young and know very little about this side of my family. I've spent some time copying letters, so finding a bookbinding apprentice among my relatives made me smile. If this finds you, I would be glad to hear about you, your work, or whatever you care to tell me. You can write to me at Garvey House, care of the Academy.
Yours sincerely,
Rowan

I read it twice, then make myself stop fiddling with the sentences. “Would you help me send the inquiry with this? I'd rather give it the chance than wonder.”
