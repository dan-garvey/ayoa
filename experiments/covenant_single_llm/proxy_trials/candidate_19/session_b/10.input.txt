“It isn't nothing,” I say. “A person who wrote complaints about fruit, and a boy learning to bind books. I can imagine them.” I pick up the copy and look for a date beside Willan's address. “I'd like to keep this, if I may. And I'd like to write to him if there's any chance of the letter finding him. Even if all I manage is to introduce myself.” I look up at Stone. “How old is the address?”
