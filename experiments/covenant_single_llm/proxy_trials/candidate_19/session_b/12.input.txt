“Thank you for doing this.” I leave the sealed letter with Stone and bring the framed letter and the index copy back to my room. I put the frame where it stood before and keep the copy beside it. The room feels a little less borrowed now.

Then I go down to the common room and look for Ysolde. Her account of the storm has stayed with me. If I find her, I say, “I've been thinking about those green clouds you described. Would you show me the western terrace sometime? I'd like to see it with someone who knows what she's looking at.”
