The court dispute thins out without quite ending. Rashid sets a small pencil beside his plate; Aldric leaves his own bowl untouched for a time, then resumes eating. A server clears the soup and brings glazed dishes of peppered squash and beans. Thessaly takes a second roll from the basket before it reaches Seraphel, who gives her a look of mild surprise and accepts half when it is offered.

After a little while, you turn to Ashara. “You said your booking is tomorrow. Is there somewhere spectators can sit? I'd like to see what training here looks like.”

Ashara considers you over the rim of her glass. “The east gallery of the covered court. It has benches and a rail. The ranked bout is public, and my booking begins at nine if you want to see the drills beforehand.”

Rashid glances over from his notes. “The gallery is a better view than the ground, unless someone sets the west line on fire.”

“No one is setting anything on fire,” Ashara says. “Stay behind the rail until a court officer tells you otherwise.”
