“Thank you.” You try the broth, sweet with onion beneath the thyme. When Rashid has taken a dark roll from the basket, you pass it along the table.

“Not the injury report,” Rashid says. He breaks the roll cleanly in two. “I have accounts from the students who were there. They agree someone was hurt. They disagree about whether it was equipment, supervision, or an ordinary fall.”

“Nor have I seen it,” Aldric says. “The faculty notice gave no cause.”

Ashara lifts her spoon, then sets it back in her bowl. “My booking tomorrow does not answer the question. It is not the group, and it is not the accident.”

“No,” Rashid says. “But a blanket restriction has consequences while they decide whether to explain it.”

“It may,” Aldric replies. “That does not make a petition an accident report.”

Rashid looks from him to the bread basket as it makes its way past Caelindra. “I will request the report in the morning before I ask anyone to sign. The students who train deserve facts as much as the Academy does.”

“Good,” Ashara says, and returns to her soup.
