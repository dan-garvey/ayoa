Breakfast passes beneath a pale, storm-lit morning. Before nine, you find the covered court beyond the dueling ground: a high-roofed hall of black stone and iron, with a fresh Academy notice fixed beside its main doors. The east gallery is open above the sand. You take a bench behind its waist-high rail while a court officer checks the empty seats and turns back to the floor.

Ashara is already training below. She wears a quilted black jacket over close-fitted practice clothes, her horns clear of the high collar. A blunted sword moves through her hands in exact repetitions: step, cut, turn; a low sweep that skims the sand; a retreat that ends with her weight set and the point waiting. Heat gathers briefly along the blade with each faster sequence, then fades before the next begins. She spends nearly an hour on the pattern, stopping only to drink water and change the weight of the weapon.

Her opponent arrives with the ranking officer: a broad-shouldered student in a gray padded coat carrying a practice spear. They bow across the marked square. At the officer's raised hand, the spear snaps forward. Ashara catches the shaft on her blade and gives ground; the spear-tip taps her shoulder before she can clear it, and a small red light flares on the score-post.

The next exchange is shorter. Ashara lets the spear pass high, steps inside its reach, and marks the gray coat at the ribs. On the third, she gives the same opening, then turns the spear aside with both hands and touches the blunt point of her sword beneath her opponent's chin. The score-post answers with a second red flare. Her opponent comes in harder after the reset. Ashara's blade rings against the spear twice, then catches its shaft near the middle; she twists, drives forward, and lands the final mark at the chest.

The officer calls the bout for Kothren. Ashara and her opponent bow again before the gray-coated student retrieves the dropped spear. Ashara wipes the sand from her blade, looks up to the east gallery, and finds you behind the rail. She raises two fingers toward the court door, then turns back to speak with the ranking officer.
