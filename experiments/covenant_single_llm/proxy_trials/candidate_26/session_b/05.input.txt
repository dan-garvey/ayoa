I look from the closed gate to the bird beside my crown. “I was counting gates as though each one was progress. I see the difficulty.”

After a moment, I smile at Thessaly. “I enjoyed that. I'd like another round some evening, after I've slept and stopped assuming every open doorway is an invitation.”

I ask where the pieces belong and help put them away as she directs. “Who taught you to play?”

I listen to her answer, then spend a little while sitting quietly by the fire. Before I get too sleepy, I wish Thessaly goodnight and make my way back upstairs to my room.
