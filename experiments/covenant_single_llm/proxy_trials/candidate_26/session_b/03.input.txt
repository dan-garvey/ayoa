“The ones where I could have worked it out,” I tell Caelindra. “Usually I haven't, but I like being able to go back and find what I missed. A detective suddenly producing a secret twin makes me feel I've been charged for a game nobody explained.”

I glance at the cover. “You've rather sold me the tiger, though. I might forgive a good deal for a tiger in a conservatory. Could I borrow it when you've finished? I'd be interested to find out whether I care about the cousins once the map's turned up.”

I try the broth while it's hot. After a few mouthfuls, I ask the table, “What were you all planning to do after supper? I'm still at the stage where finding the right staircase counts as an evening's achievement.”
