“Rowan is fine,” I tell the server, and thank them for coming up.

I leave the envelopes on the desk and go downstairs. At the entrance to the common room, I look for a gap in the conversation rather than pretending I haven't heard it.

“Evening. I'm Rowan Garvey. Would you mind some company? I can contribute ignorance about the court, and a fairly serious interest in supper.”
