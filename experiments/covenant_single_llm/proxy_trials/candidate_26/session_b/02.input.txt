“If there was a primer among the lawyers' envelopes, I haven't reached it yet,” I admit to Caelindra as I go to my place. “I know I've inherited a house. I don't even know whether I've inherited any magic. So please assume the gaps are considerable.”

When there's a chance to address Rashid without talking over someone, I add, “And I wouldn't sign the court request tonight. I don't understand what happened or what opening it would involve. I'd rather tell you that plainly.”

I help myself to bread and turn back to Caelindra. “Was that a novel you were reading? I like a good mystery, though I've an unfortunate habit of suspecting whoever owns the largest house.”
