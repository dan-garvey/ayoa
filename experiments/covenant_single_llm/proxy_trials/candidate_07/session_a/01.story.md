“Thank you.”

The server inclines her head and leads you down past the common room, where firelight catches in the varnish of the dead Garveys' portraits. The dining hall beyond is bright with candles and working lamps. Eight places are occupied at the long table; the remaining settings make the room feel larger than it is.

Your card waits midway down one side, between a pale, black-eyed woman with blue-black hair and a gold-lit angel whose small wings rest close behind her chair. You take the seat as the others look up.

“I'm Rowan,” you say. “Please tell me one of you is also pretending to understand how this place works.”

The wolf-blooded man across from you sets a folded sheet of names beside his plate. Fine fur darkens the backs of his hands. “Rashid vel Amara. I understand the dining hall. Beyond that, I have a working theory.”

“Thessaly Morrow,” says the woman at your left. “I understand some of it.”

The angel beside her smiles.

“Seraphel Dawnquill. The corridors bend and turn,\n+But supper's where the lost return.”

At the head of the nearer cluster, the horned woman in black lifts her glass. Heat shimmers faintly above the wine near her hand. “Ashara vel Kothren. Ask when you don't know something. Guessing wastes time.”

“Ysolde Thornmantle,” says the white-haired woman beside her. “The soup is good.”

Caelindra Vaeyn's pointed ears tilt toward you as she smooths one silver sleeve. “That may be the first useful orientation anyone has offered.”

“Aldric Verantus,” says the last man, precise even in the simple introduction. “Welcome to Garvey House.”

A server sets down a covered tureen. Ashara's gold eyes settle on you again. “What did your primer actually tell you?”
