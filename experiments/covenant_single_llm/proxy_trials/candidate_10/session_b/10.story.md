The two lamps keep their opposing pull while the sky darkens around them. Below, a white thread of lightning opens and vanishes inside the cloudbank.

Ysolde is quiet long enough for the wind to lift the edge of her notebook page.

"At the end of summer," she says. "Before term began. At home."

She closes the notebook with one hand.

"I have not flown since. On the Nexus, someone would see, and then everyone would decide they knew why I had done it."

Her gaze stays on the place where the lamps bend in different directions.

"Watching is enough today."
