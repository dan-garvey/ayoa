The path from the north ground joins the quadrangle beneath the low dragon-carved arches. Dena keeps her splinted hand tucked close while she walks, letting the practice crowd flow ahead of her.

"A good evening?" She considers it. "The west gardens, after the lamps come up. There is a tea cart by the wall. The tea is terrible, but the pastry cook sends over whatever won't survive until breakfast."

She glances toward the library, where students are already climbing its front steps with books under their arms.

"People go there when they want to complain about classes without pretending it is study. You can sit by the wall and listen, if that is what you want. Or talk. No one makes you choose in advance."

The corner of her mouth lifts.

"I'm going after I get this dirt out of my boots. You can come along if you want."
