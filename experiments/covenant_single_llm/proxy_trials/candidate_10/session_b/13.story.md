The final whistle sends the pairs apart. Practice blades go back into canvas cases; the supervisor checks off names while the chalk lines blur under scuffing boots.

Dena turns when you approach. Her splint is wrapped in dark leather and brass, leaving the fingers of her injured hand free. She shifts a padded sleeve beneath her other arm.

"Dena," she says. "Hi."

She looks over the north ground, where two students are arguing quietly over whose turn it is to carry the water bucket.

"It's cold, and half of us will track mud into our rooms. But there is a supervisor, and nothing is going to come loose over our heads. I call that an improvement."

Her mouth pulls briefly to one side.

"I want the covered court fixed, not forgotten. Until then, this does."

She tucks the padded sleeve under her uninjured arm and starts toward the path back to the quadrangle.

"If you come to watch again, stand upwind. You can hear the counts better."
