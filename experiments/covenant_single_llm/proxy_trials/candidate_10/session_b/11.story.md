Ysolde remains at the rail until the dinner bell carries across the quadrangle, following the lamps as they steady and then begin to lean together.

The next two days settle into bells, classrooms, meals, and the repeated creak of Garvey House's stairs. By the third evening, the dining hall has acquired its own habits: Rashid's folio beside his plate, Ashara's practice case against the wall, Aldric arriving with his cuffs still buttoned to the wrist.

Rashid has a folded Academy notice open between the salt cellar and the bread basket.

"I got the instructor's report," he says. "A tether bracket failed. It should have been replaced before the practice group used the court."

Aldric looks down at the notice. "Then the closure was warranted."

"The closure of that court was warranted," Rashid says. "Closing every practice session was not. The director has approved the north ground on Tuesdays and Thursdays, with a supervisor, until the inspection is finished."

Ashara reaches for the bread. "The north ground has no roof."

"Correct. You will not be asked to lend it."

"That is not why I object."

"It is not the only reason."

Rashid's hand rests on the petition tucked beneath the notice.

"The request had thirty-seven signatures by yesterday afternoon. It helped."

Aldric considers that, then nods once. "That is a better outcome than reopening a damaged court."

"Yes," Rashid says. "It is."
