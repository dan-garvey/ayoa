Seraphel takes the stairs at an unhurried pace, one hand skimming the polished banister. The dining hall opens below in candlelight and the warm, buttery smell of soup. The long table is too large for the eight places in use; the unused settings gleam down its far half.

Your greeting comes back in a scatter of voices and inclinations of heads. Seraphel guides you to an empty chair beside her. Across the table, Rashid vel Amara smooths a folded sheet with a furred hand. His coat is fitted closely enough that the movement looks deliberate. Ashara vel Kothren sits two places away, black-red horns throwing long shapes across the paneling. Aldric Verantus has not quite relaxed from whatever point he was making.

"It's a request for supervised access," Rashid says, returning to it. "Not a demand to throw the court open tonight."

"You don't know whether a supervisor would have prevented the injury," Aldric says.

"Neither do you," Ashara says. A faint shimmer rises from the soup near her plate. "You have a closure, and apparently that is sufficient evidence."

Aldric sets down his spoon. "It's sufficient precaution until someone responsible finds out what happened."

"I will ask the instructor in the morning," Rashid says. "The students who use the court can still tell the Academy what the closure costs them."

Ashara reaches for the bread basket. "Then ask for that. Do not ask for my allotted hour again."

Rashid's mouth shifts at one corner. "I asked whether you'd lend it. You declined with admirable precision."

"Good. You understood me."

At the other end of the table, Caelindra turns a page without looking up. Ysolde studies the steam lifting from her cup as if it has given her a problem worth considering.

Rashid takes up a pen, signs the bottom of the sheet, and slides it across to Ashara. She reads every line before adding her name in a hard, quick stroke. Rashid turns the paper toward your place setting.
