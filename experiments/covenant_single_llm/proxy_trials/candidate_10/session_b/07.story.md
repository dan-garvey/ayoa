Ysolde turns from the window. Her grey eyes rest on you without much change of expression.

"No," she says. "I don't take passengers I have just met. And I don't transform on the Nexus for a private outing. Too many people would notice."

She takes another sip of tea.

"Your promise not to wriggle helps a little."

The corner of her mouth shifts, then settles.

"After classes, I am going to the east terrace to watch the wind change. The view is better there before dusk. You may come to that, if you want."
