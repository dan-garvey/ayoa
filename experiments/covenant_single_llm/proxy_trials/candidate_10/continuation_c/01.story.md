Rashid occupies one end of the dining table, his lunch cooling beside an open notebook. Names run down one page in a small, precise hand, with tick marks crowded into the margin. He looks up as you approach.

"Sixth bell at the press," he says. "I've got the revised request."

He pulls a folded sheet from the notebook and smooths it against the table. The first paragraph asks for supervised access for students outside the Military track; below it, the request promises a roster, an instructor, and an equipment inspection before each session.

"I left out the injury," he says. "We don't have the report, and I won't make a case from rumors. If they want to defend the restriction, they can tell us what failed."

He refolds the page with the same care he used to open it. "Ashara can keep her booking. This isn't about taking it from her." A serving dish passes between two students farther down the table. Rashid closes the notebook and slides it beneath his tray.

"Sixth bell," he repeats.
