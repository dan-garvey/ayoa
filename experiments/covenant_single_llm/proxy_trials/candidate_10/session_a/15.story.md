Friday passes through lectures, library lamps, and the ordinary work of keeping up. By dinner, rain has cleared from the windows, leaving the garden paths dark and bright-edged beneath the lamps.

When you ask Rashid about the meeting, he has just unfolded his napkin.

“Better than a refusal,” he says. “Not a full reopening.”

He takes a folded notice from his coat and lays it beside his plate.

“The covered court stays closed while Facilities inspect the flooring and equipment. The instructor's report does not assign blame. Starting Monday, students outside the Military track get two supervised sessions a week on the public ground.”

Ashara looks over from her soup. “With whom supervising?”

“A senior Military student and a faculty rotation. The office will review it after two weeks.”

“That is a trial,” she says.

“Yes.” Rashid smooths the notice flat. “It gets people practice time while the actual report is sorted out.”

At the end of the table, Aldric nods once. “That seems measured.”

Rashid's glance flicks toward him. “It is more useful than a closed door.”

Ashara returns to her soup. “Do not mistake a useful beginning for a win.”

“I don't.”

The notice remains between them, its Academy seal catching the candlelight.
