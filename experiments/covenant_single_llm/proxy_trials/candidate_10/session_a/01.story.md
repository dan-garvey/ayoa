The dining hall makes seven students look temporarily lost. Seventeen places run down the varnished table beneath candles and working lamps, with bowls of buttered roots, black bread, and a rosemary-dark roast waiting in the middle.

Your greeting turns the argument into a pause.

Rashid vel Amara is standing beside a chair near the roast, one fine-furred hand on its carved back. He looks at the place setting, then at you. “A strategically sound criterion. This one is available.”

Across from it, Ashara vel Kothren leans back in her chair, black-red horns catching the candlelight. Her tail has hooked itself around one of the chair legs. “It is also nearest Rashid. Consider that before committing.”

“I can move,” Rashid says.

“You have spent five minutes refusing to.”

The corner of his mouth pulls despite himself. “I was establishing a principle.”

At the table's far side, a white-haired woman sits so still that the lamplight seems to settle around her. Beside her, an elf with metallic-silver hair has arranged her napkin with surgical care. The smaller fae woman watches the exchange over folded hands, while Aldric Verantus rises from his place in crisp white and gold.

“Rowan Garvey,” he says, with a small formal inclination of his head. “Welcome to Garvey House, and to the Conclave.”

Seraphel takes the chair beside the empty one, tucking her wings close as she sits. “You have been offered the roast,” she says. “This is how friendships begin here.”

Ashara reaches for the bread basket before Rashid can. “That is how arguments begin here.”

The vacant chair waits between Seraphel and Rashid, its plate already warm from the kitchen.
