Professor Stone blinks once. Then he takes his hands from the table.

“You are right. You did not.”

He looks briefly toward the library door.

“I came through the common room while dinner was still going. The dining-hall doors were open, and I heard Caelindra tell the story—enough of it, at least. The poem, the laugh, the second wager.”

His jaw tightens, not quite defensively.

“When I said I had not heard it before tonight, I meant exactly that. I should have said that I had heard it tonight.”

He gives the framed letter a little more space on the table, as though correcting its position might improve the explanation.

“I stopped because I heard Edda's name. That does not make the stopping less rude.”

The lamps hiss softly in their glass chimneys.

“I knew Edda. I did not know that particular story until Caelindra told it.”
