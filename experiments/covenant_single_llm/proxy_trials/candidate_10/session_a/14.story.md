The next days acquire a shape. Lessons divide the mornings and afternoons; your own pages gather ink in the spaces between them. Garvey House develops its habits around you: doors opening before dawn, someone practicing scales in an upper room, the late clink of cups carried back from the library.

On the fourth evening, dinner has lost its first-night stiffness. People choose seats without negotiating them. Rain taps lightly at the tall windows.

Rashid has a folded Academy notice beside his plate. Ashara notices it before he has finished his soup.

“They answered?”

“They offered a meeting on Friday.” He taps the notice once. “The athletics office wants the instructor's report and accounts from the students who were there.”

“You have the accounts?”

“Three students will give their own. The request stays about supervised access.”

Across the table, Aldric sets down his spoon. “That seems a reasonable response to an injury.”

Rashid's courtesy settles into place. “It is a response. I would rather it lead somewhere.”

Ashara tears a piece of bread in half. “Do not present it as a verdict before you have the report.”

“I won't.”

“Good.”

At the far end of the table, Seraphel and Caelindra are bent over an open book between their plates. Ysolde has claimed the chair nearest the rain-streaked window and watches the storm move over the gardens. The house carries several conversations at once, none of them waiting for an invitation to continue.
