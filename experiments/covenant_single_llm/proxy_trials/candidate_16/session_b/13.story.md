Kira pulls a green-bound catalogue from the shelf and opens it midway through. One entry is marked in red pencil: **Foundations of Living Grounds — soil, propagation, seasonal care, and managed enchantments; garden practicum twice weekly.** The course notes assign each student a small bed for the term and list no magical prerequisite.

“Foundations of Living Grounds fits. It is practical, taught in the gardens, and each student keeps a bed through the term.”

She enters Professor Stone's history course and the garden practicum on the timetable, then takes out a blue Arcana registry card. “For the assessment, bring this signed card to the south registry and choose an open slot; the screening is supervised, and it does not oblige you to take a magic course afterward.”

The card lists two openings: the second bell tomorrow afternoon or the sixth bell the following morning. Kira places it beside the completed course lines, along with a short reading list for Stone's course and a garden map marked with the practicum plots.
