The stew is thick with lentils, roasted peppers, and a heat that arrives slowly behind the first spoonful. Ashara tears her own piece of flatbread and uses it to lift a strip of softened onion from the bowl.

“Household fussing is useful; it tells you who someone expected to have supper with.” She takes a drink of water. “My bout went to three passes, and I won the last by two touches after wasting the first trying to impress the judge.”

The fire settles with a low shift of wood. Outside the garden doors, the paths have emptied into darkness, but the bridge still throws a pale reflection across the window glass. Ashara takes another spoonful of stew, leaving the pot between you within easy reach.
