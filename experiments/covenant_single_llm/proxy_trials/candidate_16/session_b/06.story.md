Ashara's gaze stays on the bridge for another moment. “Then you may have the chance. Meet me by the common-room hearth after the evening bell; if the bout runs late, eat without me.”

The rest of the circuit carries you past flowerbeds closed tight for the night and the old garden gate, its lower hinge furred with rust. At the doors, you thank her for the walk. Ashara gives a small inclination of her head. “Good night, Garvey.”

In your room, the primer's first-morning page is marked with an Academy seal and a neat block of instructions: breakfast begins at the seventh bell; new Conclave students report to the seminar room at the second bell after that, in Academy dress and carrying their identity seal. The morning holds an orientation with Chancellor Ashworth, a tour of the central halls, and appointments with the track advisers after midday.

The house has quieted by the time you set the primer aside. A last spill of lamp-glow reaches the framed letter, then the room goes dark around the made bed, the empty desk drawers, and the muted weather beyond the window.
