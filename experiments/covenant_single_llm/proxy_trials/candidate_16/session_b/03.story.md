The pastry gives way in crisp layers, the onions sweet beneath the salt of the cheese. Around it, the soup has begun to steam in everyone's bowls.

“Professor Gareth Stone is your best first call,” Rashid says. “He teaches human Covenant history, and he is usually in the library after noon; I came for treaty law and military policy, especially the things everyone calls neutral after a committee has made them expensive for the same people.”

Ashara takes a measured bite of tart before answering. “Strategy and Covenant law; the rankings tell you what you can do with a blade, and the law tells you who gets punished for having one.”

Rashid's mouth pulls briefly to one side at that, though he does not argue. The server passes the bread basket between them, and the white-haired woman by the window turns from the storm long enough to take a dark-crusted roll.
