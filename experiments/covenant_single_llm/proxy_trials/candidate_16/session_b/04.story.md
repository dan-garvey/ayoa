The meal takes its time. Between the soup and the roast, introductions make their way around the table without ceremony. Ysolde Thornmantle gives her name with one eye still on the weather beyond the glass. Caelindra Vaeyn offers a formal nod over her menu. Seraphel Dawnquill sets her name into four plain, unrhymed lines; Thessaly Morrow says she is hungry before she says anything else; Aldric Verantus waits until the bread has reached him, then introduces himself with careful courtesy.

The talk moves from instructors to the eccentricities of Academy meals, then to the dueling rankings posted outside the grounds. Rashid complains that the list is updated in a hand designed to be illegible at a distance. Ashara says this may be because he insists on reading it while walking. At the far end of the table, Ysolde becomes unexpectedly absorbed in a disagreement about whether the storm has shifted north since last week, and Seraphel's brief verses draw a laugh from Thessaly.

By the time the last plates are cleared, the lamps have taken the place of the fading light outside. Rashid folds the paper at his elbow into quarters. “I would, but I promised Dena Arl I would look over a request with her before I take it to faculty.”

Ashara looks toward the garden doors. “I can spare one circuit of the gardens. The bridge is less impressive from ground level, but not much.”

Rashid tucks the paper inside his cuff and stands. Beyond the open doors, pale garden paths curve between the flowerbeds toward the wall, where the hard-light bridge rises over the storm.
