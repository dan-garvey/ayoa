The practicum board and your timetable agree: **Foundations of Living Grounds** meets at the third bell after midday tomorrow. Until then, the terrace is quiet except for leaves moving against the stone edging.

You take the paths between the beds at an unhurried pace. Slate labels mark winter peas just pushing through the soil, lamp-mint with silver-furred leaves, bitterleaf kale, and a row of narrow blue-green seedlings labeled *river onions*. One bed has been cleared to bare dark earth; another is crossed with twine and a small note about testing its drainage after rain. At the far end of the terrace, a rain barrel feeds a set of clay channels running from plot to plot.

When you return to Garvey House, Rashid is in the common room with several sheets spread across a study table. He looks up from a page covered in crossed-out lines when you ask about Dena.

“She gave me useful corrections. We will make the request through attendance records and a safety plan, not one student's account; I am taking it to faculty review tomorrow.”

He taps the margin of the page with the end of his pen, then turns it over and reaches for a clean sheet.
