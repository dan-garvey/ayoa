Stone looks over the copied headings, then draws a slimmer index from the shelf behind his desk. His finger follows the Garvey entries down a page worn soft at the edges.

“Sel was Selene Garvey, Mara's younger sister; Aunt Bea was Beatrice Varo, their mother's sister.” He turns the index so the names and dates face you. “They both died long ago, and I do not find Pella or Fen in any current Academy registry.”

He marks the page with a strip of clean paper. “After your appointment, I can show you the reference if you would like to keep tracing the branch.”

You put the copied letters away with the primer. The north-gallery clock leaves enough of the third bell to cross the quadrangle, past the chapel steps and along the faculty offices without hurrying. Faculty Hall is quieter than the library; its thin doors bear small brass plates. At the end of the corridor, one reads **PROFESSOR KIRA VEL SHAAN — PRACTICAL RHETORIC**. The fourth bell has not yet sounded.
