The timetable gives the morning to the first Conclave Institutions seminar. Professor Windwhisper conducts it from the far side of the oval table, using a disputed bridge permit to trace the difference between a vote, a treaty obligation, and a courtesy. Her voice never rises, even when three students begin speaking at once. By midday, the case has been put aside without a winner and the next reading has been assigned.

After lunch, the south-registry clerk checks the stamp on the blue card and directs you to Assessment Room Three, along the lower colonnade beyond the Arcana lecture rooms. You reach its white-painted door with time to spare. The room beyond is spare and cool: a table set with a glass-cupped ember, a shallow dish of grey sand, a coil of copper thread, and a slate marked with a simple grid.

The examiner accepts the card and compares it with the Garvey signet. “Mr. Garvey, I am Examiner Sol. Sit wherever is comfortable; we begin with response to neutral foci.”

At Sol's direction, you place a hand in turn near the ember, the sand, the thread, and the slate. The ember burns steadily inside its glass. The sand remains level. The copper keeps its ordinary dull curve, and the grid stays blank while Sol notes each result.

When the second bell sounds beyond the colonnade, Sol closes the ledger. “You show no measurable resonance in the standard channels. I will record you as non-practical for now; you may study Arcana theory without restriction.”
