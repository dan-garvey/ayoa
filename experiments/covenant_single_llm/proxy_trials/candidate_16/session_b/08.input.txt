I go to Professor Stone's office at the start of his posted hours and knock. "Professor Stone? Rowan Garvey. Rashid suggested I speak to you. There is a letter from Mara Garvey in my room, addressed to her great-nephew. I lost my parents young and know very little about this side of my family. Do you know whether she left any personal letters or papers I could read? I would like to know what she was like, and how we are related."
