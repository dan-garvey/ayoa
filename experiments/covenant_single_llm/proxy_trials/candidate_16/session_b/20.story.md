Rashid squares the sheets beneath his hand. “Not an immediate reversal. I am asking for an inspection, the instructor's report, and two supervised evening blocks a week once the equipment is cleared; no student needs a private coach to keep training while the faculty decides what happened.”

He glances at the crossed-out draft. “If the injury came from negligence, the fix should answer that. If it did not, closing everyone out is still an answer of convenience.”

Upstairs, the Arcana report goes with the timetable and the copied Garvey letters. You return with Professor Windwhisper's seminar reading and take the nearest open study table in the common room. The assigned bridge-permit case unfolds through petitions, maintenance agreements, and a disagreement over whether a temporary restriction became a practical exclusion when it lasted through an entire season.

Rashid continues revising at the next table. The fire has burned low enough that the portraits' gilt frames catch more light than their faces, and the house holds the quiet sounds of pages turned, pen strokes, and someone moving upstairs.
