At the south registry, a clerk checks Kira's signature against the faculty seal and enters your name beneath the second-afternoon-bell assessment slot. The blue card comes back with a fresh stamp, the time written in a firm black hand.

Back at Garvey House, the copied letters lie beside the primer while Stone's reading list opens across the desk. The first selections move from a transcript of the Covenant's founding debates to later commentaries on bridge permissions and Council voting procedure. Their margins disagree sharply about what a phrase like *shared authority* was meant to demand, while the original speakers have left only their names, their clauses, and the objections entered into record.

By the evening bell, the windows have gone blue-grey over the gardens. The common-room hearth has been built up again, its fire throwing light across the old portraits and the empty study tables. Ashara is not there when you arrive.

A few minutes later, the garden door opens. She comes in with her hair still tied back from the grounds and a covered pot balanced on one hand, heat rising from it into the cooler room. “The bout finished on time. I secured stew before the Military cohort could discover it.”

She sets the pot on the hearthstone beside two bowls and a stack of flatbread.
