Ashara considers that over the rim of her bowl. “Mine was one proctor, a bowl of sand, and a list of responses. If yours has a ranking board, complain to me afterward; I will agree it was unnecessary.”

The meal winds down without haste. When the pot is empty, you help stack the bowls and carry it to the covered return tray at the dining-hall door. Ashara takes the flatbread basket from the hearthstone and puts its cloth back in place.

At the stairs, you thank her for the evening. “Sleep well, Garvey,” she says.

Upstairs, the stamped Arcana card goes with the timetable where the morning light will find both. The house has settled again by the time you put out the lamp. Beyond the window, the bridge's pale line remains above the storm until sleep takes the room.
