Caelindra lets the apology settle between the candle flames. One pointed ear turns slightly toward the door as a pair of servers pass behind the chairs, their covered dishes sending up a thread of rosemary and butter.

“Sunday at noon works for me,” she says. “You should have told us sooner, but floor repairs are not a personal affront. I will come to the west terrace.”

Seraphel folds her hands beside her plate. “Sunday at noon
will still receive me.
Thank you for telling us.”
