I finish dinner, fetch the two pages from my room, and bring them to Caelindra in the library. The pages tell of a village cooper who accepts an order for six barrels, with half the price paid in advance. The delivery address turns out to belong to a man who has been dead for ten years. When she returns to her shop, three of the barrels are missing. That is where the pages end; I have not written who the customer is or how the barrels disappeared. I give her time to read, then ask, "Is there anything here you would want to read more of?"
