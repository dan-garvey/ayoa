Caelindra acknowledges your good night with a nod and turns back to the lamp-lit page before her.

In your room, the primer takes up the next hour with its clean, official account of the Covenant: Council seats, bridge permissions, Academy tracks, and the forty years of war that made the treaties necessary. The human collapse occupies two restrained paragraphs under a heading about the plague. Article Nineteen receives more space, its penalties set out in careful legal language. By the time you put the book aside, the lamp has burned low.

Morning brings grey stormlight through the windows and the smell of coffee from below. When you come down dressed for the day, only a few places at the breakfast table are occupied. Caelindra sits near the gardens with a slim, blue-bound book beside the empty place across from her.

She moves it onto the table between you. "*The Minor Catastrophes.* The parrot arrives on page twelve. Do not begin there."
