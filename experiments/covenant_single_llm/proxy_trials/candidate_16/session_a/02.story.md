The roast is tender under its dark glaze, smoky at the edges and brightened by the rosemary. Ashara looks at the orderly row of silver beside your plate.

"The wrong spoon is an Academy offense," she says. "They ring a bell and hang it over the gate."

"We do not," Aldric says.

"Not yet," Rashid adds, and takes a drink to hide the beginning of a smile.

Caelindra's knife stills against her plate at your question. For a moment she studies the candlewick between you rather than anyone at the table. "A collection called *The Minor Catastrophes*," she says at last. "Human verse. There is a magistrate, a missing parrot, and three invitations sent to the wrong people. It is very exact about embarrassment."

Thessaly turns her black gaze from the citrus beans to Caelindra. "That sounds useful."

"It is not useful," Caelindra says. The answer comes too quickly; she takes another bite, then looks back at you. "Do you read poetry, Rowan Garvey?"
