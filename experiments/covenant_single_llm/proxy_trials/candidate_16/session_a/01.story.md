By the time you come down, the dining hall has gone bright with lamps. Candlelight runs along the varnished table and catches in seventeen waiting places, though only seven are occupied. A server draws back an empty chair on the garden side as you enter.

Your greeting reaches the table over the soft scrape of silverware. The wolf-blooded man nearest the bread rises first; his cuff is immaculate, his hands dark with fine fur. "Rashid vel Amara. Welcome to Garvey House."

The woman beside him has black-red horns swept back from a severe knot of hair. "Ashara vel Kothren," she says, and sits a little straighter as if the name is an introduction enough.

The rest follow without ceremony. White-haired Ysolde Thornmantle gives a small nod from across the table. Caelindra Vaeyn names herself while adjusting the fall of her silver hair. Thessaly Morrow offers hers with a brief, curious smile. Aldric Verantus greets you with grave warmth. The angel beside him, whose small white wings show between the folds of her gold-trimmed clothes, says,

"Seraphel Dawnquill.
May this house give you rest tonight."

Platters arrive: glazed root vegetables, saffron rice, a dark roast fragrant with rosemary, and a dish of green beans dressed in sharp citrus. You help yourself as the servers pass wine and chilled water along the table. Near Rashid's plate, a sheaf of papers has slipped loose from beneath his napkin; the top page bears the heading *Request for Supervised Court Access* before he tucks it back into place.

"They sent you a primer, I expect," Caelindra says, breaking a piece from the bread loaf. Her violet eyes settle on the Garvey signet. "Did the lawyers explain the Conclave, or merely teach you which hand to use for inheriting it?"

Ashara reaches for the salt. "That is unfair. Lawyers seldom explain anything that might prove useful."
