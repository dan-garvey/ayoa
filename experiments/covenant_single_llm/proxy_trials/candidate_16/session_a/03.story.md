Caelindra sets down her glass with care. The candlelight catches along the edge of her silver hair as she considers you across the table.

"Two pages is a tolerable amount of risk," she says. "Bring them to the library after dinner. Leave me long enough to read them before you tell me what they mean."

Thessaly folds her hands beneath the tablecloth. "That has all the components of a bargain and none of the useful language."

"It is an arrangement," Caelindra says.

"Those are usually worse."

For the first time since you sat down, Caelindra's mouth threatens a smile before she takes another measured bite of dinner.
