Stone's eyebrows rise, then he gives a low, unwilling laugh. He reaches into his inner pocket and sets six dull silver crescents in a neat stack beside the cold cup. The coins click sharply in the quiet library.

“Did she add that I paid her already?” He studies the stack as though it has disappointed him. “No—of course not. She would have wanted the pleasure of making a debt survive us both. Keep it, Mr. Garvey. Consider it interest on a very old lesson.”
