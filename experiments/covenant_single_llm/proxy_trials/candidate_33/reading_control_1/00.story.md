The last line leaves the common room quiet enough for the fire's small clicks to carry. Seraphel holds the harp across her knees without touching the strings. A pale flicker from the storm moves over the high window and is gone.

Then she looks at the page in your lowered hand.

“Your Ada has counted

the Fridays.

Nell has only ten o'clock.

That is why the coat

feels heavier than cloth.

‘In what?’ is cruel.

I mean that kindly.

Could we hear it once more?

I would put a small tune

under their argument—

one note for Nell,

another for Ada.”
