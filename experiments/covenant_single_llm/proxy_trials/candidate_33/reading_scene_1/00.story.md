The page makes a dry whisper as you smooth it across your knee. Seraphel has laid the harp beside the hearth, blue cloth folded over its dark frame. The low fire throws a wavering line across the paper.

You give Nell the quicker voice and Ada the lower one. Seraphel does not look away while you read. At the final question, one of the logs settles behind the iron screen. She leaves the silence where it is, then shifts the blue cloth farther from the harp's strings.

“Your last line
makes the coat vanish.

For a moment,
they have no thing
to quarrel through.

Nell can leave.
Ada has to wait
for Voss.”

Her gaze returns to the page.

“I thought Ada was cruel,
then I thought
she was afraid
to meet him alone.

Perhaps I am wrong.

I like that I cannot tell.”

Across the common room, a clock ticks on the mantel beneath a Garvey portrait. Seraphel reaches for the harp, but only rests her fingers over its cloth.

“May I hear it again,
with Ada first?”
