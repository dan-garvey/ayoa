By the time you return, Seraphel has laid the harp across the hearthrug beside her. Firelight runs along the dark wood and the edge of the blue cloth. She listens without interrupting while Nell and Ada argue over the coat, your two voices carrying into the quiet room.

When you lower the page, Seraphel keeps her gaze on it for a moment.

“Nell has the morning.

Ada has the noon.

I heard them both.

‘In what?’

is where I would wait

for your next line.”
