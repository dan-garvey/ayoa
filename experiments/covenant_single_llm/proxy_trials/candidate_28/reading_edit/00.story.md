By the time you return, Seraphel has laid the harp on its blue cloth beside the hearthrug. She listens without interruption as Nell and Ada contend for the coat, your two voices moving through the firelit room.

When you lower the page, Seraphel watches the paper for a moment.

“‘I was the one who answered.’

I felt the door close there.

Then Ada says,

‘In what?’

I like her.”

Her fingertips rest on the silent strings.

“I would wait

for the next line.”
