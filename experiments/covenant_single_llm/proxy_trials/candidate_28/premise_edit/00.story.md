The bread breaks with a thin crack under your fingers. Caelindra has set her spoon across the edge of her bowl, apparently more interested in the sisters than the soup.

“The one with the rent collector has a deadline,” she says. “Give the other one one as well.”

“An interview is a deadline,” Rashid says.

“No. It is an appointment. It could be a bad one.”

Ashara considers the torn bread in your hand. “Then make the coat wrong for it. Too fine, too old, too conspicuous.”

Thessaly looks between them. “They could sell it after the interview.”

“That is not a solution,” Caelindra says.

“It pays the rent,” Thessaly replies.

When you turn toward Seraphel, her fingers settle on the spoon's handle. The small feathers at the edge of her folded wings catch the lamplight.

“I sing.

I play a small harp.

It is upstairs in its case.

After dinner,
I can bring it down
and play something short,
if you would like.”
