The bread breaks with a thin crack under your fingers. Caelindra listens without touching her soup again.

“The pawn would end the matter,” she says. “The interview might not.”

Rashid glances at the folded request beneath his glass, then back across the table. “They could trade the coat at the door.”

“Then neither of them has it when it matters,” Ashara says.

Thessaly's spoon pauses over her bowl. “That sounds like a bargain designed to leave both parties dissatisfied.”

When you turn toward Seraphel, she sets her own spoon down beside the bowl. Her small white wings make a soft brush against the back of her chair.

“I sing when I can.

A small harp travels with me.

After dinner, I could bring it down.

Would you like to hear it?”
