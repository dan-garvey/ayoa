Your greeting turns the whole table toward you.

The dining hall is warmer than the stairs, firelight and working lamps laying soft bands across the varnished table. A place card marked **GARVEY** waits halfway down, beside a covered bowl and a folded napkin. Ashara's horns make a dark sweep above the candlelight; Rashid sits at her right, one furred hand resting near his glass. Across from them, Ysolde's white hair is almost the same pale color as the soup steam.

Rashid's smile is quick and polished. "Still food. The danger was a severe disagreement about the number of chairs."

"He counted them twice," Ashara says.

"I made a tally. Different discipline."

Caelindra looks up from adjusting the cuff at her wrist. "That distinction is useful only to you."

"The chairs have not changed since yesterday," Ysolde says.

Thessaly's black eyes settle on Rashid. "That does make the tally safer."

Seraphel folds one small white wing closer against her chair.
