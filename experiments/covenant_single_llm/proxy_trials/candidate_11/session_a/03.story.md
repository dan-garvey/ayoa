"Tomorrow, at ten. The covered court." She nods once toward the table's far end, as if marking the place on a map. "Stay outside the white line. Watch the feet before you watch the blades."

Rashid leans back in his chair. "You're letting him watch?"

"He asked to watch. I am not granting a treaty."

"That is unusually civil."

"Eat your bread."

Seraphel's blue eyes brighten.

"Bring no wagers, please.

"I was not planning to wager," Rashid says.

Thessaly takes another spoonful of stew. "That is true at this moment."

Rashid gives her a narrow look that fails to trouble her.
