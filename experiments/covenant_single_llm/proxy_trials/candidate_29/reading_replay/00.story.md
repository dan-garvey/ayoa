The edge of the paper catches the firelight as you lower it. Seraphel does not speak at once. Her thumb rests across the lowest harp string until even its faint vibration is gone.

Then a quiet laugh slips out of her.

“‘In what?’

I was with Nell

until that line.

She says interview,

and I want her to have it.

Then Ada asks that,

and suddenly I can hear Voss

on the stairs.

I am sorry for them both.

I like Ada more.”

She draws the blue cloth over the harp's polished curve, carefully enough that the strings do not answer. She stays where she is on the hearthrug, looking at the low fire while the unfinished argument keeps its place between the two chairs.
