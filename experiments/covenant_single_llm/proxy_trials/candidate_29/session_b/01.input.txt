“Thank you,” I tell the server, and follow her downstairs.

At the dining-room doorway I give the table a slightly awkward nod. “I'm Rowan. Thank you for having me—and I'm sorry to be late.”

I look for an unoccupied place with a setting. The smell of dinner has made me realize how hungry I am; once I'm seated, I'm quite happy to eat and listen while I learn which names belong to which faces.
