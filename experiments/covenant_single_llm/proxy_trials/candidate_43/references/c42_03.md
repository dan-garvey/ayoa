The paper gives a soft crackle as you smooth it across your knee. Seraphel sets her harp beside the hearth and listens without looking away while Nell and Ada take shape in your two voices.

When you lower the page, the fire shifts behind its screen.

Seraphel's thumb rests against a string.

“‘In what?’

That is where I laughed.

Not because Ada is cruel.

Because Nell asks her to come

as though there is no cost

to being asked.”

Her gaze drops to the page.

“I want to know whether Nell hears it.”
