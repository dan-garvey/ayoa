The last line leaves a brief silence by the fire. Seraphel’s mouth curves before she looks down at the harp across her knees.

“‘In what?’ is good.

Nell has offered a plan  
that requires a second coat,  
and Ada notices at once.

I laughed.”

Her thumb brushes a string without sounding it.

“Ada also says  
she answered the door last Friday.

She knows where Nell was.  
She knows why Nell was out.  
Then she keeps arguing about the coat.

Could you read from there again?  
Start with ‘He came last Friday.’  
I want to hear the pause  
before Ada answers the invitation.”
