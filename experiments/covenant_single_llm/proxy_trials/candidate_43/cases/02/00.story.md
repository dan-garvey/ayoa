Seraphel’s mouth tips into a smile at Ada’s last line. She sets the harp carefully on its blue cloth beside the hearth.

“‘In what?’ was good.

I laughed there.

Nell speaks as if the coat
will carry her through the door.

Ada has heard the Friday promise
before, I think.

I kept looking at the coat.

Would you read the last four lines again?
Leave a little space
before Ada answers.

I want to hear
whether she is angry
or only tired.”
