Seraphel looks at the page rather than at you. One hand rests on the dark wood of her harp.

“I like the way Nell
keeps trying to make a plan
while Ada is worried
about Voss coming back.

And the coat gives them
something to fuss with
while they argue.

Ada's last line made me laugh.
She has nowhere to go
in the coat.

Would you read the last four lines
once more?

Leave a little pause
before Ada answers.”
