The page gives a soft crackle as it settles against your knee. Seraphel’s thumb rests on the harp’s frame; she does not reach for a string.

“Voss is where it changes.

‘I was the one who answered.’

Before that, I thought Ada
was only trying to win the coat.
After that, I thought Nell
had asked her to carry too much.”

A brief smile finds her mouth.

“Then: ‘In what?’

Your Ada is funnier
than my goat.

That is praise.

I would leave her there.”
