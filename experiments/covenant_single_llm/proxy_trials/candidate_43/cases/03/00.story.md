The page settles against your knee with a dry little sound. Seraphel keeps the harp across her lap, looking at the lines while the fire shifts behind its screen.

“‘In what?’

That is a cruel line.

I was glad Ada had it.”

Her thumb rests on a string without sounding it.

“At first, I wanted Nell

to take the coat and run.

Then Ada says she answered the door.

She has been waiting

with Voss in the room

all week.”

Seraphel glances up.

“I do not know whose side I am on.

That makes me want

the next page.”

The common room grows quiet again around the unfinished argument, the harp, and the low fire.
