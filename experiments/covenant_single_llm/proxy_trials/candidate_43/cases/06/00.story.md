The last line draws a quick laugh from Seraphel. She shifts the harp carefully against the side of her chair.

“The coat works.

Nell needs it for the morning.

Ada needs the money from it.

I thought Ada would agree
to go with her.

Then she says, ‘In what?’”

Seraphel looks down at the page, then back at you.

“I liked that.

Your lower voice suited Ada.

Would you read her last two lines
again?”
