“Thank you.” You take the marked chair and draw a piece of bread from the basket between the plates. Its crust gives beneath your fingers; steam still clings to the torn edge.

The conversation resumes in fragments around you. Rashid waits until a server has set down the first dishes, then turns to Ashara.

“The dueling court posted the morning slots. I took sixth bell. Do you still intend to use it?”

Ashara cuts a strip of roasted root with deliberate care. “I intended to see whether you had learned anything since yesterday.”

“That is not an answer.”

“It is the answer you earned.”

The silver-haired woman gives a small exhale through her nose and reaches for the salt. Across from her, the pale woman with black eyes studies the row of serving spoons as if they have made a difficult proposal.

“Thessaly Morrow,” she says at last, looking toward you. “I believe that was an introduction. I have not heard one from the others.”

“Ysolde Thornmantle,” says the white-haired woman beside her, without lifting her gaze from her plate.

At the far end, the golden-haired man inclines his head. “Aldric Verantus.”

Seraphel folds her hands near her glass. “The names are placed upon the board; / Let supper make their purpose more.”

A server begins portioning the roast from the sideboard while Rashid and Ashara return to the question of sixth bell.
