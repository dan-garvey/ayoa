You come down into a dining hall prepared for seventeen people and occupied by eight. Candlelight lies along the varnished table. At the setting you take, steam rises from a silver tureen; you fill your bowl with clear broth and small, peppery dumplings.

"Good evening. I'm Rowan."

The woman from the hall rests a hand on the back of her chair. Warm gold light gathers at the edges of her pale hair.

"Welcome, Rowan Garvey.
Please save the soup
from a second introduction."

The tall woman with swept black-red horns gives a short nod. "Ashara vel Kothren."

"Rashid vel Amara," says the man beside her, immaculate even at dinner. "Ignore her. The soup has a long career ahead."

"Not under your supervision," Ashara says.

Across the table, the silver-haired woman sits precisely where she had been objecting to the portrait. "Caelindra Vaeyn. I have already lodged my complaint about the room."

"Ysolde Thornmantle," says the white-haired woman beside her. After a glance toward the paintings, she adds, "The portrait is not facing you."

"That makes it worse."

The small woman with black eyes leans toward her spoon. "Thessaly Morrow. It did make you change seats."

At the nearer end of the table, a golden-haired man raises his cup. "Aldric Verantus. Welcome to Garvey House."

Seraphel settles into her chair at last.

"Did the lawyers give you a schedule,
or merely a house
and a law book?"
