Seraphel glances toward the empty places at the far end of the table.

“No teachers tonight.
But Professor Gareth Stone may.
He teaches human Covenant history,
and he is often in the library
after the evening meal.”

“Stone has been here forever,” Rashid says, tearing a piece of bread in half. “Long enough that every new student assumes he knows where the building keeps its secrets.”

“The building does not keep secrets,” Aldric says. “People do.”

Thessaly tips her head toward him. “Those are the same thing when a building has locks.”

For a moment, Aldric appears to consider objecting. Then he reaches for the soup instead.

Caelindra folds her napkin across her lap. “Professor Stone will either tell you precisely what he knows or decide that your question is tiresome. He is very consistent.”

“That sounds almost reassuring,” Ashara says.

Rashid's folio remains closed by his elbow. “The library stays open after dinner. If he's there, you can find out whether the Garveys were more interesting than their portrait gallery suggests.”

From the hallway beyond the dining room, the house's old clock begins to strike the hour.
