The formal dining hall is halfway through its second course, and the warmth of the lamps has driven a pleasant haze over the table. Rashid has a narrow sheet of paper tucked beside his plate, its margin crowded with small, neat names. Ashara is cutting through a roasted pepper with more concentration than the pepper deserves.

At your question, Rashid glances up. "Still plan to? I did not think I'd made it sound conditional." He taps the paper with a knuckle. "But yes. West terrace, tomorrow at sunset."

"Provided you finish trying to reorganize the Academy before dessert," Ashara says.

"I am gathering signatures for a request."

"And that is plainly different from reorganizing the Academy."

"It is considerably more legible."

Ashara slides the pepper's blackened skin to the rim of her plate. "Your answer is still yes?"

"My answer is yes." Rashid folds the sheet once. "I would like to hear the story. I also want to see whether you have written yourself into a corner in public."

"He might simply be reading something good," Ashara says.

"Then I will be pleasantly disappointed."

Ashara turns to you. "I have training tomorrow. Do not count on me, but I may come afterward."

Rashid rests his forearm on the table. "West terrace at sunset, then. Has anything changed?"
