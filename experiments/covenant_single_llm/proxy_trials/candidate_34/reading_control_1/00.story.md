The last line leaves the room quieter than the song did. Firelight moves across the fold in your page. Seraphel's thumb rests on the lowest string of her harp; from the dining hall comes the small chime of a spoon against china, then a voice too low to distinguish.

“‘In what?’

is the right place

to stop.

Nell hears ten o'clock.

Ada hears noon.

But Ada also hears

last Friday.”

She draws one fingertip across the string. It gives a muted note rather than a tune.

“‘I was the one

who answered.’

That is the line

that turns the room.

Their voices separate

before their names appear.

I like that.

You have enough

to make me wonder

what they will do

with the coat.”

For a moment she studies the page in your hands, then looks up.

“Does Ada want Nell

to get the job?”
