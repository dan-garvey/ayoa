Rashid inclines his head. “Fair enough. I’ll have something worth reading tomorrow.”

The disagreement survives the rest of dinner without becoming louder. Aldric calls caution a duty; Ashara calls it an excuse only when no one has bothered to investigate. By the time the plates are cleared, neither has yielded an inch.

Footsteps scatter through Garvey House afterward. Rashid takes the faculty notice and a sheaf of paper to a study table. Ashara carries a narrow practice case upstairs. Aldric leaves by the garden door, white-and-gold sleeves bright in the dark beyond it.

The fire in the common room gives the portraits above it a shifting, disapproving light. Thessaly arrives with a black-backed deck of cards and settles at the low table opposite your chair. She deals two neat hands, then looks up.

“Seven Stones,” she says. “No stakes. Do you know it?”
