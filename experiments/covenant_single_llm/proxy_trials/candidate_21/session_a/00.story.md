The green-shaded lamp makes a hard circle on the desk, catching the edges of the Academy primer: welcome dinner at dusk, seven signatories, Article Nineteen set in heavier type than the rest. Below, Garvey House is discovering its evening voice—china touches china, a chair protests across the floorboards, someone laughs without restraint.

A single knock sounds at your door.

“Rashid vel Amara,” says the voice beyond it. Warm, controlled, and near enough to the wood that it carries clearly. “I thought I would spare you an entrance into a room that has already decided seating is a constitutional matter. We are about to sit. Would you like company going down?”

Candlelight reaches under the door from the landing.
