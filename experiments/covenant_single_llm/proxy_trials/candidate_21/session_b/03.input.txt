"Not yet," I tell Caelindra. "I only learned I had a place here recently. I was distracted by the letter in my room, and then by the prospect of dinner. Is there something in the primer I ought to know before I accidentally offend the whole table?"

I begin eating while I listen. When there is a natural opening, I add to Ysolde, "I would like to see the herb plots tomorrow. If you were going anyway, may I come along? I can at least carry a watering can without a primer."
