"Garden, please. I like having something to look at when I have nothing clever to say."

I take the garden-side place if it is free, leaving room for Ysolde to choose her own seat, and make myself useful passing whatever needs passing as dinner begins. "Is the garden just for looking at, or are we allowed to meddle with it?"
