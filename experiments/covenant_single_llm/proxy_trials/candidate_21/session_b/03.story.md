The first course is a peppered lentil stew, steam fogging the candlelight above the long table.

Caelindra lifts the lid from a dish of roasted pears and studies the steam rather than you. “You cannot offend the whole table accidentally. That requires an audience or effort. You have managed neither.”

“Read the forms of address before tomorrow. Tedious, occasionally wrong, but better than three separate explanations of why a title is not an introduction.”

Seraphel Dawnquill, warm-gold beside Aldric, smiles.

“Names are a good beginning.
Dinner is a better one.
You can be ignorant
and still pass the bread.”

Ysolde folds a corner of bread between cool fingers. “I am going to the herb plots after morning seminar. I want to see what grows in weather without winter. You may come, if you do not mind a detour through the garden.”
