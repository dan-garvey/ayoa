"A detour sounds good. I will look for you after the seminar," I tell Ysolde. To Caelindra I add, "Forms of address. I will read them tonight. Thank you."

I relax into the meal and listen to what the others have to say, without trying to turn every pause into a question about myself.

When dinner is winding down, I wish them a good evening and return to my room. I sit beside the lamp and read the framed letter slowly, paying particular attention to the person it was written to, the signature, and any date. Then I open the primer to the forms of address, and look for whatever tells me where and when tomorrow's seminar takes place.
