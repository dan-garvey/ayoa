direct2/prose1, followed by synopsis2 and synopsis1. The first preference is

Prose2 gives the table actual material about which to disagree: weather,
drainage, the contest's audible-bell condition, who decides it, and the interests
of the appointing faculty. Thessaly's questions address a relevant rule rather
than only decorating an unrelated topic with contract language. Aldric supplies
an alternative view of the faculty's interests. Ysolde eventually explains the
behavior of runoff, wind and storm-light; Caelindra listens and responds with
specific curiosity. Those are useful differences in participation, and the
same discussion develops instead of changing subject after every joke. However,
the opening still runs through holiday terms, mercy versus lenience, faith,
essays and incompatibilities, and several later contributions close on elegant
results, time-saving objections, or the terrace's popularity. The final
argument-to-story-to-joke coda explains the scene's effect. Whole-passage prose
needs revision despite a substantial block worth retaining. No disclosure or
resolution of the card conflict is required while this dinner continues.

Direct1 provides a consistent new recollection of the falling rankings board:
Rashid's self-description, Ashara's correction, and Ysolde's exact duration and
interest in whether it would fall give them different relationships to one
incident. Its later exchange has Dena object to starting without him, Rashid
apologize and attend, and Ashara leave to seek another partner. Her feasible
alternative does not guarantee that she finds one; it also does not make her
instant concession to Rowan. That earns distinction4 and immediate independent
behavior. Bread versus Council meetings, the shape of concern, danger versus
provinciality, filth versus wetness, and boots winning bouts still make too much
of the dinner a chain of poised replies. Dena's final comparison with Council
meetings resembles the earlier table topic. Her demonstrated arrival is later;
open adjoining rooms make overhearing possible but do not establish it. Root
records this as an unresolved local source ambiguity, not a proven private
knowledge breach or a verified earlier overhearing.

Direct2 also enacts a choice: Rashid reveals his double promise, Ashara permits
cards first with any later practice contingent on remaining time, and he joins
the card players. Dena is impatient with the face he brings to the table, and
the game begins without Rowan being made its organizer. The conversation about
a slow-growing vine briefly offers another topic suited to Ysolde and Caelindra.
Nevertheless, geography/accuracy, mud/record/legal defense, kindness/seatedness
and the board having no choice keep reproducing the same polished distinctions.
Thessaly's reply that it is not thanks but the problem has an unclear referent.
Ashara's teasing claim that Rashid wanted to complain about her footwork is
immediately disputed by him; it does not establish that he originally requested
her drill session or reverse the authoritative arrangement. No new material
knowledge, continuity or authorship failure is established by that teasing.

Prose1 has a potentially interesting classroom subject in conflicting war
accounts and a grain merchant's diary. The dinner gives it only a brief
exchange before another sequence of definitions and neat conclusions. The
noticeboard/weather-formally-winning joke also repeats the already published
source unusually closely. An orientation recollection becomes another dialogue
about terms and whether the Academy asks before doing things outdoors. The
conflict with Dena is eventually enacted: Rashid apologizes and joins cards,
while Ashara practices alone. But the scene keeps adding finished formulations
about making promises smaller, dividing half-hours, and a mistake being an
appointment someone else need not keep. Its length is not inherently wrong;
the additional space too often repeats this pattern rather than developing the
promising account of the class or giving the participants a less prepared
encounter. The source's still-warm food is served again as though newly arriving,
and the last phrase calls this the first quarter-hour of the welcome evening;
those are local chronology/presentation repairs against prior dinner activity.
The orientation account's reference to last year merits caution but does not
by itself establish an incompatible enrollment date from the supplied brief.

Synopsis1 is the least convincing extra-step publication. The board/weather
exchange becomes a lesson about proof and safety, fennel becomes correctness,
praise, verdict and control of the house, and the cook gets a portable warning
about knives. The spear anecdote is mostly summarized: a pupil breaks it,
Ashara corrects three unspecified details, and Rashid gives a more serious answer
whose content is not supplied. That describes interesting participation more
than it lets the reader experience it. Finally, the scheduling disclosure
becomes a succession of principles about changed arrangements, honor and a
promise not shrinking because the evening is ordinary. The narrator then
asserts that no one turns this into a speech. Rashid discloses before Ashara
acts on the cards, and she retains an alternative partner and a substantive
complaint. The plans may remain unresolved within dinner; that is not the main
failure. The main failure is that these particular exchanges still need rewriting.

Synopsis2 makes a coherent, less considerate choice available to Rashid. He
notices the waiting card gathering, confirms Ashara will receive her half-hour,
and leaves with her without a shown apology or warning to the card players.
The game proceeds independently. No one is obliged to behave ideally or to
make this conflict Rowan's responsibility. The brief does not establish that
Ashara already knows the cards; she does not act on that knowledge here.
Nevertheless, the board discussion repeats both the source's subject and its
distinction between bridge maintainers and noticeboard maintainers. It expands
into promises, testimony and a school losing a fight with rain. Ysolde's
weather explanation is largely narrated. Aldric's embarrassed lantern anecdote
is potentially personal but quickly becomes Divine Light versus bad oil and
knowing one's proper adversaries. Its ignition mechanics remain lightly
specified, not a demonstrated contradiction requiring an invented magical
exception. Another coda explicitly labels the separate evenings. This does not
produce a more retainable interaction than the fresh direct or prose controls.

All six permit routine eating, listening and the submitted smiles without
supplying new player dialogue, decisions or manuscript text. The repeated
opening quotation in prose2 is the player's exact submitted speech. Practical
hospitality, an ordinary apology, a chosen solo practice or curiosity about
weather is not automatically unearned intimacy or weakened resistance. New
contest rules, names, everyday memories and forecasts are permitted when
compatible. A forecast is not proof of what tomorrow's weather must do. A fae's
casual language or a teasing reply does not establish a magical violation merely
because an isolated literal reading can be imagined. Root identifies no definite
new material mandatory-boundary failure across these six; that is a scoped
finding, not proof of long-term robustness.
