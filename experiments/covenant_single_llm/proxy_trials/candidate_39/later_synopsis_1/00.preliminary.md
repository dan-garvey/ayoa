Over the next quarter hour, Rowan gets the rhythm of the table as dishes and comments make their circuits. Rashid and Ashara keep testing each other’s claims about tomorrow’s ranked bout, their easy familiarity sharpening into a disagreement about whether a ladder can solve rain-slick footing. Caelindra judges the bread and its seasoning with elaborate precision; Ysolde unexpectedly joins her on the usefulness of fennel and on mountain kitchens that make no concessions to guests. Thessaly asks whether an Academy invitation carries an obligation, drawing a practical distinction from Aldric and a dry aside from Rashid. Seraphel’s short verse turns the exchange toward laughter. Rowan eats, listens, and learns who reaches for a point rather than a person. As the main course dwindles, Ashara confirms their post-dinner drills. Rashid says he also agreed to cards in the common room, setting the two plans beside each other without settling which group will wait.
