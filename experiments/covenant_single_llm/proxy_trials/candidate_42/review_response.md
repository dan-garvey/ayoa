reduced aphorism/ready-verdict burden in all four short edits, and both short
dinner edits retain and develop the treaty disagreement. The independent
reviewer judges both dinners retainable; root prefers them to the long controls
but remains dissatisfied with the surrounding conversation. The reading edits
show the tradeoff: plainer language can leave a more generic reaction. The
predeclared advancement gate fails, without erasing the positive dinner result.

assessments remain unchanged. Root read the entire independent report before
writing this comparison; neither reviewer nor any author received corrective
feedback or a second literary attempt.
