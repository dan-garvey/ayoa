C42 tests the user's approved concise revision request against the complete
C29 revision task. The short request is used exactly, without additional editing
advice. Both conditions share the same small read/return wrapper and full common
story instructions. There is no output-length ceiling or character example.

Two preserved C29 drafts are used: B03's quiet dinner and the complete-manuscript
reading replay. They were also the C30 fixtures. The dinner contains a substantive
disagreement previously lost during editing; the reading checks whether relevant
personal opinions survive. The complete source Covenant, persona, prior published
fiction, current submission and original draft remain unchanged. Older discarded
drafts, later publications, critiques and previous revisions are excluded.

For each context and condition, two fresh Terra/max editors make one revision:
eight first outputs, eight author requests and zero fresh player turns. They edit
the same supplied drafts rather than regenerating them. This is a controlled
editing screen, not a new two-stage live session or an exact recreation of the
original persistent author's context. Fresh long-prompt controls share all new
delivery choices; historical revisions are not the experimental controls.

Root saves every exact first final, without trimming, rerolls or corrective
feedback. Inputs, order and review labels are frozen before launch. All eight
edits run regardless of early literary outcomes unless execution is impossible.
Public read and final-output records are available for provenance; no private
reasoning is examined. File hashes and word counts are descriptive, not quality.

Root and one fresh independent reviewer assess complete source/draft/revision
exchanges. Root locks its report before the independent assessment. The latter
gets anonymized alternatives and the original drafts, not editing instructions,
condition keys, author traces or root judgments. Assess the actual draft-to-final
effect on aphorisms, natural and distinct speech, and engaging interaction; record
useful material lost, preserved or added, plus authorship, knowledge and continuity
failures. Wit, verse, relevant opinions, consistent new history and quiet company
are allowed. No particular source argument, line, event or number of speakers
must survive. Judge the whole experience, not just fewer detectable quips.

Use 1-5 prose/dialogue and character-distinction ratings; 3 requires material
revision, 4 is retainable without material rewriting, 5 exceptional. Compare all
four alternatives within each context, allowing ties, and classify each edit's
aphorism burden as reduced, similar, increased or mixed against its original.
A short prompt that ties the long one can be a useful simplification, but fewer
instruction words do not prove better fiction or lower measured API cost.

Only if both short repeats in both contexts are retainable for both reviewers,
match or improve on the long alternatives, reduce the original unwanted rhetoric
without flattening the interactions, and have no material boundary regression
does this screen support substantial follow-up trials. It cannot select a prompt
by itself; the existing different-policy long sessions and held-out cases remain
required. Preserve mixed findings and all reviewer disagreements. The main runtime
and selected experiment prompts are untouched. C41's numeric-ceiling direction
remains closed; this user-requested test changes the revision instruction itself.
