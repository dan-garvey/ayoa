The last line hangs in the room. A log shifts behind the fire screen; china clicks faintly in the dining hall.

Seraphel looks down at the page.

“Ada sounds afraid of Voss.

She wants the coat safe,

but she does not know

how to help Nell.”

Her thumb brushes one harp string.

“I liked the last line.

It made me laugh.

Read the part about last Friday again.

Then the end.

Let Ada wait a moment

before she says it.”
