The last line leaves a small, clean silence. Seraphel’s fingers rest across the harp strings until a quiet laugh escapes her.

“‘In what?’

That is very good.”

She looks at the page again.

“At first I thought the coat  
was the important thing.

Then Ada makes it clear  
that time is worse.

Nell still has ten o’clock.  
Ada has noon.”

She plucks one low string and lets it fade.

“Your lower voice made Ada sound  
as if she has already argued this  
once today.

I was angry with her.

Then I was sorry for her.

I would like to know  
whether the coat gets home.”
