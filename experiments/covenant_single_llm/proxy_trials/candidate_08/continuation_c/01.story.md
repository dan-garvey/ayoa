Caelindra acknowledges your thanks with a small inclination and opens her book again before you reach the spiral stair.

At lunch, Rashid occupies the end of the long dining table with one sleeve rolled above the fine fur at his wrist. A folded draft waits beside his plate beneath the salt cellar. He looks up when you approach and draws it free.

"We are." He taps the heading with one claw. "Sixth bell at the press. I pared it down to supervised access, the students willing to attend, and a request for the instructor's report. No speech about Academy principles. Those get used as kindling."

He opens the draft just far enough to show a tight column of signatures, then folds it again.

"I'll take the copies to the covered court afterward and ask the rest to sign there. Unless you've found some reason we shouldn't print forty."
