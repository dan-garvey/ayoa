Rashid's hand closes over the draft for one measured beat. Then he lays it across your palm.

"Then I misunderstood. When you said I could find you at the press, I took it for an arrangement, not a coincidence."

His claws click once against the tabletop as he reaches for his notebook. "The cracked platen is not your fault. Telling me at lunch leaves me less time to make another one."

He opens the notebook to a page of names. "Your story has the west workshop. I won't try to take it. I'll ask whether the copy room has a free machine, and if it hasn't, we will find another way."

Then he points to a sentence in the request.

"Read this before you decide you have nothing to do with it. Does asking for the instructor's report give them an excuse to postpone the whole matter?"
