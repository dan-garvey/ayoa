Caelindra reads without lifting her eyes from the pages. At the end, she turns the last sheet back and lets it settle over the others.

"It holds my interest." Her fingertip rests near the keeper's departure. "The medicine is concrete. So is the clock. I find myself counting from this decision to midnight, which is a useful discomfort."

She looks toward the rain-dulled library window, then back at the manuscript. "I don't need him to be admirable. I need to know whether he has exhausted the smaller choices before he abandons the light. Otherwise the neighbor is merely an excuse you have placed in his hands."

Her book remains closed beside the inkwell.

"Did he ask anyone else to fetch the medicine before he left?"
