Ashara gives one small nod. “Then you answered accurately.” Rashid retrieves his coat from the rail while she dusts the earth from her palm. Neither calls after you as you leave the octagon.

The gardens hold their own warmth behind pale stone walls. For ten minutes, the paths carry you between beds of night-blooming flowers and low trees whose leaves catch the lamp-glow. The storm remains below the Nexus, visible in white flashes through gaps in the branches but too far away to make a sound. A clipped hedge gives way to another curve of path, then the path returns you to Garvey House.

Your room is as you left it: the lamp beside the framed letter, the trunks under the window, the bridges fading beyond the gardens. The smell of damp leaves and crushed herbs follows in from the walk.
