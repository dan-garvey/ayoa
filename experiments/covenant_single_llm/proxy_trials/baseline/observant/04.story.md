When the last plates are cleared, no bell marks the end of dinner. The servers draw back to the paneled wall with trays tucked against their aprons, and conversation thins into smaller pockets beneath the lamps.

Aldric is first to rise. He smooths the front of his white-and-gold coat and leaves by the garden door without ceremony. Rashid sets both hands on the table. “One bout?”

“Until the lamps change,” Ashara says. She is already on her feet when he circles around his chair, and the two of them go out toward the quadrangle. Caelindra gathers the loose edge of her silver hair over one shoulder, says something too low for the far end of the table, and takes the library passage instead. Ysolde remains by the window a moment longer before following the garden path alone.

Thessaly is still seated across from you, one hand resting beside the empty breadbasket. Her black eyes settle on your place at the table. “Has anyone told you that you must stay?” she asks.
