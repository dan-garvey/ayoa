The dining hall is brighter than the stairwell, candle flames repeated in the varnish of the long table and in the glass of the working lamps. Academy-liveried servers move between the seventeen settings with covered dishes balanced on their palms. Near the hearth, a red-skinned woman with swept black-red horns has been speaking to the wolf-blooded man opposite her; her sentence ends when your greeting reaches the table.

The man rises halfway from his chair, his immaculate dark coat falling straight despite the fur along his hands. “Rashid vel Amara,” he says. His eyes pass over the Garvey signet before he indicates an open chair beside the pale, white-haired woman at his right. She gives you a small, level look, then resumes breaking a piece of bread between cool fingers. Around the table, silver-haired Caelindra Vaeyn adjusts a cuff; a woman in white and gold folds her small wings closer; an entirely black-eyed fae smiles at no one in particular.

“Ashara vel Kothren,” says the horned woman. Her gold eyes stay on you. “The chair is free. Before you take it, what do you intend to do with the human seat?”
