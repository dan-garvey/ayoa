Ashara’s tail touches the leg of her chair once. “Then do not,” she says. “A declaration made over a first meal would be a poor use of any seat.” She reaches for her glass, and the pressure at the table loosens enough for Rashid to sit again.

The first course arrives under silver lids: dark bread, roasted roots glossed with herb oil, thin slices of peppered meat, and a clear broth fragrant with citrus peel. Conversation reforms in separate currents. Rashid tells Aldric, “A temporary tariff that lasts four years is a system.”

“A system can still be necessary,” Aldric replies, setting his spoon precisely along the edge of his bowl. “If its removal leaves a province unable to feed itself, calling it unjust does not feed anyone.”

“It does not make it just, either,” says Caelindra without looking up from her plate. Thessaly’s smile appears a beat later. At your side, Ysolde watches the hard-light bridge beyond the window and the white movement of storm beneath it. “Does it sound different below the cloud?” she asks.
