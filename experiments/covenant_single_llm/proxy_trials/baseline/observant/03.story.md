Ysolde keeps her gaze on the window. “At this height, it has no sound,” she says. “Only light.”

Rashid and Ashara resume an argument about the dueling ground as the bowls empty. “You still take the low turn before a lunge,” he says.

“Because it works.”

“Against first-years.”

Ashara tears a piece of bread in half. “Then they should stop being first-years.” Across from her, Seraphel slides the breadbasket toward Thessaly. “For bread, no oath is due; / Yet take it, if it pleases you.” Thessaly accepts it with both hands. “It pleases me.” The lamp flames tremble above the table as servers clear the first course and bring in the next.
