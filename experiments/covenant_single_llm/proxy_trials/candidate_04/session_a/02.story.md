The white-haired woman turns from the window as you settle beside her. “Ysolde Thornmantle,” she says.

The small woman on your other side gives a belated nod. “Thessaly Morrow. The chair belonged to the house before any of us did.”

Servers bring the first covered dishes from the sideboard: roasted roots glazed dark at their edges, bowls of pale grains, and a broth carrying pepper and citrus through the candle heat. Conversation breaks into smaller pieces while plates are passed. At the far end, Caelindra has stopped reading the place cards and begun eating with exact attention to the arrangement of her knife and fork.

Rashid waits until the servers have moved on before addressing Ashara. “The covered court remains closed tomorrow. I am gathering the accounts from the practice group and asking for supervised access until they establish what injured the student.”

“Ask,” Ashara says. “My booking remains mine.”

“I did not ask for your booking.”

Aldric sets down his spoon. “The Academy may close a court after someone is hurt. Caution is not an insult to the people who use it.”

“A blanket closure made without their account is still a choice,” Rashid says.

Caelindra glances between them. “Then obtain the instructor's report before you make that choice sound corrupt.”

Ashara nods once. “And say what supervised access actually means.”

Rashid draws a folded sheet from his inner pocket, makes a note at its margin, and returns it there. “I will have both in the morning.”

The matter settles for the moment beneath the ordinary clink of cutlery. Outside the tall windows, lightning moves silently below the gardens.
