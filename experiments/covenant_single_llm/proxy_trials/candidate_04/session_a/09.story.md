Caelindra's eyes return briefly to the ink-stained edges of your fingers, then to the page beside her notes.

“That accounts for the type,” she says. “Most people who want a press want an announcement.”

She touches the nearer of her open volumes with one fingertip. “Professor Windwhisper assigned the earliest revisions to the bridge charters. I am trying to establish which changes were made to the agreement and which were only made to later copies of it.”

The page is dense with parallel columns in several scripts. A thin slip of paper marks the same passage in both books.

“It is not exciting,” Caelindra adds, without looking away from the texts. “That does not make it simple.”

She moves the printed story to the clear space beside her notebook, leaving it unopened. Then she draws an empty chair out from the table with one hand.

“You may sit if you mean to continue the conversation. I will continue working.”
