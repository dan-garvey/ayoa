As the meal breaks apart, Ashara takes up her training coat and leaves by the garden doors. Aldric excuses himself toward the chapel. Caelindra carries her notebook and the unopened story back toward the library, while Rashid, Ysolde, Thessaly, and Seraphel remain with their tea.

Rashid answers first. “People do, informally. The common room is permitted until ninth bell, if no one is trying to work there.”

“Its acoustics are poor,” Ysolde says. She lifts her cup, considering the steam. “That does not prevent music.”

Thessaly rests both hands around her own tea. “The poor player is usually forgiven. The one who insists on being admired is not.”

Seraphel's blue eyes move toward the firelit doorway of the common room.

“By firelight, later, if you choose,
I have a song that needs no news.”

Rashid gives a small, practical nod. “There are instruments in the common room cabinet. Most of them belong to former residents, so treat them as borrowed.”

Beyond the dining hall, the house's fireplace sends a low amber light across the study tables. The tea remains warm, and the evening has not yet reached the ninth bell.
