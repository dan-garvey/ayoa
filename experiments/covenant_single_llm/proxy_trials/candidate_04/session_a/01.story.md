Rashid steps back from the door, his dark jacket fitted precisely over broad shoulders. Fine fur shows at the edge of one cuff when he adjusts it.

“There is a press in Commerce for notices and student journals,” he says. “Whether it suits print-shop work, I cannot tell you. The library staff will know the better answer tomorrow.”

He turns toward the stairs without making the direction into an occasion. The common room below is warm with the fire banked in its oversized hearth. Portraits watch from the paneled walls as the two of you pass through toward the brighter dining hall.

The long table has begun to fill. A tall woman with swept black-red horns stands near one end, tightening the cuff of a dark training coat. Beside the window, a white-haired woman sits very still with her attention on the storm beyond the gardens. A silver-haired elf has claimed a place beneath one of the lamps, where she is turning a spoon by its handle while reading the place cards. Across from her, an angel in white and gold is arranging a folded napkin with unnecessary care.

Rashid enters first. “Rowan Garvey,” he says to the room. “He has found the stairs.”

The horned woman looks over. “Ashara vel Kothren.” Her gaze drops briefly to the open place midway along the table. “Sit before the food does.”

The setting waits between the white-haired woman and a small, blue-black-haired figure whose entirely black eyes lift from the candle flame.
