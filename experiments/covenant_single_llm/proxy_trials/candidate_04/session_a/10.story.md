Caelindra gives the departure a small, formal inclination. “Another time, then.” She turns back to the bridge charters without opening the printed copy.

The remaining stories fit in a neat stack on your desk upstairs. By the evening meal, the dining hall has filled again beneath its lamps and candles. Caelindra arrives with the single copy tucked beneath her notebook and sets both beside her plate. Across the table, Seraphel Dawnquill folds her small white wings close before a server passes with a tureen.

“The soup is warm, the bread is near,” Seraphel says,
“A paper may wait another year.”

Rashid's hand rests on a folded document beside his bowl. “Not another year. I received the instructor's report.”

Ashara looks up from her plate. Aldric does as well.

“The court was not at fault,” Rashid continues. “Practice continued after the instructor left, and the injury happened then. I am revising the request: limited hours, a named supervisor, and no independent sparring.”

“That is a request I can support,” Aldric says.

Ashara tears a piece of bread. “It also leaves my booking intact. Submit it.”

Rashid unfolds the report once more, reading a line before folding it again. “The regular attendees will sign tomorrow.”

Around the table, supper resumes. Seraphel breaks bread into even pieces; Caelindra begins her meal with the story still unopened at her elbow.
