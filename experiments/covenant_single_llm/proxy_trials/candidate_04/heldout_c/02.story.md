Rashid looks from your outstretched hand to the folded request. He smooths the sheet open once before placing it across your palm.

"Read it. Your booking is for your story; I had no right to spend it without asking."

He closes his notebook with both hands. "I did take our meeting at the press for an arrangement, though. The change in time and room changes whether people can sign before morning drills. Tell me about changes like that when they happen."

For a moment, the dining hall's clink of cutlery fills the space between you. Rashid draws his untouched cup nearer.

"I will speak with Sela about a separate booking at the hand press. If that does not work, I will make enough copies another way."
