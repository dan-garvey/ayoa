Caelindra reads in silence, one hand resting lightly over the lower corner of each page as she turns it. At the end, she returns to the first sheet and traces the margin beside the keeper's departure with one pale fingernail.

"Yes," she says at last. "It holds my interest. The keeper has two duties, and neither is trivial. That is sufficient."

She looks across the table, violet eyes level. "The absent replacement is the sharper matter. Medicine can be carried by someone. The light cannot be tended by everyone at once. Whether he had reason to trust that arrangement is more interesting than whether he meant well."

The pages are squared carefully against the tabletop. "Do not arrange a calamity merely to demonstrate that darkness is serious. The dark is already serious to anyone who had expected the lamp."

The library clock gives a thin quarter-bell. Caelindra leaves her own book face-down beside the inkwell and keeps the manuscript between you rather than sliding it back.
