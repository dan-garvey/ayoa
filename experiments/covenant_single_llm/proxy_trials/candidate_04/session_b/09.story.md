The timetable card leaves second bell unmarked. At the marshal’s desk, the foundation-course ledger already holds a column of names in several hands. Your name joins them beneath the heading for the north ring.

By dinner, Garvey House is lit against the darkening gardens. The dining hall has fewer candles than it did for the welcome meal, but the long table is set again. Rashid, Ashara, Ysolde, Thessaly, and Seraphel are already there when you arrive.

“Good evening,” Rashid says, lifting a hand from a small stack of papers.

Ysolde gives a short nod from her place near the window. Seraphel’s greeting comes softly over the clink of serving spoons. “The house is warm, the table laid; / come take the supper daylight made.”

Once you are seated, Rashid squares the papers beside his plate. “Twelve signatures. I will submit the access request in the morning.”

Ashara cuts a piece of roasted squash. “Submit it. The report should not take long.”

“It has already taken longer than the restriction was announced to take,” Rashid says.

Thessaly reaches for the bread. “Those are different measurements of time.”

“They should not be,” Rashid replies, though the sharpness has gone from his voice.

The servers bring in a dish of rice and spiced vegetables. Beyond the windows, the gardens hold their lamp-globes like a second, smaller constellation.
