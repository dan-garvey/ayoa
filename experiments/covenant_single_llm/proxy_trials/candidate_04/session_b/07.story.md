By the sixth bell, the benches have filled three rows deep. Two wardens stand inside the octagon with a marshal in grey Academy robes, while a healer waits beside the gate with a case of glass vials. Silver powder marks the boundary in an unbroken eight-sided line.

Ashara enters in a fitted practice jacket with a blunt-edged saber at her hip. Varo Kest, broad-shouldered and bronze-scaled along the temples, carries a padded spear. The marshal reads the terms in a voice that reaches the back benches: one sustained spell at a time; no attack after a raised hand, a surrender, or a call to halt; a clean mark, a forced step beyond the boundary, or a three-count pin takes an exchange.

The first bell sends them forward. Kest keeps the spear between them, testing distance with quick thrusts. Ashara gives ground twice, then catches the shaft against her saber and turns it aside. On the third pass, Kest changes height and strikes the padded point cleanly against her ribs.

“Hold.” A warden’s hand rises. Both weapons stop where they are. The marshal marks Kest’s first exchange, checks Ashara’s jacket, and signals them back to their starting stones.

The second exchange lasts longer. Ashara lets Kest press her toward the silver line, then slips inside the spear’s reach. A brief red flare runs along her blade as she knocks the weapon wide; her shoulder drives into Kest’s chest, and Kest’s heel crosses the powder.

Another raised hand, another halt. The score is even.

On the last exchange, Kest abandons the long reach for a rush. Ashara meets it without magic, catches the spear under her arm, and turns with the motion. Kest hits the packed earth on one knee. Before the count reaches three, the saber’s padded edge rests against the side of Kest’s throat.

The marshal steps between them and lifts Ashara’s wrist. “Two exchanges to one. Kothren retains rank.”

Kest accepts the offered hand up. The crowd breaks into applause as the wardens brush the disturbed silver back into a clean line.
