By the time the last of the fruit tart and bitter tea is gone, the servers have begun removing the center dishes. Rashid has his packet open again, writing names into a narrow column beside the request.

“I’ll speak to the other attendees before they settle in,” he says, sliding the paper beneath his arm. “The lower library study is usually full after dinner.”

Caelindra sets down her cup. “Not the western tables. I need those tonight.”

“Then I will use the eastern ones.” Rashid rises, his chair making only a soft sound against the floor.

Aldric stands as well. “If the report shows a remedy is possible, bring it through the proper channel.” He straightens the cuff at his wrist. “I will consider it then.” His route takes him through the common room toward the chapel doors, where the dusk lamps have begun to glow.

Ashara remains seated long enough to finish her tea. “You will have an answer before tomorrow’s booking,” she tells Rashid.

“I expect one.” He leaves the dining hall with the packet tucked close to his side.

The formal arrangement loosens around his departure. Caelindra gathers a slim stack of notes and goes toward the library; Seraphel lingers by the candles, speaking softly with Thessaly. Ysolde has not left her chair, her attention on the storm-white bridge beyond the windows.

The common room fire is lit, and the stairs to the upper floors stand open beyond it as the last plates are cleared away.
