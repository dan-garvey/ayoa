The marshal finishes checking the red flare residue from Ashara’s saber before returning it to her. Kest has already collected the padded spear and gone through the gate with two friends. When the last official steps away, Ashara loosens the wrap at one wrist; heat still lifts faintly from the dark fabric at her shoulders.

She listens without looking past you toward the thinning crowd.

“Thank you,” she says. “Kest committed to the rush before the spear was clear. That makes the turn possible.”

She secures the saber at her hip. “There is a foundation weapons course. They begin with stance, distance, falling, and how to stop when someone calls a halt. You will use padded sticks until an instructor decides otherwise.”

Her gaze shifts once toward the north side of the grounds, where a smaller practice ring sits beyond the benches. “Second bell tomorrow. North ring. Master Lorne keeps the roster.”

Ashara starts toward the gate, then adds, “Wear boots you can run in. The first week is mostly learning not to hurt yourself.”

The marshal’s desk remains open near the entrance, with a narrow sign-up ledger beside the stack of bout cards.
