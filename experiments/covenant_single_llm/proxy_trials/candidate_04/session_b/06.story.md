Morning begins with the house bell and the scent of bread rising from the dining hall. Breakfast gives way to orientation in the Conclave seminar room, where the oval table sits beneath the framed Accords and an Academy registrar moves through timetables, access rules, and the routes between departments.

The scheduled classes carry the day onward in smaller rooms: introductions, reading lists, names to learn, and faculty who expect the first-years to find their seats without ceremony. By the time the last session lets out, the quadrangle lamps have not yet lit and the storm below the plateau has turned a dull slate blue.

The dueling grounds lie past the low dragon-carved arches, an open octagon of packed earth surrounded by stepped benches. Public notices cover a broad black-stone board at its entrance. One fresh placard bears a red ranking seal:

ASHARA VEL KOTHREN v. VARO KEST  
RANKED BOUT — SIXTH BELL

Below it, a separate Academy notice confirms that the covered court remains restricted pending Master Oriven’s report. Someone has added a neat strip of paper beneath the order: SUPERVISED-ACCESS REQUEST AVAILABLE IN THE LIBRARY, EASTERN TABLES.

The octagon is empty for now, its surface newly raked. The sixth bell is still ahead, and a few students have begun claiming places on the benches.
