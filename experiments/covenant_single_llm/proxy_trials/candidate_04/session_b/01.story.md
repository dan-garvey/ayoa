The server’s lamp travels ahead of you down the stairs. The dining hall opens warm and bright beyond the common room: seventeen places span the long varnished table, though only seven chairs are occupied and one is waiting. Candles stand between low bowls of herbs and fruit; working lamps soften the paneling.

A conversation near the center breaks at your greeting. The wolf-blooded young man nearest the head of the table closes a thin packet of papers and gets to his feet. “Rashid vel Amara.” His cuffs fall precisely over the dark fur at his wrists. “Welcome to Garvey House.”

Across from him, the horned woman in a sleeveless black formal coat gives a brief inclination of her head. Heat shimmers faintly above the tea at her place. “Ashara vel Kothren.”

“Ysolde Thornmantle,” says the pale woman two chairs farther down, before returning her gaze to the storm beyond the tall windows.

A silver-haired elf offers only, “Caelindra Vaeyn.” Near her, the small fae woman watches the place cards rather than the introductions. “Thessaly Morrow.”

The angel with small white wings tucked neatly behind her shoulders smiles and says, in clear verse, “Seraphel Dawnquill, at your table tonight; / May supper prove kinder than the weather’s light.”

Aldric Verantus rises last, his white-and-gold sleeve brushing the back of his chair. “Aldric Verantus.”

An ivory card bearing ROWAN GARVEY marks an empty setting midway down the table, between Thessaly and Ysolde. A server holds the chair clear.
