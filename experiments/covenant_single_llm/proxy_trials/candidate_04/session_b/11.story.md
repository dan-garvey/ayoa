Rashid gives a brief inclination of his head. “So do I.”

Dinner continues into quieter matters: tomorrow’s reading, a complaint about the registrar’s timetables, the last of the tea. When the table begins to empty, goodnights pass between the remaining students without holding anyone in place.

Upstairs, the room is cool after the warmth of the dining hall. The suitable clothes and boots wait beside the bed, ready for the foundation course. The desk lamp is turned low; beyond the window, the garden globes keep their pale paths visible until the stormlight erases them.

Morning comes with the house bell and a thin wash of grey through the curtains. The clothes and boots remain where they were set out, and the second bell has not yet rung.
