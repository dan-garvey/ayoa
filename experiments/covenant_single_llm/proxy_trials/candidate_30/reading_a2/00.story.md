The paper settles against your knee. Seraphel keeps the harp quiet beneath her palm. Firelight touches the small white feathers at her shoulder; beyond the open door, a stack of plates gives a soft porcelain clink.

“Your lower voice
makes Ada tired,
not cruel.

That is harder.”

She looks at the page rather than taking it.

“‘I was the one who answered’—
that is the line
I believed most.

Nell looks toward ten.
Ada has been looking
at the door.”

One corner of Seraphel's mouth lifts.

“‘In what?’ is sharp.

I laughed,
and then I worried
for the coat.”

She draws a low, brief chord from the harp, as if testing whether it belongs after the scene, and lets it fade into the fire's small crackle.
