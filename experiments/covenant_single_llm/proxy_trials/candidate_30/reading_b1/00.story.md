The page makes a faint crackle as it settles against your knee. Seraphel's hands have gone still on the harp. Beyond the tall windows, lightning moves soundlessly through the storm; the common room holds its small circle of firelight around the two of you.

She looks at the paper once more, then at you.

“Nell asks Ada
to come with her.

Then she gives Ada
the pavement.

I did not expect that
until Ada asked,
‘In what?’

It made me laugh.
It made me wish
I had not laughed.”

Her mouth curves briefly at the confession.

“Nell believes Friday
will arrive because she needs it.
Ada has already met Friday.

Your voices made that clear.”

Seraphel picks out two notes: one quick and high, the other low enough to settle beneath it. She lets the second fade before she speaks again.

“I would listen
when you find
the next part.”
