The paper makes a soft sound as you lower it. Seraphel has stopped touching the strings; the harp rests dark across her knees. In the fire, a knot in one log gives way and sends two sparks behind the screen.

“‘In what?’” she says.

She gives the line the lower voice you used for Ada, gently, as though trying its weight.

“That is good.

Nell is in a hurry.

Ada has already opened
the door for Voss.

You make them sound
as if they have had
this argument before.”

Her thumb brushes a harp string without sounding it.

“I like Ada.

Not because she is right.

Because she does not let Nell
make the coat
belong to her alone.”

Seraphel looks at the page again, then back at you.

“I would hear more
when there is more.”
