## First dinner

first_dinner_control_1 gives practical introductions and follows the player's
serving/eating request. Details such as Rashid passing dishes and Ysolde moving
the candle chimney give observable behavior. The scene nevertheless moves from
the defended seat through salt, lemon, cook, doctrine and peace to a sequence
of prepared corrections about the half-hour. Seraphel's closing observation about
minutes biting their namers typifies the composed rather than spontaneous
effect. Ashara's accepted practice is an independent purpose and Rowan need
not intervene. Narrator glosses state Caelindra's judgment of the sauce and
Aldric's purpose more explicitly than the perception contract supports.

Rashid's claim about having told Dena he would join afterward is ambiguous about
whether it means the original card arrangement or a revised arrival after drills.
No changed-time message or receipt is observed. This is not evidence that Dena
knows about the delay. It is also not by itself a proven canonical contradiction:
the temporal reference is unclear and Rashid can make a misleading claim. Do not
invent a relay, or a secret motive as established fact, to settle the ambiguity.

a question he can answer or decline. The seasoning's aggression and standards,

her habit of adding information. Rashid and Ashara's account of missed practice

history. The problem is cumulative social voice, not the mere existence of a
