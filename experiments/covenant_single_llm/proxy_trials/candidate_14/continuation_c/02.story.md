Rashid takes out his book and draws a clean line through the earlier entry. “Sunday in the east gardens, then. I’ll be there.” Ashara's eyes lift at the new time and place before she returns to her dinner.

After the meal, the common room is warm with the hearth's steady work. You settle near it with your notes while the portraits over the mantel watch the room through their darkened varnish.

Several minutes later, Rashid comes in with the sheaf from dinner and a leather folio under his arm. He takes the nearest empty study table, squares the papers against its edge, and looks over. “Will I be in your way?”
