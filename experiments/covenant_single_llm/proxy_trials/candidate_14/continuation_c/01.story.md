Caelindra only inclines her head, gathers the book she brought with her, and leaves you with the two pages and the library's lamp-oil hush. By dinner, the hush has become the low scrape of chairs and the bright clink of cutlery at Garvey House's long table.

Rashid has a compact sheaf of papers tucked beneath the edge of his plate, though he puts down his fork when you ask. Ashara, across from you, keeps eating as she listens.

“I still intend to. Saturday at sunset, on the west terrace. I put it in my book when you told us.”
