Caelindra takes the pages by their upper corners, careful of the ink, and reads them beneath the nearest lamp. The library goes on around her in small sounds: a chair settling somewhere below the iron stair, the dry brush of a turning page. When she reaches the end, she returns to the first sheet and reads the deputy's refusal once more.

She slides both pages back across the table. “I want the ferrywoman's choice. The bell has made it unavoidable, and the deputy makes it personal without trying to be interesting. I want to see what she believes she owes people she will never meet. Have you decided?”
