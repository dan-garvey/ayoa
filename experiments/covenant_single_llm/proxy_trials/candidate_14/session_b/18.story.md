Stone receives the letters without gathering them too quickly. He reties the faded ribbon around the packet, sets it beside the green-shaded lamp, and returns your good night with a quiet courtesy before the book and correspondence reclaim the table.

The common room is warmer than the library, its oversized fireplace down to a bed of red coals. Rashid occupies one end of a study table beneath the dead Garvey portraits. A neat stack of papers lies before him: the request on top, signatures beneath, and an Academy notice weighted flat by an ink bottle.

He looks up when you ask about the court. “Enough for a meeting tomorrow at fourth bell; the court remains closed until the instructor's report is reviewed. Ashara signed, Aldric wants the report before his name joins, and the injured student's account will not be made public.”

Rashid slides the notice across the table just far enough to show the date and the dean's office seal. The request itself is written narrowly: supervised access, an explanation of the restriction, and a review after the report is available. No one name is made to carry the whole argument.
