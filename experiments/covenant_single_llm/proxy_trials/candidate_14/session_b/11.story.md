Rashid's expression eases as he reaches for his coffee. “I am glad you found him. Stone is better company once he decides a question deserves an answer.”

The porridge is thick with toasted oats and a little honey, and the coffee has been brewed strong enough to cut through the morning's damp chill. At the sideboard, Thessaly looks up from the tiles as you address her. One pale blue piece rests between two darker ones, its painted path ending at the edge of the grid.

“It is Seven Paths,” she says. “You turn one tile each move and must connect every border gate to the center without sending a road back across itself; yes, I will teach you when this round is finished.”

She studies the blue tile again, then sets one fingertip beside it without moving it. The empty chair across from her has been pulled a little clear of the sideboard.
