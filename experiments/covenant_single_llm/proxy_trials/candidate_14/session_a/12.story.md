The next two days acquire the Academy’s working rhythm. Morning bells pull students through the quadrangle in coats and uniforms; assigned reading fills the intervals between seminars with treaty clauses, case studies, and arguments that have survived longer than their authors. In the afternoons, the gardens offer a quieter surface: flowering walls under the pale paths, clipped shadows from the sheltering stone, and the hard-light bridges beyond the hedges. Your sketchbook gathers the bends of stems, lamp brackets, and the storm’s changing edge beneath the plateau.

On the second evening, the dining hall is warm with the smell of cumin and baked roots. Rashid arrives with a blue Academy envelope tucked under one arm. Aldric’s name now appears beneath Ashara’s on the copy of the request at his place.

“Administration will hear us Friday; the court stays closed until the instructor’s report arrives, but they will consider a supervised trial.”

“That is a reasonable answer,” Aldric says.

“Then bring a plan worth accepting,” Ashara says.

Rashid folds the Academy letter back along its creases and sets it beside his bowl. The request has become an appointment, not a victory, and dinner continues around it.
