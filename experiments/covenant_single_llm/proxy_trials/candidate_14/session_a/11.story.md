The library absorbs the rest of the afternoon in lamp-oil air and the hush of high shelves. The primer gives the Covenant its public sequence again—war, treaties, Council, Academy—then returns to the small hazards of address, seating, gifts, and promises. Light slips away from the reading tables before you reach the final page.

At dinner, Rashid has a clean copy of his request folded beside his plate. Ashara takes a seat farther down the table from him, her hair still damp at the ends from the changing rooms. The servers set out bowls of barley and greens while the other conversations find their places.

Rashid opens the paper. “I am submitting a request for supervised access in the morning: no names attached, and no verdict on the accident.”

Aldric sets down his spoon. “Give it to me after dinner. I will read it before I decide.”

“That is what reading is for,” Ashara says.

She draws the request toward herself, reads it through, and signs at the bottom with a decisive stroke. Rashid folds the paper once more after she passes it back. Around them, the meal resumes its quieter business of bread, lamps, and low talk.
