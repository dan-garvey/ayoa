Rashid tears a piece from his bread and considers it for just long enough that the question does not disappear into the table’s other talk. His amber eyes flick once toward Ashara.

“Practice, cards, and dinner with people who do not ask me to chair anything. I also enjoy an evening in which Ashara agrees with me once, though that is less common than a clean draw.”

Ashara’s tail taps the chair leg once. “Your definitions require revision.”

Rashid’s mouth turns at one corner as he reaches for the glazed onions. Across from him, Seraphel lowers her gaze to her bowl with the suggestion of a smile, while the storm flashes soundlessly beyond the gardens and turns the far wall of the dining hall briefly white.
