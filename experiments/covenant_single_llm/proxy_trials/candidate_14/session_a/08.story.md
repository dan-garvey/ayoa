Sleep passes without ceremony. In the morning, Conclave seminar begins beneath the framed Accords with Professor Windwhisper separating a dispute over Article Seven into the meanings of three nearly identical words. Practical Rhetoric follows in the afternoon, where Professor Kira has the room take opposing sides of a trade petition until the last bell releases everyone into the cooling light outside.

The public dueling ground is busy enough that a small crowd has gathered along the rail. Chalk on the ranking board gives the score before the action does: KOTHREN 2, VOSS 2. Inside the packed-earth octagon, Ashara faces a dark-haired dragon student with a long ash staff. The air around his weapon tugs dust into thin, circling lines. Heat shimmers close to Ashara’s blunted saber and the skin of her forearms.

Voss comes in with the staff high, then whips its butt toward her ribs on the turn. Ashara gives ground once, twice, her tail clearing the path of her heels. On the third step, she catches the staff just below its midpoint with her blade. A finger-width flare runs along the flat of the weapon, bright as a coal drawn from a fire.

Voss shifts his grip away from the heat. Ashara is already inside the staff’s reach. Her shoulder turns, her saber comes around, and its padded tip strikes the center of his chest before the gust around him can tighten again.

The referee’s raised hand brings the crowd’s noise up all at once. A runner changes the board to KOTHREN 3, VOSS 2. Ashara steps back and lowers her blade while Voss bows over the length of his staff, then the two of them leave the marked center to their respective sides of the rail.
