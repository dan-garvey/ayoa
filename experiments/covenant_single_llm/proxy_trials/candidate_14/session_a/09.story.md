The referee finishes recording the score and hands Ashara a strip of clean cloth for the sweat at her brow. She takes it, gives the board one brief look, and sets her blunted saber into its scabbard before turning from the rail.

At your invitation, she stops beside the equipment bench. Her gold eyes hold on you without wandering toward the dispersing crowd.

“Thank you. No; I do not know you well enough to spend an evening alone with you.”

She loops the cloth around the hilt guard, takes up her water flask, and heads toward the changing rooms along the outer edge of the ground.
