The dining hall is bright with candles and working lamps, their light caught in the varnish of the long table. Seven places are occupied beneath the portraits on the paneled walls. A server draws back from the doorway as you enter.

“—not every student who practices there was even present when the injury happened,” says the young man nearest the fire. Fine dark fur shows at his cuffs where his hands rest beside an untouched glass. “Supervision is what we asked for.”

Across from him, the gold-haired angel has one palm flat against the table. “And until they know whether supervision would have prevented it, the restriction is sensible.”

Your greeting carries over the table. The angel turns first. “Aldric Verantus.”

The other man stands. “Rashid vel Amara. We were discussing the covered court.” His courtesy is exact, but he gives no apology for the discussion.

At Rashid's right, the horned woman shifts an empty chair out from the table with one boot. “Ashara vel Kothren,” she says. “There is a place.”

The silver-haired elf inclines her head without rising. “Caelindra Vaeyn.” Beside her, the pale woman with grey eyes says, “Ysolde Thornmantle.”

The woman with black eyes watches the exchange a moment longer. “Thessaly Morrow.”

Small white wings settle against the shoulders of the woman at the far end of the table. “A human comes where old names remain; / may supper prove less sharp than claim,” she says.

Ashara glances toward her. “Seraphel Dawnquill.”

The empty setting waits opposite Rashid, its name card marked ROWAN GARVEY in gold. A covered soup tureen steams at its center while the argument about the court hangs unfinished between Rashid and Aldric.
