Ysolde waits while the last of the three students takes the petition from Rashid's table. When there is room to pass, she carries her tea pot toward the fire.

At your question, she stops beside the nearer mantel column.

“The south garden wall,” she says. “Go out this door and follow the path left. There is a stone bench under the glass-leaf trees.”

She shifts the pot slightly in her hands. “The wall keeps most of the wind off it. People use the path, so it is not private.”

Beyond the open garden door, the pale path runs between low flowering beds toward a line of taller trees. Their leaves catch the lamp-glow with a faint green sheen. The storm remains below the plateau, its distant light briefly whitening the sheltered wall at the far end.
