Stormlight shifts beyond the window, pale and soundless over the gardens below. The nearest bridge rises from the plateau in a narrow bar of white, then disappears into cloud.

Your room has been made ready with the efficiency of people who expect a name to arrive before they expect its owner. The bed is turned down. A row of clothes sent by the lawyers hangs beside the wardrobe. On the desk, the Conclave primer lies open beside a lamp, its notes on the welcome dinner and the Covenant's prohibitions marked with blue ribbon. The framed letter on the wall is written in an older hand and addressed to a great-nephew.

Your charcoal coat rests across the chair back, its careful mending visible at one cuff. The Garvey signet catches the lamp's light when you move near it.

Below, crockery begins to clink in the dining hall. A voice carries up through the stairwell, then another answers; neither stays distinct through the house's old wood and high ceilings.

A knock comes at the door. A server in dark Academy livery waits in the corridor with a covered silver tray balanced on one hand. “Mr. Garvey. The others are gathering now. Dinner is in ten minutes.”

Behind the server, the stairs lead down toward the warm light of the common room. The tray holds a glass of water beaded with condensation and a folded card bearing only the Academy seal.
