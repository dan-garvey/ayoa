When the last bowls have been cleared, no formal dismissal follows. The servers set a pot of tea and a plate of small pears at one end of the table, then begin gathering the unused silver.

Aldric rises first. “I have chapel.” He gives the table a brief nod and leaves through the hall's outer door, white and gold disappearing into the darker passage beyond.

Rashid draws the petition back toward himself and slips it into his jacket. “I will ask the director for the report in the morning. If anyone wants to add their name after that, I will be in the common room this evening.”

“Ask for the accident record as well,” Ashara says. “Not an account of it.”

“I intend to.”

She stands, chair legs quiet against the floor. “Good.” Her own copy of the request goes into a narrow leather case with the rest of her papers.

Ysolde remains seated while a server pours hot water over a handful of dark leaves in a small pot. Caelindra closes the book she has brought to dinner but does not yet pick it up. Thessaly takes one of the pears, turns it in her fingers, and says to Rashid, “The director may refuse the report.”

“Then I will know he has refused it,” Rashid says.

From the common room, firelight reaches through the open doors and lays a wavering band across the dining-hall floor. Rashid heads that way with the petition; Ashara takes the opposite corridor toward the stairs, while the others linger among the cups and cooling lamps.
