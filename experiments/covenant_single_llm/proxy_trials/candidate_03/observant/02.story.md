A server lifts the tureen lid, and the table fills with the scent of pepper and roasted roots. Bowls arrive one by one; bread follows in a wicker basket.

Rashid waits until the servers have moved away. “I am not asking the Academy to pretend nobody was hurt. I am asking for supervised access while they establish what happened.” He takes a folded sheet from inside his jacket and sets it near his plate. “The students who attend the group have given me their accounts. I have not seen the instructor's report.”

“Then you cannot know whether a supervisor would address the cause,” Aldric says.

“Neither can the faculty know that closing the court addresses it.”

Ashara breaks a piece of bread between her fingers. “The restriction is broad. That does not make my booking tomorrow available for your group.”

“It is not in the request,” Rashid says.

Thessaly reaches for the sheet, reads the first lines, and passes it back. “It is not.”

Ysolde looks from the paper to Rashid. “Who would supervise?”

“A Military-track student approved by the court master, or an instructor. That is part of what I want the director to answer.”

Aldric takes the sheet when Rashid offers it. He reads in silence while the candles burn steadily between the place settings. Caelindra has turned her attention to her soup; Seraphel hums a few quiet notes under her breath.

After a moment, Aldric folds the paper along its existing crease. “I will read the report before I sign anything.”

Rashid accepts it back and places it beside the salt cellar. “Fair enough. I am meeting the director after morning chapel.”

Ashara's copy of the request remains by her bowl as the first course cools.
