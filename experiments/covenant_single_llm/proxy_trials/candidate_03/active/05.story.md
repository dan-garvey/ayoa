Your greetings earn brief answers over breakfast. Rashid sets his pencil aside long enough to return them; Aldric closes his prayer book around one finger and nods from farther down the table. Ysolde raises her cup a fraction without turning from the window.

At the third bell, the first-years take their places in the oval Conclave seminar room beneath the framed Accords. Professor Windwhisper speaks through orientation without raising her voice: course placements, access rules, faculty offices, and the permissions required before anyone may cross a bridge. The scheduled sessions that follow carry you between rooms until the afternoon lamps are beginning to wake in the quadrangle.

The pressroom door stands open when you return to the Commerce wing. Inside, cases of type line one wall, their drawers marked in neat black letters. A young man with rolled sleeves is sorting small metal pieces into a tray beside the press.

“Toma Oris?”

He looks up when you ask about the booking. “That is me.” He wipes his fingertips on a rag and checks the board in the passage. “Your block is tomorrow, fifth and sixth bell. Twelve copies will fit in that once the type is set. It will not cover setting, proofing, corrections, and two pages as well.”

He taps an unclaimed interval earlier that day. “This is still open for preparation. Before I approve the second form, I need to know whether you have run a platen press before. Setting type is useful, but it is not the same work.”
