The dining hall is warmer than the corridor, lamps and candles bright against the paneled walls. Only eight of the seventeen places are occupied or waiting. At the near end of the long table, a dark-haired beastkin man moves a folder of papers from the chair beside him and draws it out a little from the table.

You give your name, your former work at the printer, and your question about a press.

"Rashid vel Amara," he says. "There is one in the Commerce wing. The student circular has it two afternoons a week; the rest is booked through its steward. You pay for your paper and type, and keep the room clean. If you have actually set type, they will be glad of another pair of hands."

Across from him, the horned woman in a dark academy coat looks up from the folded notice at her plate. "Ashara vel Kothren. The booking board is outside the pressroom. It is usually accurate."

"Ysolde Thornmantle," says the pale woman beside her. After a moment, she adds, "It is loud enough to hear from the stair."

The silver-haired elf gives her name as Caelindra Vaeyn without lifting her attention from the small book open before her. A small fae woman with black eyes says, "Thessaly Morrow," and begins reaching for the bread basket. The angel in white and gold inclines her head.

"Seraphel Dawnquill, glad you come to dine;\n+May ink find honest hands and good design."

Rashid taps the folder square against the table's edge and returns it to the space before him. The empty chair remains drawn back beside him, its place setting still untouched.
