The envelope holds a cream-coloured timetable and a short notice from the Conclave office. First-year orientation is set for the third bell in the seminar room, with Professor Elara Windwhisper presiding. The notice asks new residents to bring any questions about their course placements and says that bridge permissions will be addressed after the opening session. A narrow map of the Academy is enclosed behind it, its routes marked in seven coloured inks.

By the time you have dressed and folded the papers back into the envelope, the tray outside has cooled enough that the tea no longer sends up steam.

Downstairs, the dining hall has shed the candlelit formality of the previous evening. Morning light lies across the long table, where covered dishes and a pot of coffee stand among the place settings. Rashid is already there, writing at one end of the table with a fresh stack of papers arranged by his elbow. Aldric sits farther down with a cup before him, reading a small prayer book. Ysolde occupies a window-side chair, one hand curved around a bowl of tea while she looks out toward the storm.

The envelope remains in your hand as you reach the doorway. A server in Academy livery carries in a platter of eggs and pauses to set it between the empty places.
