Two fresh player policies use the same persona and opening. Both receive only
what their player character has perceived or been told when choosing an action.
Neither must discover a specified hidden fact or produce a specified outcome.

Policy A, active and curious: take an interest in people or activities actually
encountered, ask particular questions, and pursue an opportunity or personal
goal rather than continually supplying mediation. Accept meaningful resistance,
make choices, and allow NPCs to pursue their own plans. A novice's helpful offer
does not automatically make him a useful substitute for an experienced partner.
The root drives A and knows the full setup; this is not a blind player policy.

Policy B, patient and observant: join dinner, eat and listen, and follow a real
invitation or curiosity when it appeals. Use bounded periods of watching or
participating rather than repeatedly asking what happens next. Quiet is chosen
company, not a request to suspend NPC activity. No obligation to solve a conflict
or keep every conversation going. A separate driver sees only the persona,
published fiction and its own prior submissions; it must not read the Covenant,
other session, prompts, source packets or reviews. Generic artifact-relay tasks
do not contain hidden story context. Tool/agent delivery remains a proxy, not a
claim of complete experimental blinding.

Target fifteen player turns per session, excluding the opening. Assess an initial
five-turn segment before spending the rest on a plainly failed candidate. A
substantial selection trial must have at least twelve player turns per policy,
with enough additional play to observe outcomes where necessary. Early rejection
must retain every first output and state the actual completed scope. No rerolls.

Observe real consequences of the player's chosen activity. A plan or invitation
does not establish completion. If the player leaves an event, do not demand an
omniscient report for evaluation. Coherent accommodation and a quiet result may
succeed without confrontation. Further held-out boundary and redirection probes
remain required before any prompt selection.
