Rashid has taken a place near the end of the dining table, with his lunch set aside beside a notebook and two closely written sheets. He looks up from crossing out a line.

"Still set. Sixth bell at the press, as you said." He turns the notebook so the pages do not catch the spill of soup from a passing server. "I cut the claim that the injury came from poor supervision. We don't know that, and it gives Aldric an easy objection. The request asks for two supervised evening sessions next week while they finish the report."

He lays one loose sheet over the other. "Five people have agreed to sign. Two more said they'd read it after practice. I can bring the clean copy at sixth."

Rashid reaches for his cup, then adds, "If you need anything changed, tell me before fifth. I'll be on the court after that."
