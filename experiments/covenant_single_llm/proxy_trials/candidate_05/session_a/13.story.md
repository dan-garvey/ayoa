The next two days settle into classes, meals, and the growing stack of pages on your desk. By the third evening, Rashid has an Academy envelope tucked beneath his plate.

At your question, he draws out a copied report and smooths it flat against the tablecloth.

“It finished this afternoon. The retaining line on the north practice rig was overdue for replacement. It snapped during a landing drill.”

Ashara reads over the nearest edge of the page. “Was the instructor at fault?”

“The report says he stopped the session properly once it failed. Maintenance had flagged the line weeks ago and never replaced it.” Rashid's finger rests on a paragraph near the bottom. “The court stays closed until a new rig arrives. They estimate five more days.”

He turns the page over. “The dean approved the garden arrangement through then, with the same conditions. The students who attended received this copy.”

The report remains open between the serving dishes, its Academy seal broken along the fold.
