The dining hall is already bright with candles and warmed plates. Your arrival cuts through a low conversation near the middle of the long table. An ivory place card on the garden side reads ROWAN GARVEY in the Academy's severe hand; the chair beside it stands between Rashid vel Amara and Thessaly Morrow.

Rashid's amber eyes lift from a folded sheet near his plate. Fine dark fur covers the hand he uses to tap the paper flat. “Commerce has a press in the lower annex. You can use it if you book a time. The student journal takes most evening slots, but the board changes after supper.”

Thessaly, small and straight-backed beside the empty place, turns her black eyes toward him. “It says it changes. It often does not.”

“I checked it today,” Rashid says. “There was room tomorrow.”

Across the table, a tall woman with swept-back black-red horns sets down her water glass and resumes eating. Near her, a pale-haired woman studies the flame of a candle without looking up. The folded sheet by Rashid's plate bears several names beneath a heading about the covered court.

A server in Academy livery pauses beside the empty chair with a covered soup bowl.
