At Garvey House, the brown-paper wrap becomes a small stack on your desk, with one unwrapped copy kept aside. The library is lit for the evening when you reach it. Lamp-oil hangs lightly in the air beneath the iron stair, and most of the lower tables are occupied by students bent over their own work.

On the upper gallery, Caelindra Vaeyn has a table to herself near the railing. A thick volume lies open before her, held flat by a silver paperknife. She is copying something from it into a narrow notebook when your offer reaches her.

Her violet eyes settle on the printed pages, then on you. “Two pages,” she says. “That is merciful.”

She sets the paperknife across the open volume and moves a clear space beside it. “Leave it there.”
