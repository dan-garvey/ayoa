Rashid keeps the new sheet and the signed petition at his place when dinner ends. The rest of the evening passes in the quiet of your room, with the house settling around you and the garden lamps beyond the window dimming one by one.

At breakfast, Caelindra is already at the long table. Your printed copy lies beside her plate, folded once along its center. She looks up when you ask about it.

“I did.” She rests one fingertip on the paper. “The bowl is the better choice. The umbrella gets Tobin into the room; the bowl tells him he may stay. You stop before explaining it, which is why it works.”

She refolds the copy along its existing crease and sets it beside her cup.
