Over the following days, classes keep their hours and the pages of your second story gather across the desk. The garden practice continues under its posted limits until the afternoon named in the repair notice.

The covered court doors stand open when you reach them. A fresh retaining line runs above the north rig, pale against the darker beams. Near the entrance, an Academy notice bears a new inspector's seal: COURT REOPENED FOR SUPERVISED USE. A second sheet beneath it records the replacement date and the maintenance order number.

Rashid is already inside with the practice group. He checks the line's attachment once, then moves them into the open floor beneath it. The students leave their garden boundary cord coiled by the wall, along with the small sign that had kept the paths clear.

“Pairs,” Rashid says. “Start with the landing sequence. Take the full space this time.”

The first two students step out across the packed court floor as the others spread back to give them room.
