Dinner disperses without lingering speeches. By the time you bring tea into the common room, the fire has been built up against the evening chill and only a few lamps are lit.

Thessaly occupies one end of the long table with Rashid's revised request open beside her notebook. She has copied a line from the new notice onto a separate sheet and is comparing the two in silence.

Near the hearth, Seraphel sits in a leather chair with a book open across her lap. The gold at her skin softens in the firelight. She looks up as you enter, then returns to the page without speaking.

Thessaly notices you after a moment. "Rashid left this for me to read before he gives it to anyone else," she says, touching the petition. "The new version names what the Academy must provide. It is better."

She moves her notebook and a spare chair out from the table with one hand. "There is room here."
