The next two days pass through lecture bells, library lamps, and the public paths of the Academy. The quadrangle changes character with the hour: crowded between classes, nearly bare at dusk except for the chapel steps and the slow traffic toward the bridges. Garvey House remains busy around meals, then falls into separate rooms and study tables.

After the second day's classes, the covered court door stands open.

A new notice is pinned beside it.

*PROVISIONAL SUPERVISED ACCESS: three evening sessions. Named Academy supervisor required. Equipment inspection before use. Independent practice remains suspended pending review.*

The notice gives no account of the injury.

Inside, the court's high roof holds the late light in long grey strips. Eight students stand spaced across the marked floor with padded staves and practice blades. An Academy instructor in a plain dark coat watches from the center line, correcting grips one at a time.

Rashid is among the group. He lowers his staff when the instructor calls for a reset, then steps back to give a smaller student room to try the sequence again. His folio rests against the wall beside a stack of unused ward bands.
