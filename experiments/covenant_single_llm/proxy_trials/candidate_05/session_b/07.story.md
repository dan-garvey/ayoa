Breakfast is quieter than dinner. Morning light fills the high windows, and the servers move along the table with covered dishes of eggs, dark bread, and stewed fruit.

Ysolde sits near the window with a plate of bread and pears before her. Her untouched tea sends up no steam. When you approach after serving yourself, she looks up from the garden paths below.

Your question holds her attention for a moment.

"Not soon," she says. "I don't take strangers into the air. It would be unsafe for both of us."

She takes a small piece of pear from her plate, then looks back through the window toward the bridges.
