The instructor ends the session at the next bell. Practice weapons go back into the rack one by one; the ward bands are counted before anyone is allowed to leave. Students drift out in small groups, talking over bruised knuckles and missed steps.

Rashid unfastens the wrap at one wrist and reaches for his folio when you approach. At your words, he glances toward the notice by the door.

"For three evenings," he says. "It's less than we had, but people can practice while they decide what to do with the report."

He closes the folio over the remaining copies of the request. "Thessaly caught the problem in the first wording. I might have handed them a petition they could approve without opening the court at all."

The instructor calls for Rashid from the equipment rack. He tucks the folio under his arm and heads across the marked floor.
