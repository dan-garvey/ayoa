Rashid steps aside as the door opens, then turns toward the stairwell without ceremony. His dark coat is cut sharply at the shoulders; fine fur darkens the backs of the hands he keeps loose at his sides.

"Dinner's formal enough without making the walk there formal too," he says as he leads downstairs. "You'll pick it up. Most of it is just knowing which fork people pretend matters."

The dining hall is warmer than the corridor, crowded with candlelight and the scent of peppered root broth. A long varnished table runs beneath paneled walls, set for far more people than have arrived. Seven students occupy the near half of it, their conversations thinning as Rashid brings you in.

"Rowan Garvey," Rashid says. He names the others around the table: Ashara vel Kothren, seated straight-backed beside a covered dish; Ysolde Thornmantle, white-haired and motionless near the window; Caelindra Vaeyn, silver hair gathered over one shoulder; Seraphel Dawnquill; Thessaly Morrow; and Aldric Verantus in white and gold.

Your greeting draws a few nods and brief returns. Seraphel's comes in a soft, measured couplet.

"May supper's warmth and candle's gleam
Make gentle welcome of the scene."

Ashara glances toward the remaining place setting. "Sit before the soup cools."

At the table's open place, a small card bears ROWAN GARVEY in dark green ink. It stands between Thessaly's black-eyed stillness and Ysolde's untouched water glass.
