Ysolde answers your promise with a single nod and returns to her breakfast.

By the time the scheduled classes release for the afternoon, fresh blue ink has gone up on the noticeboard outside the dueling ground: a ranked challenge at fourth bell, public observation permitted. The open octagon is already ringed by students when the bell sounds.

At its center, a narrow-shouldered angel in grey practice clothes faces a beastkin woman with tawny fur at her temples and a padded staff held low. A proctor checks their ward bands, then raises one hand.

"First clean scoring touch. Stop when I call it."

The angel moves first, a white flare skipping from her palm to the packed earth. Her opponent springs clear, closes the distance, and drives the staff toward the angel's ribs. The angel catches the shaft on a brief plane of light. Dust kicks up around their feet as they separate.

The second exchange is shorter. The beastkin fighter feints high, then cuts low. Light flashes at the wrong height. Her staff thumps against the angel's calf before the proctor's hand comes down.

"Touch. Challenge succeeds."

Applause breaks unevenly around the octagon. The defeated student bows, limping only slightly as the ward band cools at her wrist. A clerk pins a revised ranking strip beneath the notice while the small crowd begins to peel away toward the quadrangle.
