The paper stays folded beside Rashid's plate through the rest of the meal. Conversation turns to the courses, the coming term, and which lecturers have a habit of beginning on time. The servers clear the bowls and bring out small pears poached in spiced wine.

When the last plates are nearly empty, Ashara holds out a hand. Rashid gives her the request and a pen. She reads it without hurrying, then signs beneath the first line.

"Supervised access," she says, handing it back. "That is what this asks for. It doesn't take my court time."

"It doesn't," Rashid says.

Aldric leaves his name off the page. "Bring me the instructor's report, and I'll consider it."

"I'll ask for it tomorrow," Rashid replies.

Chairs begin to move back from the table. Aldric gathers his napkin with care and heads toward the chapel doors for dusk prayer. Caelindra collects a slim book she had set beside her chair and tells no one in particular that the library's west reading tables are less cold after the lamps have been lit. Ashara rolls a narrow practice schedule into her palm before leaving by the garden entrance.

Rashid tucks the signed request into a leather folio. "I'm copying this in the common room before people scatter. It'll be on the long table if anyone wants to see what they're signing."
