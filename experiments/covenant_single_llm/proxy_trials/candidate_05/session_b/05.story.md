Rashid stops his pen and turns the marked page so the sentence faces you. The ink is still wet where he has struck through a phrase.

"It says: 'We request that the Academy permit supervised evening practice at the covered court for students unable to secure private instruction.'"

Thessaly taps the word *supervised* with one narrow finger. "It does not say who must supervise. They may approve this and provide no one."

Rashid draws the fresh sheet closer. "I'm changing it to ask the Academy to designate a supervisor and restore access once one is named." He looks from the page to Thessaly. "If I only ask for permission, they can grant it and leave the court shut."

"That is more exact," Thessaly says.

Rashid writes the first line of the revision, then sets the pen beside the inkwell. The crossed-out page remains turned toward you.
