The common room has loosened after dinner. Firelight moves across the old Garvey portraits and the high leather chair backs; a pair of students at the far study table argue quietly over a map. Steam curls from the tea in your cup, sharp with mint.

Rashid takes the long table beneath the stairs. He opens his folio, weights the signed request with an inkwell, and begins copying it in a firm, even hand. He makes three copies before Thessaly comes in from the dining hall carrying a small notebook.

She reads the first page standing up. "Who is the supervisor?"

"That depends on who the Academy will approve," Rashid says.

"Then it does not yet request supervised access. It requests permission to ask for a supervisor."

Rashid looks down at the line she indicates. "Fair. I'll change it."

Thessaly closes the notebook. "I will read the changed version."

Two students from the next table pause by the fireplace, glance over the request, and carry on toward the gardens without approaching. Rashid crosses out the sentence, draws a fresh sheet from his folio, and starts again. The unsigned copies gather at his elbow while the fire settles into a lower crackle.
