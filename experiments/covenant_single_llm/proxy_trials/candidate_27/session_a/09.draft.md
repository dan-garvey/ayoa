Aldric nods once. "That is enough for tonight."

Ashara reaches for the water. "Ask Facilities, not only the instructor."

Rashid folds the paper beneath his hand and the servers clear the next course. The talk turns to schedules, a late seminar room, and the way rain has driven everyone indoors. When there is a pause, you glance toward the storm-lit windows and turn to Ysolde.

"Have you ever flown through weather like this?"

Ysolde's grey eyes remain on the white pulse beyond the glass. "Yes. Not from the Nexus. The plateau's storm is deeper than it looks from here."

She shifts in her chair, looking outward rather than at the table. "Over the northern sea, a storm has one wind at a time. You climb above it, or you go through it. Here, the wind comes up from below, turns under the stone, and takes your wing from the wrong side. Lightning follows the wet air, not the bright air. You watch the rain on the scales of your shoulder and turn before it reaches the wing joint."

Her fingers rest against the stem of her glass. "I flew into one like that when I was sixty-one. The gust folded my left wing before I saw it. I tore the outer membrane and landed in a quarry pond."

After a moment, she looks at you. "Have you ever been above a storm?"
