Rashid comes in after your invitation, keeping clear of the desk and bed. His dinner coat is dark and sharply fitted; fine fur darkens the backs of his hands. He returns your introduction with a brief clasp.

"Thank you," he says. "The bowls aren't out yet. We haven't missed anything."

He takes the narrow stair at an easy pace. Firelight reaches up from the common room below, catching on the gilt frames of dead Garveys above the hearth. Study tables stand between leather chairs, and the library door across the hall breathes lamp-oil air into the passage. As Rashid turns toward the dining room, a woman's voice carries through the open door.

"Rashid, it is one hour. The booking is mine."

The dining hall is bright with candles and working lamps. Six students occupy the places nearest the center of the long varnished table. A server in Academy livery waits beside the setting marked for you, while a second empty chair faces a tall horned woman with one hand on a folded sheet of paper. The golden-haired man opposite her has paused with his spoon halfway to the bowl. White hair, silver hair, tucked white wings, and blue-black hair frame the rest of the table.

Rashid says your name. The horned woman folds the paper once and looks up. "Ashara vel Kothren. Your seat is there."

The white-haired woman gives a small nod. "Ysolde Thornmantle."

"Caelindra Vaeyn," says the woman with silver hair. "I was informed Garvey House would have a human resident again."

The angel beside her smiles. "Seraphel Dawnquill.
Welcome to this table,
Rowan Garvey."

"Thessaly Morrow," says the small woman with black eyes. "I checked. No one has claimed your chair."

The man in white and gold acknowledges you from his seat. "Aldric Verantus. Welcome to the Conclave."

The server draws out the offered chair between Ysolde and Seraphel, and you take it as clear broth scented with herbs arrives from the kitchen. Rashid settles into the chair across from Ashara and opens the folded sheet beside his place.

"One of the evening group was injured during practice," he says. "The Academy closed the covered court. I'm asking for supervised access while they sort out what happened."

Aldric lowers his spoon. "The announcement said the instructor's report is pending. We don't know whether the court caused the injury."

"I'm not saying it did. I'm saying a supervisor could be there while the report is pending."

Ashara takes up the earlier thread. "And I said no to using my hour. I have a ranked bout tomorrow."

"I asked whether you would take one session."

"You did. My answer remains no."

Ysolde studies the petition. "Has anyone inspected the floor?"

"Not as far as the group knows," Rashid says. "I've spoken to everyone who was there."

"Then have it inspected," Ashara says. "The people in his group don't have private instruction to fall back on."

Rashid taps the paper once. "The request is for a supervisor. It doesn't claim the court is safe."

"I'll read the report before I put my name to it," Aldric says.

Rashid leaves the petition beside the salt cellar. Beyond the high windows, slow lightning passes through the storm without a sound.
