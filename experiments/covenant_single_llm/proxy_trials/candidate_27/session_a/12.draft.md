"Not this one. I'd like to try." You set the primer aside and move to the table opposite Thessaly. "Show me how it works."

Thessaly draws the board closer between you. "White goes first. That is you."

She arranges the black stones in a small pile by her hand and the white ones by yours. The board has two pale notches at opposite edges and two dark ones across from each other.

"One stone each turn, on any empty crossing. Once a stone is down, it stays. White wins by making an unbroken line from pale notch to pale notch. Black does the same with the dark notches."

She lays two white stones on neighboring crossings and taps the carved line between them. "Corners do not join. This does."

Her fingertip follows the carved path between the two white stones. Then she clears the demonstration pieces and places a single white stone beside the board.

"Anywhere except a notch. Start your path."
