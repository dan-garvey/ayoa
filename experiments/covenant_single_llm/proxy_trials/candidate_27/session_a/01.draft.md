Rashid steps in on your invitation without straying farther into the room than he needs to. His dark dinner coat is cut with almost military precision; fine fur darkens the backs of his hands where they rest at his sides. He meets your introduction with a brief, firm clasp.

"A pleasure, Rowan. And thank you." His amber eyes flick to the closed primer, then back to you. "We're still ahead of the soup. That is the best possible time to arrive."

He leads the way down the narrow stair. At the first landing, the common room opens below in firelight: leather chairs pulled toward the oversized hearth, study tables under portraits of dead Garveys, doors standing open onto the dining hall and library. From the latter comes lamp-oil air. Rashid turns toward the dining room as a woman's voice carries out ahead of him.

"I said supervised access," says Ashara, "not that you may have my booking tomorrow."

The formal dining hall is bright with candles and working lamps. Six places nearest the center of the long varnished table are occupied, while a server in Academy livery waits beside the place marked for you. A tall demon woman with swept-back black-red horns has one hand on a folded sheet of paper. Across from her, the golden-haired man in white and gold has set down his spoon. White hair, silver hair, small folded wings, and blue-black hair mark the others along the table in a scatter of candlelight.

Rashid gives your name to the room. Ashara looks up first, her gold eyes steady. "Ashara vel Kothren. Sit before he starts handing out responsibilities."

"I haven't handed anyone anything," Rashid says.

"Yet."

The white-haired woman inclines her head. "Ysolde Thornmantle."

"Caelindra Vaeyn," says the silver-haired woman. One pointed ear shifts beneath the fall of her hair. "The notice said a human would take the Garvey place."

The angel beside her smiles, wings tucked close against her white-and-gold sleeves. "Seraphel Dawnquill.\nWelcome, Rowan Garvey."

"Thessaly Morrow," says the small woman with entirely black eyes. "Your chair has been waiting exactly as long as ours."

The man in white and gold rises just enough to acknowledge you. "Aldric Verantus. Welcome to the Conclave."

The server draws out the offered chair between Ysolde and Seraphel, and you take it as bowls of clear, herb-scented broth arrive from the kitchen. Rashid settles into the remaining empty chair across from Ashara.

Rashid lays the folded sheet beside his place. "I'm collecting signatures for supervised access to the covered court. The evening group was barred after someone was injured."

"Until the instructor's report is available," Aldric says. "That is a precaution, not a punishment."

"No one has said when it will be available," Rashid replies. "The students who were there have given their accounts. We still don't know whether it was the surface, the equipment, or a mistake in the drill."

Ysolde looks at the paper. "Has the court itself been inspected?"

"Not that anyone has told us."

Ashara breaks a piece of bread between her fingers. "Then inspect it. A temporary closure does not require taking every practice hour from people who cannot buy their own instruction."

"Your practice hour remains yours," Rashid says.

"It does. I have a ranked bout tomorrow."

"And I did not ask you to surrender it. I asked whether you would supervise one session."

"Which is still my time."

Aldric reaches for his spoon. "Read the report first. Then make the request with facts rather than guesses."

Rashid leaves the petition beside the salt cellar. Outside the high windows, slow lightning moves through the storm without a sound.
