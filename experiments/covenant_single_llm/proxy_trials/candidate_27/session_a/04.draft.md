You go to bed with the primer closed on the desk and wake to stormlight washed thin across the window. After washing and dressing, you eat breakfast in the dining hall while the lamps are still lit against the grey morning. By the time you leave Garvey House, the quadrangle's black stone is slick with mist and the bridges hang white beyond the low walls.

The dueling ground waits at its far side: an open octagon of packed earth, a rope stretched between iron posts, and a handful of students gathered along the rails. You take the north position behind the rope. From there, the eastern glare does not reach the ground.

At ten bells, an Academy referee in plain grey steps into the octagon with Ashara and her opponent, Mera Voss. Mera is broad through the shoulders and carries a long ash staff; Ashara has a blunted saber in one hand, its guard wrapped in dark leather. Her black hair is tied higher than it was at dinner, exposing the sweep of her horns. Neither speaks after the referee's brief instructions.

The first exchange belongs to Mera. The staff snaps toward Ashara's knee, changes line, and taps the padded strip at her left ribs before Ashara can close the distance. The referee's hand cuts down. Mera backs away at once, staff lowered.

Ashara does not rush the second pass. She lets Mera's staff travel, turns just enough for its end to pass her shoulder, and closes under the next swing. Heat shimmers along the saber's guard when their weapons meet. Mera releases contact too late; Ashara's blunt point rests against the white mark at Mera's collarbone. The referee signals the score.

The third exchange lasts longer. Mera keeps the staff low and makes Ashara circle, testing the rope line without stepping over it. Ashara's tail brushes the packed earth once as she shifts her weight. When Mera commits to a high strike, Ashara catches the shaft near its middle, takes the blow on the reinforced guard, and turns it aside. Her saber touches Mera's side beneath the arm before the staff can come back around.

The referee calls the bout for Ashara, two marks to one. Mera salutes with the staff; Ashara returns it with her saber, then accepts a towel from an attendant at the edge of the ground. Applause rises from the south rail and runs around the rope. You remain quiet at the north rail as the referee records the result on a slate.
