Rashid gathers the loose edge of the petition clear of the salt cellar as you leave it unsigned. "Fair enough," he says. "I don't want anyone signing blind."

Ashara takes a spoonful of broth. "You'll ask again once you have the report."

"Once I have the report."

Aldric reaches for the bread basket. "Bring it with the request."

"If the instructor releases it to me, I will."

"You will still need someone else for the supervised session," Ashara says.

"I know."

The servers complete their circuit around the table, setting down clear broth with small green leaves floating on its surface. Caelindra accepts pepper from the nearer server with a quiet word; Thessaly tears her bread into even pieces and lines them beside her bowl. Seraphel folds her hands for a moment, her wings giving a faint rustle against her sleeves. The talk loosens into the scrape of spoons and the low exchange of names around the table.

When there is a pause, you turn to Ashara. "Is tomorrow's bout open to spectators? I'd like to watch."

Ashara sets down her spoon. "The ground is open. Ten bells, at the octagon. Stay behind the rope, and keep quiet if the referee calls a hold."

Rashid reaches for his own bread. "The north side has the clearer view in the morning."

"It does," Ysolde says.

Ashara glances toward the windows, where the stormlight whitens the glass. "Then stand there."
