"No. The highest I've been is a warehouse roof, and I was looking down rather than up." You set your spoon down. "What happened after you landed in the quarry pond?"

Ysolde looks back to the window. "The pond was shallower than it appeared. I struck a shelf of slate and could not lift the left wing."

She takes a sip of water. "The quarry manager came out in a narrow boat. She asked whether I could speak. I said yes. She told me not to make another wave, because the boat was new."

For the first time, the corner of Ysolde's mouth moves. "She sent for a healer. The membrane needed stitching, so I had to remain in human form for five weeks. My mother arrived on the third day. She waited until the healer had gone before asking why I had crossed the storm front alone."

Ysolde turns the glass once between her fingers. "The quarry manager sent me a bill for the broken slate. My mother paid it."
