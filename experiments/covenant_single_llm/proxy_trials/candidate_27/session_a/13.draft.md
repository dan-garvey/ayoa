"Before I place one: what happens if we've both used all seven stones and neither of us has connected our notches?"

Thessaly rests one black stone against her palm. "Then it is a draw. We clear the board. Stones do not move once placed."

You place your first white stone beside one of the pale notches. Thessaly answers from the lower dark edge. The next few turns make the board begin to divide: your white stones reach toward the middle on one route while hers gather along the crossing that would join her dark notches.

When a black line begins to form across the board, you put a white stone on its open junction. Thessaly shifts to a second route. Her next black stone cuts across one of the white paths you have been building, and you turn toward the other pale notch instead.

By the sixth pair of stones, the board is crowded with short chains and blocked crossings. Your seventh white stone closes the last route that would give Thessaly a clear line. She studies the board, then sets down her final black stone where it stops your remaining white chain without reaching her own dark notch.

All fourteen stones remain on the board. Neither color joins its notches.

Thessaly touches one black stone with her fingertip. "Draw."

She begins gathering the pieces into their separate piles. "Would you like another?"
