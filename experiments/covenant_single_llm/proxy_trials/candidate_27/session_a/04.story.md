You go to bed with the primer closed on the desk and wake to stormlight thinning across the window. After washing and dressing, you eat breakfast in the dining hall while the lamps still burn against the grey morning. The quadrangle's black stone is wet with mist by the time you leave Garvey House, and the bridges beyond the walls hang white in the storm.

The dueling ground occupies the far side of the quadrangle: packed earth within an open octagon, a rope between iron posts, and a small crowd already gathered at the rails. You take a place at the north side behind the rope, clear of the eastern glare.

At ten bells, a referee in plain grey enters with Ashara and her opponent, Mera Voss. Mera carries a long ash staff and wears padded black at the ribs and throat. Ashara has a blunted saber in one hand, its guard bound in dark leather; her black hair is tied high, leaving the sweep of her horns bare in the damp air.

The referee looks from one fighter to the other. "Two clean marks. Break on my call. Ready."

The first bell rings.

Mera takes the first mark. Her staff comes low toward Ashara's knee, changes line, and touches the padded strip at Ashara's left ribs before Ashara can close. The referee's hand drops.

"Mark, Voss."

Mera steps back with the staff lowered. Ashara waits through the next bell rather than meeting the first swing. When Mera sends the staff across her shoulder line, Ashara lets its end pass, closes under the return, and catches the shaft on her guard. Heat wavers along the leather binding. Mera breaks contact a moment too late; Ashara's blunt point rests against the white patch at Mera's collarbone.

"Mark, Kothren."

The third exchange keeps them moving around the octagon. Mera holds the staff low, drawing Ashara nearer to the rope and changing direction whenever Ashara tests the space inside her reach. Ashara's tail brushes the packed earth as she shifts her weight. When Mera finally commits to a high strike, Ashara takes the blow on the reinforced guard, turns the shaft aside, and puts the saber to Mera's side beneath the arm.

"Mark, Kothren. Bout."

Mera salutes with the staff. Ashara returns it with her saber, then takes a towel from an attendant at the edge of the ground. Applause rises from the south rail and carries around the rope. You remain quiet at the north rail while the referee writes Ashara's two-to-one result on a slate.
