Ashara gives a brief nod toward the rack. "Keep the blade dry."

You return it and make your way back through the rain to Garvey House. After washing and changing out of the damp clothes, you take the signet from the desk drawer and put it back on. The common room is quiet through the afternoon, its fire low and its study tables empty. You read the primer there until the lamps come on: the Academy calendar, the public descriptions of each track, the rules for requesting passage across the bridges.

At dinner, you take your place with the others and eat without beginning a conversation. Rashid arrives after the first bowls are set down, a folded paper tucked beneath one arm. He waits until a server has passed, then says, "I spoke to the instructor."

Ysolde looks up from her bowl. "What was broken?"

"A joining plate beneath one of the court mats. It cracked under a turn. Facilities have taken the mat up."

Aldric sets down his spoon. "Then closing the court was sensible."

"For the first hours," Rashid says. "The instructor says they will replace the plate at dawn, inspect it afterward, and reopen for supervised groups once that is done. No one told the group any of that."

Ashara breaks bread between her fingers. "They should have."

"I asked for the reopening time in writing," Rashid says. "I am not collecting signatures tonight."

Thessaly turns her black eyes toward the folded paper. "What time did they give you?"

"They did not. They gave me 'after inspection.'"

"Then you do not have a time."

Rashid's mouth tightens. "No."

The storm taps at the windows while the servers bring the next course. The petition remains folded beneath Rashid's hand.
