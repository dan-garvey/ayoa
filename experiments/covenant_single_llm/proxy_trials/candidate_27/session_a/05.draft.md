You applaud once the referee has finished with the slate. Mera has already gone toward the equipment shed with her staff over one shoulder when Ashara passes through the opening in the rope, towel draped around her neck. Her skin still holds a hard ember warmth from the bout.

When you approach, she stops beside the north rail and listens to your congratulations and request without looking away.

"She wanted me to keep closing on the same line," Ashara says. "I gave her the first mark because I did."

She considers you for a moment, then nods toward the rack of practice weapons beside the octagon. "Yes. After lunch, south end of the ground. Half an hour. Wear boots you can turn in, leave the signet in your room, and take a wooden blade from that rack. I will show you the grip and the first guard."

The referee calls for the next pair from inside the octagon. Ashara pulls the towel from her shoulders. "Do not be late."
