You lower the blade and offer a hopeful grin. "Could you spare ten more minutes? It's just beginning to make sense."

Ashara glances at the storm beyond the rail, then lifts her wooden sword again. "Ten. We do not add a strike. You still lose the point when you move."

She sets the guard between you. "Step forward. Stop. Step back. Stop."

For the extra minutes, she makes the work narrower still. Each time she shifts to either side, you move your feet and keep the wooden point between you. When it drifts toward her shoulder, she taps it aside and has you reset. When it stays in line, she moves again without comment.

Rain begins to tick against the far rope. At the end of the tenth minute, Ashara lowers her blade. "That is enough. You found the guard twice without looking for it. Rack the sword before it swells in the rain."
