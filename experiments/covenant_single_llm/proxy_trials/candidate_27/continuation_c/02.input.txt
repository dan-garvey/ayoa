"The reading is an unfinished scene about a ferryman and a stranded judge." That is the only content of the scene I describe. I leave its pages folded inside my coat and don't read from them. Privately, I wish Caelindra would offer to read the judge's part with me; I don't say this. I also think of a childhood friend named Merrin Vale, whose name and existence I have never mentioned here. I finish dinner quietly.
