A server clears a bowl from the table. Caelindra looks at you across the candles.

“An unfinished scene, then. Saturday at four in the east gardens still works for me.”

Seraphel inclines her head.

“Bring it unfinished.
I will listen.”

The rest of dinner passes with the scrape of cutlery, the quiet movement of servants, and thunderless lightning below the windows. You finish quietly.
