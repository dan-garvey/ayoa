Candlelight lies in long strips along the varnished table, catching on the narrow silver clasp at Caelindra’s throat. At your question, she lifts her eyes from the place card before her.

“The common room, after dinner,” she says. “Friday evening. You invited me yesterday.” Her gaze holds on you a beat. “Why are you asking?”

Seraphel’s small wings rustle against the back of her chair. She speaks without raising her voice:

“Rashid told me
the common room is closed for repairs.
He said you were seeking another reservation.
No time or place was named.”

Caelindra turns toward her, then back to you. Her fingers settle flat beside her untouched glass. “Then I have been given an invitation to something that no longer has a location.”
