Steam from the serving dishes softens the candlelight between you. Caelindra lays down her fork.

“A ferryman and a stranded judge,” she says. “I’ll hear it in the east gardens on Saturday.”

Seraphel gives a small nod.

“Bring the pages
as they are.
I will listen.”

Caelindra returns to her plate. Seraphel watches slow lightning move below the dining-hall windows, and the meal passes with the scrape of cutlery and the soft work of the servers. By the time the last dishes are cleared, you have finished dinner.
