Steam curls from a covered serving dish at the far end of the table. Caelindra pauses with one finger resting on the stem of her glass.

“The common room, after dinner. Tonight,” she says. “That was the invitation.”

Seraphel turns from the candlelight, her small wings close against her back.

“At noon Rashid told me
the common room had closed for repairs.
He said you were seeking another reservation.
He knew no place or hour.”

Caelindra’s violet eyes return to you. “No one has told me of a replacement. Where is it now?”
