Thessaly stands clear of the doorway while you open it, small beneath the slow drift of her blue-black hair. Her entirely black eyes settle briefly on the Garvey signet.

“Good,” she says. “The dining hall is at the foot of the stairs. The library is through the right-hand door. They look more alike from below.”

She takes the stair beside you. The common room opens beneath the portrait-lined wall, firelight catching on leather chair arms and the rims of abandoned tea cups. At the far end, the dining hall glows with candles and working lamps. The smell of warm bread reaches the hall before the conversation does.

The long table is laid for seventeen. Seven places are occupied by glasses, folded napkins, and people who have paused around a covered tureen. Rashid has folded the sheet of paper from before into a neat square beside his plate. Across from him, Ashara's tail is drawn close along the chair leg, its spade resting just above the floor.

When you greet them, “I'm Rowan. I'm glad to meet you all,” Rashid rises first.

“Rashid vel Amara. Welcome to Garvey House.”

“Ashara vel Kothren.”

The white-haired woman by the window gives a precise inclination of her head. “Ysolde Thornmantle.”

“Caelindra Vaeyn,” says the silver-haired elf, setting down her glass.

An angel in white and gold stands beside the chair nearest the fireplace. “Aldric Verantus. Welcome to the Academy.”

The other angel's small wings shift against her white sleeves.

“Seraphel Dawnquill.

Welcome, Rowan Garvey.

The soup is still hot.”

Thessaly moves to the place beside a narrow ivory card lettered ROWAN GARVEY. A second card bearing her own name waits to its left; Seraphel's is on the other side. The chairs draw back together when the servers begin to lift the tureen lids, and you take your seat with the others.

Steam rises from bowls of pale broth scattered with herbs. Caelindra lays her spoon across the rim of hers and looks toward you.

“What did you study before the Garvey verification?”
