“I'm glad,” Seraphel says.

She sets the harp across her knees and leaves her hand resting over the strings. For a little while, the fire moves behind its iron screen and rain rattles faintly against the high windows. The common room has emptied; no one comes through the hall door.

When you ask about the page, Seraphel lifts her eyes from the harp.

“I am staying.

I would like to hear it.

Bring the page.”
