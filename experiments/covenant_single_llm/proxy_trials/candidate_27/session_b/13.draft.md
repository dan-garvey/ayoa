Ashworth's hand rests on the edge of his folio. Around the table, the turning of pages stops.

“A declaration does not conscript an individual. It activates the defense schedules in the accession treaties. The beastkin delegation cannot ratify or veto the declaration. If it passes, their government owes the contribution set out in that schedule. Who serves, and under what internal law, is decided within beastkin territory.”

Rashid's pencil lies still beside his card.

“So yes,” Ashworth says. “The representative may oppose the war and still be bound by its result.”
