Thessaly sets the curved tile flat again instead of putting it away. Her fingertip follows the amber lantern's last route.

“One,” she says, touching the first mark at the bend. “Two.” Her finger stops at the next mark, then rests on the painted moon. “The gate is the end of the road. It is not a third space.”

She glances toward the west side of the board. “Your last blue lantern would have reached its gate on the next turn. I put the fork down because of that.”

Then she slides the curved tile into its fitted row and sorts the remaining roads into the box. The lacquered lid closes over the last of them.
