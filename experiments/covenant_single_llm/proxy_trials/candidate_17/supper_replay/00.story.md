The water in the two cups is cold enough to bead against the clay. By the time you return to the hearth, Ashara has uncovered the pot. Lamb, lentils, and strips of red pepper crowd the dark broth; the first cautious spoonful is rich with pepper and lamb.

Ashara tears flatbread over her bowl. “On points. My opponent was better at the boundary than I expected and tried twice to drive me there; the second time, I let them think they had the angle and used their momentum to put them on the ground. What did Mara fuss over?”
