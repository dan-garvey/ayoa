"Tomorrow after dinner," Thessaly says. She draws the game box toward herself, then returns your good night with a small lift of her hand.

The common room has gone quiet by the time you cross it. The fire has settled to red beneath its ash, and the portraits above it are only darker shapes in the low light. Upstairs, the framed letter still catches the edge of the lamp when you return to your room.

For half an hour, the primer gives the Academy back in its official language: bridge permissions, dining customs, the names of the five schools, the public history of the Covenant and its long peace. Article Nineteen appears again in narrow, exact print among the conduct rules. The comparison to Nessa Vale remains unspoken, with everything else that belongs to the life before Garvey House.

When the lamp has burned low, the primer closes on the desk. Rain works steadily at the window while the room goes dark around you, and sleep takes the rest of the night.
