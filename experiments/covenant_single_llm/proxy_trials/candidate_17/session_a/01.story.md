Rashid steps back from the threshold without looking past you into the room. His dinner jacket is dark blue and cut with almost severe plainness; fine dark fur shows at the backs of his hands where the cuffs end. He falls into step beside you down the broad stair, past a Garvey portrait whose gilt frame has been polished more recently than anything else in the common room.

"No," he says. "If you avoid a chair, someone will make it into an occasion. Sit where you can hear people; I will take the place by the draft before Aldric makes it a principle."

The dining hall has room enough for a council, not merely the first-years: a varnished table set under candles and working lamps, Academy servers aligning glasses with quiet concentration. Ashara sits nearest the door, black-red horns bright against the paneled wall, one hand around a cup. Caelindra studies an unfolded napkin with the grave attention of someone finding fault in it. At the window end, Ysolde's white hair nearly disappears into the stormlight, while Seraphel leans toward Thessaly over a conversation too low to reach the doorway.

Rashid heads for the chair nearest the open transom, where cool air slips into the warm room. Aldric is standing at the middle of the table with a small stack of place cards. In white and gold, he lifts the card beside his own setting and leaves the chair clear.

"I put you here, Garvey," he says. "You should not have to lean across half the table while everyone asks after your family. Does that suit you?"
