At the sound of your name, the table gives its attention back in different measures. Rashid's brief laugh at the lawyers draws a faint change at the corner of Seraphel's mouth. Ysolde inclines her head once from the window end; Caelindra sets down her napkin and looks at you directly for the first time. Aldric gathers the unused place cards into a tidy stack before the nearest server can reach for them.

Dinner arrives without further ceremony: bowls of herb-studded rice, charred squash glazed with honey and pepper, dark bread still warm from the kitchen, and a broad dish of stew fragrant with wine and rosemary. The heat from Ashara's side of the table makes the candle nearest her burn with a quick, unsteady flame.

"I train; the rest argue about it afterward," Ashara says.

Thessaly opens a small lacquered box beside her glass. Inside, polished counters in green, ivory, and black sit in fitted rows. "After dinner I am teaching Nine Lanterns; the rules are clear, and the loser only puts the counters back in their box. You may join us, if you wish."
