Thessaly follows the cramped writing for another few lines. Then she reaches for a narrow library catalog card and lays it over the next note in the margin, leaving the printed paragraph uncovered.

"So am I. Let us try your method once: I will cover the correction, and you tell me which claim earned it."

She turns the book a little farther toward you. The next paragraph begins with the author praising an eastern road as the gentlest route into the hills.
