"Ah. That is better," Thessaly says. "The complaint should speak first."

She slides the catalog card across the printed paragraph and leaves the next narrow column of ink uncovered. The note begins in a harsher hand than the last one:

*Eastern road? Tell that to someone who has climbed it in rain. Your gentle route breaks axles at the third turn, and the northern descent reaches the post-house before dark.*

Thessaly holds the page flat with two fingers and waits.
