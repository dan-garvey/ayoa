Morning begins above the storm with breakfast in the dining hall and orientation in the Conclave seminar room. Chancellor Ashworth speaks beneath the framed Accords in all seven scripts, covering bridge permissions, faculty access, and the habit of treating public disagreement as something that must survive a written record. The first day then breaks into rooms and names: Professor Windwhisper's careful reading of treaty language, Professor Kira's exercises in making a request without surrendering its purpose, and the ordinary confusion of finding the right corridor before a bell.

The free hours give the Academy its shape. In the quadrangle, black demon stone meets white angel marble and living elven timber without any of them quite matching. From a public terrace, the storm below turns slowly under the plateau; by the gardens, pale paths hold the day's last warmth between high walls. Lamp-globes brighten one by one as you make your way back to Garvey House.

At the evening meal, Rashid has a folded copy of his request beside his plate. "The Academy clerk accepted it for a meeting tomorrow after lessons. Fourteen people who use the covered court have signed."

"Fourteen names make a hearing," Ashara says. "They do not make a cause."

Thessaly has been quiet through the exchange. She breaks a piece of bread and looks across the table. "I left the table beneath the stair clear. For the travelogue."
