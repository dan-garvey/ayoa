The green counters return to their fitted grooves one by one, then the black ones, then the small score-pins. Thessaly watches without taking the work from you. When the box is full, she closes its lid with both hands and leaves it on the table rather than carrying it away.

For a few minutes the library settles around the two of you. Rain gives a soft, regular sound against the high windows. Somewhere above, the iron stair gives a brief tick as it cools. Thessaly turns the empty board so that its inlaid lanterns catch the lamplight.

"I enjoyed it too. You paid attention."

She looks from the board to you. "How did the lawyers find you?"
