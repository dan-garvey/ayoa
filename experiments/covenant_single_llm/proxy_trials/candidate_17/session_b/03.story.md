Across the table, a woman with honey-blonde hair and small white wings visible above the back of her chair turns from the conversation at her side. Her smile reaches her eyes before she speaks.

“Then I will spare you more notices.

A server lifts the lid from the nearest tureen, and steam carries the clean sweetness of pear and thyme across the table.
