Ysolde considers the bridge for another moment. The stormlight traces a thin pale line along the side of her face.

“I am walking from the quadrangle to the north terrace after afternoon seminar, if the wind settles. I am not crossing a bridge tomorrow; this one may close, and I have no permission to visit its far end. You may come, but I will stop to look at things.”

She picks up her book again, holding it closed rather than opening it. Outside, the nearest span fades in and out behind the garden trees as the clouds shift across its light.
