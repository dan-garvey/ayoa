Ysolde gives a small nod.

“Then we have a plan. If the wind is not quiet, I will not be in the quadrangle.”

Thessaly fits the lid firmly over Nine Terms. “Good night, Rowan. The box will still be here tomorrow.”

You remain by the fire until the logs settle inward and the stormlight thins behind the windows. When you wish them both good night and go upstairs, the common room keeps its low warmth behind you.

In your room, the coat hangs from its hook beside the door. The blue primer is waiting on the bed where the lawyers’ papers left it. Its first page bears the Academy seal above a map of the Nexus: the plateau at its center, the gardens and quadrangle marked in fine lines, and six hard-light bridges reaching into the surrounding territories.

The opening pages give the public account in crisp, impersonal paragraphs. The Covenant followed forty years of war; the Council has a seat for each signatory race; the Academy exists so future leaders learn one another’s laws and customs before they inherit them. A sidebar explains that the human Council seat has stood empty since the plague, while the Conclave’s first-years are expected to dine together at Garvey House and attend their opening seminar in the morning.

The next section begins with a red-ink heading: **Article Nineteen: Prohibition of Cross-Signatory Issue.** Its legal language is exact about what it forbids and the penalties attached to it. Nothing in the section addresses friendship, courtship, or the ordinary work of living beside people from other races; it simply moves on to rules for formal sponsorships, travel permissions, and titles of address.

The page after that opens on a diagram of the Academy’s five tracks, with **Conclave** printed at the top in gold. The rest of the primer waits beneath your hand.
