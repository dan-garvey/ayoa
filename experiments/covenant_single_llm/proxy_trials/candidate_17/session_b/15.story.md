By half past seven, the dining hall has traded candlelight for the grey-gold of morning. Steam rises from porridge bowls and a covered dish of fried eggs; the long table feels less ceremonial with only a handful of places occupied.

Rashid sits halfway down it in a dark morning coat, one hand around a cup of tea and the other holding a folded Academy map. Seraphel is across from him, white wings tucked close enough that their edges barely show above her chair. They both look up at your greeting.

“Good morning, Rowan.
There is toast enough
for another person.”

The open chair beside Rashid has a clear view of the breakfast dishes and the common-room arch. He folds the map once more and sets it beside his plate.

“Morning, Garvey. A few of us are leaving at eight-forty; the colonnade is simple to find, but Academy diagrams are designed by people who have never carried a book in wind. Sit with us, if you want—there is room.”

Outside the high windows, the gardens lie pale with mist, and the nearest bridge is only a bright suggestion beyond them.
