The red ribbon remains caught at Article Nineteen, its loose end lying across the legal text. Beneath the primer, the papers on the desk include a folded Academy schedule with the first day’s entries set in a neat, severe hand.

**Breakfast — Garvey House Dining Hall — 07:00–08:45**

**First-Year Conclave Opening Seminar — Conclave Seminar Room — 09:00**

A smaller note beneath the seminar entry directs new students to cross the quadrangle and use the arched door beneath the white-marble colonnade. The map on the reverse marks it with a gold circle.

You set the primer aside, leave the schedule on top of the desk papers, and put out the lamp. The room settles around you in darkness: the frame and copied card on the desk, the faint stormlight beyond the garden window, and the old house quieting one floor at a time.
