I read it twice. The argument over my name catches a small laugh out of me; after that I have to sit quietly for a little while.

"They meant to bring me here. I'm glad I know that now."

I look up at Stone.

"Could I copy this before I go? I'd like to have their words in my room. And if you'd welcome another visit, I'd like to hear about them sometime when you haven't a desk full of work."
