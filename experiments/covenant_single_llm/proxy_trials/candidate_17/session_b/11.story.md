“You did not interfere with the rules. That was enough.”

Ysolde sets her book face down on her knee and looks from the window to you. A line of white lightning moves through the clouds beyond the gardens, too far away to bring any sound with it.

“I do not study it as a subject. I have watched it from height long enough to know its habits, and I used to fly the southern route with my mother. The bridges hold; they turn colder before bad weather, so use the rail the first few times and look out rather than down.”

Another faint tremor passes through the windowpanes. Ysolde lifts her gaze toward the violet-dark bridge beyond the garden wall, as if measuring the distance by habit.
