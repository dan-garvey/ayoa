The paper makes a dry little sound when you lower it. Seraphel has set the harp on its blue cloth beside the hearthrug, though one hand still rests across the strings. She waits long enough for a coal to settle behind the iron screen.

“Nell speaks
as though ten o'clock
might save them.

Ada has been keeping
the other clock.

I like that.”

Her thumb draws once across a single string—not a tune, only a quiet note that disappears into the room. Her eyes move from the page to you.

“‘In what?’ is hard.

It is also the first time
she says she might go.

Or I think it is.

Does Ada have another coat?”
