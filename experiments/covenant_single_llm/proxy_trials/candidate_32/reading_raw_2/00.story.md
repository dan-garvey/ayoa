The last line sits between the fire and the high windows. A log shifts behind the iron screen, sending a brief red glow across the lower edge of the paper on your knee. Beyond the common room's open arch, porcelain touches porcelain as the dining hall is cleared.

Seraphel does not answer at once. Her thumb rests lightly against the lowest harp string. She has watched the page while you read, rather than the different voices you give the sisters.

“That last question stays in the room.

Not because it is clever.

Because neither of them
has anything suitable.”

She looks up at you then, her expression thoughtful.

“Ada makes it hurt
when she says
she answered the door.

Nell is not foolish.

She is trying to get to Friday.

Both of them are right
for one more hour.”

Her fingers close gently over the harp's strings, stopping a faint sympathetic hum before it can gather into a note.

“Twelve lines is a scene.

Would Ada go with her?”
