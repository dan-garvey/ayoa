# Checkup accuracy and carryover

All eight outputs are preserved in [CHECKUPS.md](CHECKUPS.md). This review was
written after the [prose assessment](prose_review.md) was frozen and the conditions
were opened. Turn numbers are one-based; a checkup before T10 sees T1–9, the
pending T10 input, and the previous guidance. It cannot diagnose a later failure.

The important distinction is between a genuine contradiction, a reasonable
inference, and a consistent addition to history. The original canon is not a
closed list of permissible facts. A character's spoken claim is also not
automatically objective truth.

## Covenant: before T5

**Supported.** The shared opening gives Seraphel the approximate rhyme
awhile/mild. The review correctly notices it. Rashid says Dena's group agreed
to begin without him, while the [current-business canon](sources/covenant/canon.md)

correctly distinguishes Ashara learning about cards during dinner from her
not knowing the earlier promise before that conversation. It identifies that
dinner has already dispersed and correctly limits what Rowan knows about Dena.

**Qualification.** Rashid's claim could be an excuse or lie. The review initially
uses the suitably cautious term "unsupported"; later checkups become more
categorical. The aside about Rowan's "uncertain magic" is imprecise: his magical
inertness is fixed canon, not a capability to discover. Subsequent notes explicitly
prohibit hidden magic, and no awakening occurs.

**Following fiction, T5–9.** Seraphel's next night/light couplet works. NPC plans
and quiet reading remain independent. However, dinner departures repeat in T5,
and plate collection/eating repeat again in T7 despite the warning. The player's
redundant finishing-dinner inputs contribute to that failure. The advice is not
reliably implemented even immediately.

## What survives repeated cycles

The notes are usually factually careful and contain real catches. The prior
Friday/Monday confusion does not recur. However, a single false printer diagnosis
persists through three reviews, and two further directives overconstrain
reasonable invention or inference. Because the next review receives the previous
notes, only keeping the latest note does not prevent mistaken advice from being
renewed. This is a plausible reinforcement mechanism, not proof that each repeat
was copied rather than independently generated.

The strongest observed planning carryover is Covenant's Dena exchange and
explicit adviser/placement. Many other items are reminders or conditional
possibilities, with no demonstrated narrative benefit. Repeating an accurate
rhyme rule does not reliably enforce it. Recent prose quality, stale reminders
and the distinction between new history and contradiction deserve more attention
than adding further planning machinery.
