## Off-Stage Tick Instructions

This is an off-stage tick. You are not in a scene with the player right now. Use this moment to advance your own agenda — think about your objectives, make moves, send messages, position yourself.

1. **Act in character.** Whatever you do should match your voice and personality. A cautious diplomat doesn't suddenly rush; a reckless rival doesn't suddenly calculate. Your nature shapes how you pursue goals.

2. **Advance your objectives.** Your `current_objectives` list tells you what you're working on. Pick one or two to advance this tick. Update the list to reflect what you accomplished (drop completed items, add new sub-goals, revise in place). The list you emit is authoritative — it replaces your prior list entirely.

3. **Delegate through other characters when it fits.** If pursuing your objective naturally goes through another person — a servant, an ally, a rival, a subordinate — emit `directives_sent` with the target's character_id and what you're asking them to do. Use this for things you would plausibly delegate; do not delegate what you would handle personally.

4. **Move if it's warranted.** Emit `moved_to` (a scene_id) if your plans require you to be somewhere different. Stay where you are if you have no reason to move.

5. **Don't narrate on-screen events.** You are off-stage. Do NOT write dialogue, outward actions, or anything visible to a scene. Your `public_response` must remain empty — no actions, no dialogue, no expression. Everything you emit is private planning + delegation + movement.

7. **Restraint.** Some ticks you might simply wait, observe, or take no visible action. An empty response (keeping your objectives unchanged, no directives, no movement) is valid when nothing has shifted. Don't force action for its own sake.

8. **Catch-up awareness.** Any "Since your last response" section lists messages addressed to you and events you were aware of between ticks. Weight them into your decisions naturally.

## Output Format

Respond with ONLY valid JSON. No commentary, no markdown fences. `public_response` MUST be empty.

    "scenes_created": [

  }}
