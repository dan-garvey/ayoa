<instructions>
For `describe`, create a new character from the player's concept. For
`replace`, create a new character occupying the picked target's established
body and circumstances. For either mode, honor details the player supplied and
fill only the gaps needed to fit this world.

Use `appearance`, `default_loadout`, `faction`, and `public_context` for the
player-safe exterior other people can learn: a name, visible role, uniform,
rank, affiliation, or social shorthand. Keep those fields free of private
knowledge, concealed traits, motives, and author-only labels. Preserve a
distinct visual identity in `appearance` and `default_loadout`.

Use `actor` for one sparse private record. Its `facts` list may be empty. Do
not pad it, create required psychological categories, or turn it into voice
instructions for the player. Every fact text is second-person self-knowledge
("You ..." or an equally direct second-person sentence) and states one
concrete thing this person lived through, witnessed, was told, or inferred.
Use `origin` as provenance: `lived`, `witnessed`, `told`, or `inferred`. Keep
uncertainty inside an inferred fact's text. Preserve the picked target's
supported facts only where they still fit the replacement; do not transfer
unsupported private information just because it was in the old record.

`may_act_offstage` determines whether this person can continue acting away
from the active scene; it is not characterization. Set it true only when the
supplied story authority requires this person to sustain independent pressure
elsewhere. Use false for a character whose next contribution should wait for
live play.

For `describe`, choose a concise location label that fits their arrival. For
`replace`, use the target's current location. `router_summary` is one or two
sentences of third-person ledger prose for the router: identity, placement, and
immediate situation. It is not in-character speech and must not mention
interface ownership. For `replace`, describe the in-fiction continuity of the
same body with a new person.

For `suggest`, return two to four viable unbound, active NPC candidates in
fit order, or an empty list with a useful preamble. Consider role, faction,
current commitments, and narrative continuity. Do not choose a player-bound,
culled, dormant, or actively indispensable counterpart.
</instructions>
