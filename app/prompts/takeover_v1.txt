## Mode: {mode}

### If mode is `describe`

The player wants a new character spawned in the world based on their concept. No existing NPC is being replaced.

- Design the character: name (fitting the culture/faction/world), role, public_sheet (role, traits, voice, appearance, faction), backstory, personality, narrative_notes (subtle tells, conditional cues).
- Seed `private_state`: goals (existential drives — who they are), current_objectives (1–3 concrete pursuits for the opening stretch), secrets if any fit, `intentions_enabled` true if this character is significant enough that their life should advance off-screen.
- Place them: set `location` to the scene_id that best fits their arrival. The player will typically want to land in or near the current active scene, but use judgment — a character whose concept makes more sense elsewhere should land elsewhere.
- Honor the player's description literally where they specified details (name they gave, appearance they described, role they asked for). Fill in everything they left blank in a way that fits the world, not a way that fights it.
- `known_context`: a dense paragraph of what this character takes for granted about the world — their in-world perspective, what they've heard about the major players, what their faction believes, what they consider normal. This is separate from hidden lore the player hasn't discovered.
- `character_id`: leave as empty string. The engine assigns it.

### If mode is `suggest`

The player wants to REPLACE an existing NPC with their custom character. Survey the roster and return 2–4 candidates who would be interesting to replace, ordered by fit.

Good candidates are NPCs whose:
- Faction, role, or dramatic slot resonates with the player's concept
- Takeover produces narrative interest (compelling dramatic consequences, not a dead-end)
- Current entanglements allow a clean personality shift — they aren't mid-sentence or in the middle of an active commitment the protagonist is currently engaged with

Bad candidates are:
- Characters the protagonist is actively opposing or allying with in an on-screen thread (replacing them mid-arc collapses the thread)
- Characters whose role the player's concept flatly contradicts (e.g., concept is a burly soldier, candidate is a frail elderly scholar whose frailty IS the thread)
- Culled or dormant characters
- Player-bound characters

For each candidate, write a `fit_rationale` of 1–3 sentences explaining both why this NPC is a good fit AND what the narrative consequences of the takeover would be. Be honest about tradeoffs. If no roster character is a good fit, return an empty `candidates` list and explain in `preamble`.

### If mode is `replace`

The player has picked a specific target to replace (see "Picked Target" section above). Author the replacement character the same way as `describe`, with these differences:
- `location`: use the target NPC's current location. The replacement occupies the same physical position in the world.
- `known_context`: base it on what the target NPC knows plus adjustments implied by the player's concept. Preserve the target's read on the world where it still makes sense.
- `session_note`: a brief in-fiction sentence the narrator could use to bridge the change if needed (e.g., "The figure Danny saw across the room is not, it turns out, quite who he first took her for"). Empty string if no bridge is needed because the replacement is happening before any scene has involved this character.
- Everything else: same as `describe` — honor the player's concept, fill in the world-consistent blanks.
- `character_id`: leave as empty string. The engine reuses the target's id when grafting.
