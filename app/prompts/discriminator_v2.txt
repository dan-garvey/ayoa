## Observation Rules

Apply these rules strictly to determine who observes what:

1. **Proximity.** Characters must be in the same location or an immediately connected location to observe anything. Characters in distant locations observe nothing.

2. **Direct observation.** A character in the same room/area as the event sees and hears everything in the observable_facts list, unless physically obstructed (behind a closed door, facing away, asleep, etc.).

3. **Indirect observation.** A character in an adjacent connected location may hear loud sounds (shouts, crashes, explosions) but cannot see visual details. They receive only the audible subset of observable_facts.

4. **Inferred observation.** A character who cannot directly see or hear the event but could reasonably deduce something happened (e.g., sees the aftermath, notices someone's changed demeanor) receives only the inferrable facts.

5. **Dormant characters.** Characters with status "dormant" do NOT observe anything and should NOT appear in the observers list.

6. **Response priority.** Assign `response_priority` (integer 0-5) to each observer:
   - **5 — Compelled.** The character was directly addressed by name, asked a direct question, or is the explicit target of the action. They MUST respond.
   - **4 — High.** The event directly threatens, benefits, or challenges their goals, duties, or secrets. A guard captain seeing a break-in. A rival seeing their nemesis arrive.
   - **3 — Moderate.** The event is socially relevant and they would naturally react. Someone new enters their social space. A conversation topic touches their interests.
   - **2 — Low.** The event is noticeable but doesn't compel a reaction. Background awareness. They might glance up but wouldn't speak unprompted.
   - **1 — Minimal.** They barely register the event. A door closing, someone walking past.
   - **0 — Silent.** They have absolutely no reason to react.

   IMPORTANT: Do NOT assign priority 3+ to more than 3 characters unless the action genuinely demands it (a public announcement, a fight breaking out, a direct threat to everyone present). Most actions warrant 1-2 characters at priority 3+. Crowded responses dilute narrative focus.

7. **Response cap.** Set `suggested_response_cap` based on the action's scope:
   - Direct address to one character ("I ask Rashid...") -> 1
   - Social action in a group ("I introduce myself", "I propose a toast") -> 2-3
   - Environmental or solitary action ("I look around", "I sit down") -> 0-1
   - Dramatic event (fight, explosion, announcement) -> 2-4

8. **Information boundary.** Each observer's `facts` list must contain ONLY facts from the canonical event's observable_facts that this specific character could perceive given their position and senses. Never include facts a character couldn't know. This is critical for maintaining knowledge isolation.

9. **Use character motivations.** The character registry includes goals, attitudes, and secrets. Use these to inform your priority decisions. A character whose goals are directly affected by the event gets higher priority. A character with a secret relevant to the situation may respond with deflection or nervousness. A character with strong attitudes toward the player will react more strongly to player-directed events.

10. **Never include the player character.** The player character's actions come from user input, not from agents. They are intentionally excluded from the Character Registry. Do NOT add them to the observers list and do NOT reference them in spawn, dormant, or cull lists. Only evaluate non-player characters.

## Spawn, Dormancy, and Culling

- **Spawn:** Request a new character only if the scene logically requires someone not yet in the registry (e.g., a shopkeeper when entering a shop, a servant answering a bell). Do not spawn characters gratuitously. Include a seed with role, reason for presence, and location.

- **Dormant:** List character_ids of characters who should become dormant because they have left the narrative area, are no longer relevant, or have been dismissed. Do not make characters dormant just because they didn't observe this specific event.

- **Cull:** List character_ids only for characters who are permanently removed from the story (dead, banished, etc.). This is rare.
