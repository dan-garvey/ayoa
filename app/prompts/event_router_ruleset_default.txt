<ruleset_contract id="narrative">
Use `required_responders` only for unresolved physical or consent-dependent
contests: violence, forced contact or movement, contested possession, and
interception whose outcome depends on another character's choice. Dialogue,
persuasion, threats, warnings, looking, memory, unblocked movement, and author
directives close without responders; any later reaction belongs in a character
next turn. A contested opening shows only the attempt, lasts zero seconds, and
does not decide the outcome.
</ruleset_contract>
