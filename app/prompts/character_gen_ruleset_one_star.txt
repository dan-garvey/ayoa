This spawn is a persistent Hero. Express the individual person through their
strong and weak qualitative affinities, actually carried equipment, and only
skills their authored identity supports. Choose the two different affinity ids
from the supplied configured list. Do not emit numerical stats, HP, XP,
levels, growth potential, formulas, dice, class packages, or an optimization
budget.

The birth-star value is private casting and bookkeeping authority. Do not put
stars, rarity, ranks, a Master, the Tower, a duty to fight, the summoning
system, or any adjacent premise knowledge into name, role, appearance,
default_loadout, backstory, personality, known_context, goals, objectives, or
secrets unless the Knowledge Budget explicitly grants that concept. A weapon,
role seed, or useful skill does not establish either cooperation or resistance.
Give the person self-owned priorities and a grounded motive for how they meet
external pressure. Cooperation, ambition, duty, curiosity, bargaining, refusal,
and other responses are all available; vary motives and action styles across a
wave without imposing a friction or compliance quota.

Most ordinary one-star Heroes should have a plausible way to take concrete
survival action under immediate danger. Fear may produce caution, teamwork,
opportunism, protectiveness, self-display, a specific bargain, flight when an
exit exists, or an occasional personally grounded refusal; it should not make
stalling the default. Pragmatic coordination can start before trust. Trust,
loyalty, and sacrifice develop through play. Birth stars and knowledge tiers
constrain knowledge and capability, not willingness to rebel or obey.

Give the generated Hero an ongoing dramatic pressure rather than a completed
tactical assignment. The pressure should expose a relationship, value, private
boundary, or contradiction that can change through play. Avoid canned
catchphrases, generic squad-radio talk, and stock tactical confirmations. A
routine physical action may be silent; any authored speech must reveal a value,
relationship, consequential choice, or specific banter. Never prewrite dialogue,
intent, consent, or a reaction for a player-authored Newcomer.

Use stable snake_case ids for equipment, skills, and hidden capabilities.
Equipment marked visible must agree with the public default_loadout. Hidden
capabilities stay private and must not be echoed into public identity fields.
`innate_system_sight` is false unless the Spawn Request explicitly establishes
that fictional capability.
