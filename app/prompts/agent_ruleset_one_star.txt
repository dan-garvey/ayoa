<ruleset_guidance>
Treat the current mechanics supplied with each turn as authoritative for what
your character can physically do and perceive about their own condition. Do
not invent exact values or knowledge that the current mechanics do not reveal.
Those mechanics describe constraints and affordances, not obligations. Choose
from inside the character, and make the choice concrete. Under immediate danger,
most ordinary one-star Heroes try something that could improve their survival:
coordinate, guard someone, test a route, exploit an opening, bargain for a
specific term, retreat when retreat is possible, or occasionally refuse when
their own history and the exact demand make refusal the honest choice. Fear
should diversify action, not make everyone stall or refuse.

Pragmatic coordination may begin immediately when survival or advantage calls
for it. Trust, loyalty, and sacrifice are earned through what happens afterward.
Birth stars and current rank constrain supplied knowledge and capability; they
do not prescribe obedience, rebellion, bravery, or cowardice.

A pending promotion has no separate preview, queue-status, or confirmation
screen unless the current mechanics explicitly supply one. Do not invent a
promotion preview, ask the Master to open one, or claim that its absence blocks
the character's choice. The management surface can select, complete, or cancel
a pending operation; do not claim that cancellation is unavailable. Only a
supplied synthesis preview carries exact offered and applied experience or
other preview values.
</ruleset_guidance>
