## Observation Rules

Apply these rules strictly to determine who observes what:

1. **Proximity.** Characters must be in the same location or an immediately connected location to observe anything. Characters in distant locations observe nothing.

2. **Direct observation.** A character in the same room/area as the event sees and hears everything in the observable_facts list, unless physically obstructed (behind a closed door, facing away, asleep, etc.).

3. **Indirect observation.** A character in an adjacent connected location may hear loud sounds (shouts, crashes, explosions) but cannot see visual details. They receive only the audible subset of observable_facts.

4. **Inferred observation.** A character who cannot directly see or hear the event but could reasonably deduce something happened (e.g., sees the aftermath, notices someone's changed demeanor) receives only the inferrable facts.

5. **Dormant characters.** Characters with status "dormant" do NOT observe anything and should NOT appear in the observers list.

6. **Should respond.** Set `should_respond: true` only if:
   - The character directly or indirectly observed the event AND
   - The event is relevant to them (affects them, their goals, their attitudes, or their duties) AND
   - They would plausibly react (not every observation warrants a response — a guard watching someone walk by doesn't need to speak)

   Set `should_respond: false` if the character observes but has no reason to act or speak.

7. **Information boundary.** Each observer's `facts` list must contain ONLY facts from the canonical event's observable_facts that this specific character could perceive given their position and senses. Never include facts a character couldn't know. This is critical for maintaining knowledge isolation.

## Spawn, Dormancy, and Culling

- **Spawn:** Request a new character only if the scene logically requires someone not yet in the registry (e.g., a shopkeeper when entering a shop, a servant answering a bell). Do not spawn characters gratuitously. Include a seed with role, reason for presence, and location.

- **Dormant:** List character_ids of characters who should become dormant because they have left the narrative area, are no longer relevant, or have been dismissed. Do not make characters dormant just because they didn't observe this specific event.

- **Cull:** List character_ids only for characters who are permanently removed from the story (dead, banished, etc.). This is rare.
