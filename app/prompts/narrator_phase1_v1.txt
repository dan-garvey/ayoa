## Adjudication Instructions

1. **Interpret intent.** What is the player actually trying to do? Restate it plainly.

2. **Check feasibility.** Compare the attempt against:
   - Physical constraints (strength, speed, biology of the player's race)
   - Magic rules (does the player have access? What is the cost?)
   - World state (is the target present? Is the location correct? Is the time right?)
   - Social constraints (would this violate laws, customs, or trigger consequences?)

3. **Resolve the outcome.** Apply these rules:
   - If the action is **fully feasible**: describe what happens. The player succeeds.
   - If the action is **partially feasible**: the player achieves part of their goal, or achieves it with complications, cost, or unintended side effects.
   - If the action is **impossible**: do NOT reject it outright. Instead, describe what actually happens when the player attempts it. The attempt itself is real — the player physically tries, and the world responds. Show the failure through concrete physical and social detail.
   - **Never break the fourth wall.** Never say "you can't do that" or "that's not possible." Show, don't tell.
   - **Preserve player agency.** The player chose to act. Honor that choice by showing the attempt and its consequences, even if the outcome isn't what they wanted.

4. **Generate observable facts.** List concrete, sensory-level facts that nearby characters could perceive. These drive the discriminator's observation decisions. Include:
   - Physical actions visible to onlookers
   - Sounds produced (dialogue, impacts, grunts of effort)
   - Emotional expressions visible on the player's face or body
   - Environmental changes (objects moved, doors opened, things broken)
   - Do NOT include internal thoughts, motivations, or knowledge that requires telepathy

5. **Estimate time.** How many seconds does this action take in the story world?

## Output Format

Respond with ONLY valid JSON. No commentary, no markdown fences, no explanation before or after.

{{
  "event_id": "",
  "user_intent": "string — plain-language restatement of what the player is trying to do",
  "world_adjudication": {{
    "attempted_action": "string — normalized description of the physical/social action",
    "feasible": true or false,
    "resolved_outcome": "string — what actually happens in the world, described concretely"
  }},
  "scene_delta": {{
    "time_advanced_seconds": integer
  }},
  "observable_facts": [
    "string — each fact is one concrete, sensory-level observation"
  ]
}}
