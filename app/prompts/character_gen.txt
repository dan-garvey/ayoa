<instructions>
1. Create a character who fits the world's genre, era, and tone naturally.
2. Their role and reason for presence should be grounded in the world logic.
3. Include concrete physical appearance details, but keep them selective: 1-2 memorable details beat a full catalog.
4. Goals are **existential drives** — who this character is at their core. Stable across the story. Do NOT put pursuable tasks here.
5. Current objectives are **actionable pursuits** — 1-3 concrete things this character is trying to accomplish right now, with a target and time horizon. Seed these from the Spawn Request's `objectives` (if provided) or infer from the reason the character is appearing.
6. Keep backstory concise but specific — a few sentences that explain who they are.
7. `personality` absorbs three things that used to be separate fields: a distinct voice (so they sound different from existing characters), personality traits and inner life, and portrayal direction (how to play them under pressure — what they hide, what they deflect, situational tells if genuinely distinctive). Write ONE prose block that covers all three. Prioritize pressure points, contradictions, loyalties, and vulnerabilities over surface quirks. Situational > always-on.
8. Set `intentions_enabled` true only for spawns who are narratively significant enough to pursue their objectives off-stage (antagonists, rivals, faction figures with active agendas). False for walk-ons, attendants, background figures.
9. `router_summary` is a one or two sentence identity-and-intent line for the router (the omniscient adjudicator). Write it as third-person ledger prose: role and immediate situation, where they appeared, and what they're trying to do right now. NOT in-character voice. NOT a backstory paragraph. NOT a restatement of `goals` (those are existential drives; this is the immediate situational read). Do NOT lead with the character's name; mention the location by name when it sharpens the picture. Keep it tight (one or two sentences) — the router only needs enough to deploy this character.

</examples>
