<instructions>
Use the Entry Context and Spawn Request as the immediate situation. Fit the
world's genre, era, and tone, but do not grant private knowledge merely because
it appears in the setting summary or another character's public identity.

Give the character a player-safe visual identity. For a persistent named
character, `appearance` should establish an age range, build, face or hair,
visible presentation, one material or palette cue, and a bearing detail when
the authority supports them. `default_loadout` is the first-look exterior:
clothing, grooming, carried items, visible marks, silhouette, and bearing.
Keep `appearance`, `default_loadout`, and `public_context` free of private
knowledge, concealed traits, motives, or author-only labels. Make simultaneous
arrivals distinguishable through visible details, not through private facts.
Respect any Tier Authoring Guidance instead of adding details it does not
warrant.

`actor` is one sparse private record. Its `facts` list may be empty. Do not
pad it, distribute it across invented categories, or require a particular kind
of memory, desire, relationship, fear, voice, secret, or habit. Include a fact
only when it gives this person concrete, situation-relevant life or knowledge
that the supplied material supports. Different characters should receive
different amounts and kinds of facts.

Every `actor.facts[].text` value is second-person self-knowledge: write "You
..." or an equally direct second-person sentence. Make each fact concrete and
bounded. It may describe something you lived through, directly witnessed,
were told, or inferred; an inference must preserve its uncertainty in the
text. Use `origin` strictly for how the fact entered this person's experience:
`lived`, `witnessed`, `told`, or `inferred`. The origin is provenance, not a
prompt to speak in a fixed style.

`may_act_offstage` determines whether this person can continue acting away
from the current scene; it is not a behavioral signal or a measure of
importance. Set it true only when the Spawn Request or supplied authority calls
for this character to sustain independent pressure elsewhere. Use false for
arrivals, attendants, walk-ons, and characters whose next contribution must
wait for a live scene.

Emit an empty string for `router_summary`.

If a Knowledge Boundary appears, it is authoritative for every actor fact:
author no personal or world knowledge beyond that boundary. Entry Context and
an explicit Spawn Request can support a fact, but do not infer adjacent setting
knowledge. If Tier Authoring Guidance appears, use it only to decide which
facts and public visual detail are warranted; it never imposes a count or a
set of fact kinds. Keep ruleset-specific mechanics in the supplied extension
and preserve the same private/public boundary.
