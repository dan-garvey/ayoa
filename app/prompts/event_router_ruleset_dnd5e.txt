<ruleset_contract id="dnd5e_basic">
Each event draft has one `interaction_mode`:
- `narrative` for ordinary fiction and non-initiative contests
- `dnd_combat_start` when imminent attack, ambush, damaging magic, grapple,
  shove, weapon exchange, or training/sparring makes initiative the governing
  risk
- `dnd_combat_end` when active hostilities clearly end

Combat start facts show only the perceptible trigger. Do not decide attack
rolls, hits, damage, resources, or spell effects; the active combat resolver
owns them. Put established participants in `combatant_ids`. A hostile creature
not already in the roster may use `combatant_spawns` with either an authorized
reviewed `statblock_ref` or one conservative inline stat block, never both.
Named allies and social characters use the ordinary persistent roster instead.

Seed `battle_map_seed` only when established spatial context supports a simple
square grid. Use visible blockers, cover, hazards, and difficult ground; do not
invent tactical detail. Narrative and combat-end events use an absent battle
map seed and empty combatant start fields.

Outside combat, uncertain attempts involving another present character's
belief, attention, concealment, resistance, cooperation, or willingness may
open the ordinary `required_responders` contest. Explicit Persuasion, Deception,
Intimidation, Insight, Perception, Investigation, Stealth, Medicine, Religion,
Arcana, or Nature checks should do so only when another present character's
agency or concealment actually matters. Speech or looking alone closes normally.

Unknown exploration spaces do not authorize a new speaking listener. Prefer
established silence, tracks, airflow, odor, movement, light, hazards, or other
surface evidence. Spawn a persistent non-combat respondent only when reviewed
content or current canonical fiction specifically authorizes that social actor.

`dnd_reaction_ids` is separate adapter work, not a narrative next turn. Name
only direct observers in active combat with a rules-eligible immediate reaction.

`loot_offer` is a choice surface for visible takeable items or currency, not an
already-applied inventory mutation. Use it for opened containers, searched
bodies, explicit rewards, unopposed handoffs, and issued mundane supplies. Do
not use it for hidden contents, unresolved purchases, gear merely handled by its
owner, or unidentified properties the observers cannot perceive. Scope private
offers to exact eligible ids. Otherwise emit the complete absent offer object.
</ruleset_contract>
