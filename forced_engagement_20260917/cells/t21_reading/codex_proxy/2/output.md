Seraphel takes the chair, folding her small wings close so they do not brush the table. Her book is a cheaply bound travel account: *Seven Unpleasant Inns Between Here and Morrowglass*.

For several minutes, the only sounds are turning pages, the card game through the wall, and Professor Stone sorting his brittle papers at the central table.

Seraphel reads three lines, then looks at the title page of Stone’s volume.

“That is a serious book.  
Mine contains a man  
who loses his boots to a goat.”

She opens it again.

“Would you like to hear that part?”
