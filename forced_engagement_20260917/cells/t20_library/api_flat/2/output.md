Professor Stone’s expression tightens slightly.

“Those are sensible questions,” he says. “The Academy covers your room, meals, and course materials. A Conclave student should also receive a stipend. Your estate is separate from that, and the lawyers ought to explain it plainly.”

He slides the brown volume across the table to you.

“Keep this until Thursday. It won’t make you an expert, but it will give you the vocabulary people expect you to have already.”

The laughter from the card game rises briefly through the library wall, then fades beneath the soft scratch of Stone’s papers.

“And when someone tells you a thing is merely custom here, Mr. Garvey, ask whether they mean a rule—or whether they hope you won’t.”
