Professor Stone’s expression shifts only slightly.

“Of course. The letters assume a household has already handled those details.”

He slides the brown volume across at last. A folded card marks the first section in compact handwriting: **Thursday — pp. 1–31: The Human Seat, Mediation, Record, and Consent.**

“The house provides dinner and a room. I can’t tell you what the estate office has promised beyond that. Ask for figures in writing before you let anyone call an expense ‘covered.’”

He turns back to his brittle papers, easing a page flat with the handle of a small knife.

“And don’t thank me for assigning homework. Read the first section, Mr. Garvey.”
