Professor Stone’s eyes flick to the repair at your cuff, then back to the books.

“Food and clothes,” he repeats. “Sensibly immediate. People raised for these rooms tend to forget that questions can have practical answers.”

He slides the brown volume fully across the table. “Keep it until Friday. Pencil in the margins if you must; ink has ruined better books than this one.”

He opens it to the first marked section.

“Start here. Mediation, record, consent. It’s less exciting than duels, but a human delegate’s signature used to matter because people trusted the record behind it.”

His papers go back into their folder one careful sheet at a time. Then he pauses.

“Did the lawyers give you access to any money of your own, Mr. Garvey?”
