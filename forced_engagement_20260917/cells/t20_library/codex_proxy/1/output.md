Professor Stone’s expression shifts, briefly, into something closer to sympathy.

“Those are sensible questions,” he says. “A title does not fill a plate.”

He slides the brown volume across to you at last. “Keep it until Thursday. Read the first section, and make a note of anything that seems designed to confuse you. There will be plenty.”

He gathers his brittle papers into a neat stack.

“The lawyers gave you a room, food, and a signet. Did they give you anyone’s name to contact if something goes wrong?”
