# Covenant of Thrones - delivered player transcript

Exact prose from the durable outbox, in stored delivery order. This excludes hidden NPC activity and internal model diagnostics. Story-update numbers are not player-turn numbers.

## Story update 2

You stand in your room at Garvey House wearing a charcoal coat, its repairs made with care. The inherited Garvey signet rests with you.

## Story update 6

You fasten the inherited Garvey signet over the cuff of your carefully mended charcoal coat. Ink stains remain at your fingers.

Beyond the dining-room doorway, a long formal table waits under its place settings. Ashara vel Kothren sits nearest the door. Farther down sit Ysolde Thornmantle, Caelindra Vaeyn, Seraphel Dawnquill, Thessaly Morrow, Rashid vel Amara, and Aldric Verantus.

You step inside without waiting for an announcement.

Ashara’s gaze travels over your face, coat, signet, and ink-stained fingers before reaching your eyes. She rises from her chair.

“Rowan Garvey. You entered without an announcement. Is there a reason?”

## Story update 8

You give Ashara a small bow. “I wasn't sure whether I was supposed to knock, bow, or wait for someone to find me,” you admit. “Walking in seemed the least likely to leave me outside until breakfast.”

“Rowan. Is there a place I'm meant to sit, or is choosing it my first examination?”

Ashara indicates the chair opposite hers with two fingers. “There. Sit. Choosing would not have been an examination—only a chance to choose badly.”

## Story update 10

You take the chair opposite Ashara. “Then thank you for saving me from my first diplomatic incident.”

You set both hands on the table, ink stains uncovered. “I've spent the afternoon in a house that seems to be holding its breath. I'd rather hear about the people here than the seating rules. What do you all do when nobody is grading you?”

“I cannot answer for everyone in this house,” Ashara says. “As for me, I train or study. The ink on your fingers suggests you write—do you do that when no one is grading you?”

## Story update 12

“Mostly stories. I give imaginary people better lines than I ever manage for myself.” You glance down at your ink-stained fingers, then back to Ashara. “Occasionally letters. Garvey House isn't much of a correspondent.”

You turn toward Rashid. “What about you? What would you do with one afternoon that belonged entirely to you?”

Rashid keeps his hands folded on the table and holds your gaze. “Sleep. Then I’d find out whether I know what to do with an afternoon no one is measuring.”

## Story update 15

“That sounds painfully familiar.” You let the joke fall away. “Come to Garvey House tomorrow afternoon, if you want. No audience and no assessment. We can discover whether either of us knows how to waste an hour.”

You look back to Ashara. “You’re invited too. Though I’m not promising anything worth studying.”

Rashid keeps his hands folded on the table. “Tomorrow afternoon, then. I’ll come. But if you call it wasting an hour, Garvey, be prepared to defend the term.”

Ashara looks from Rashid to you. “I’ll attend tomorrow. I won’t promise to waste the hour, but I’ll refrain from measuring it.”

## Story update 18

You lift your glass toward the table. “Tomorrow, then. To learning something about each other that isn't already written beside our names.”

You drink, settle back in your chair, and leave the next silence unfilled.

Ashara raises her glass and meets your eyes. “To that.” She takes a measured sip and sets it down.

## Story update 20

You glance along the quiet table, then address Caelindra with a tentative smile. “Is dinner always this solemn? I’m beginning to suspect Ashara is the only one allowed to answer.” You tip your glass toward Ashara. “Or have I simply asked very dull questions?”

Caelindra raises her glass a fraction. “Neither, Garvey. Your questions are not dull. I was taught to regard an unguarded answer as a concession; dinner grows solemn when a room cannot tell candor from surrender.”

“Ask another.”

## Story update 23

You lower your glass. “Then I'll risk a concession first. I'm relieved to have a home, and frightened that it belongs to a dead family more than it belongs to me.”

Without the teasing, you address Caelindra. “What do you miss about yours? Not the court or the advantages. Something small you wish you could have brought with you.”

Caelindra looks down at her glass for a moment. “The ease of being silent. At home, silence did not require a defense. Here, it seems to become evidence.”

## Story update 24

“Then I won’t ask you to defend it to me.”

Later, quietly to Caelindra, you say, “I’m going to take some air before I turn in. You’re welcome to walk with me, and you don’t owe me a conversation.”

## Story update 25

Caelindra sets down her glass, rises, and smooths one silver sleeve.

“Very well. I will walk with you. You have made an unusually acceptable offer.”

## Story update 26

You nod goodnight to the table and leave the dining room with Caelindra.

Outside, you cross the Academy grounds with comfortable space between you. Cool night air moves over the open paths. For several minutes, neither of you speaks.

You look along the grounds for a quiet place to sit, keeping clear of closed doors and forbidden ways.

## Story update 27

At the next junction, three open paths divide the grounds. One curves through low gardens flowering despite the season; another follows a lit colonnade toward the main academic buildings. The third slopes toward a broad public terrace, where a stone bench stands beside the open railing above the storm below the Nexus.

## Story update 30

You take the terrace path and sit at one end of the stone bench, leaving the other open. Beyond the railing, the storm turns beneath the Nexus.

For a while, you watch it without speaking.

“I've spent all evening trying not to look overwhelmed,” you say at last. “I think I'll give that up for a minute.”

Caelindra takes the open end of the bench, preserving the space between you. “Then give it up. The Nexus is quite capable of overwhelming people who are not fools.” She watches the storm below. “You need not make a performance of being unimpressed.”

## Story update 32

Caelindra folds her hands in her lap and keeps her gaze fixed on the storm below the terrace railing.

## Story update 33

“Thank you. For the company, and for that.”

You remain on the stone bench a little longer, watching the storm turn beneath the Nexus. Then you wish Caelindra goodnight and leave the terrace without asking her to come farther.

Garvey House receives you in its familiar quiet. You return to your room, lie down, and close your eyes.

## Story update 44

Caelindra inclines her head toward you. “Goodnight, Garvey.”

She remains on the stone bench, watching the storm turn beneath the Nexus.

## Story update 45

The remainder of the night passes without disturbance.

Morning light reaches your room in Garvey House. You wake in your bed.
