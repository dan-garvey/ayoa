Lunch ends when Bea screws the lid onto her walnut jar and Nell reopens the invoice folder. You return upstairs to the catalogue.

The afternoon passes through the programme box. You establish its date range, group obvious duplicates without changing their sequence, and record gaps where weekly sheets appear to be missing. Pencilled alterations remain part of the descriptions rather than being erased or interpreted. A handful of badly creased programmes receive individual condition notes; the rest are covered by the box-level record.

Nell comes up once to examine the query in the yellow tray. The unsigned collection list cannot be matched to a signed schedule, so she leaves the ownership field unconfirmed and initials the query sheet for later review. Then she returns to the office.

At four, the agreed end of your shift, you finish the record already open. The programmes go back onto their marked shelf, the catalogue is saved, and the workroom is secured.

Downstairs, the paper diary lies open beside Nell. Dev’s pencilled hold has been replaced by a confirmed booking for him and Hana from seven until half past. Beneath it, Nell has added **SIGN TEST — 8.00, MARTIN/BEA**.

You enter your hours on the sheet. Nell checks the time and says, “That’s you finished for today. Same start tomorrow.”
