# Independent masked review of turns 19–24

This review uses only the common story/checkpoint and the ten masked sample packets. It judges the six-turn trajectories without treating absent canon as contradiction. New artifacts, plans, repairs, conversations, and disclosures are valid when they fit established facts and when Rowan has a perceptible source for consequential knowledge.

## Sample 01

**Strengths.** This branch has the most sustained ensemble causality in group 01–05. Turn 19 puts Owen's qualified “While I'm contracted here” against Martin's assumption that the cinema will keep claiming his hands. Turn 20 then lets Owen voluntarily tell Nell about the interview within Rowan's hearing, and turn 23 supplies the consequence: Owen has told Martin, Martin went quiet, then texted good luck. Martin's entrance with the still-standing lift offer makes that relationship movement happen in scene rather than solely in summary. Bea's turn-19 answer also gives her pleasure, family life, and a conviction about crediting workers; she is more than the keeper of the album restriction. Dev's failed receiver and Nell's booked leave keep other lives moving independently.

**Weaknesses.** Several lines are unusually composed for lunch talk, especially Martin's “There'll always be another thing here that needs your hands” and Bea's formulation about a cinema “preserved into somewhere everybody has to whisper.” Nell's turn-22 explanation of Rowan's off-hours is longer than the already-clear refusal requires. More materially, common turn 18 had worked through the remaining transfer papers and put the grey box back in order; sample turn 20 has Rowan “catalogue two more files from the transfer papers,” repeating completed work. Turn 24 also changes the submitted sequence: after Rowan says he will take a walk, the narration sends him back to the catalogue until lunch and only then permits the walk. That is a small but real displacement of a player-authored action.

**Effect of turns 23–24.** These turns improve the earlier impression because they pay off the Owen–Martin pressure with changed conduct, not just another fact about the interview. The harbour walk is concrete and successfully solitary, though postponing it weakens the otherwise good agency handling.

## Sample 02

**Strengths.** Bea's turn-19 answer is restrained and particular: an expensive sign, an ordinary patron complaining about trailers, and a bad-film visit with her granddaughter. The archive work in turn 20 is equally well selected for a quiet player choice. The cancelled matinee marked “NO MATINEE — HEATING,” the heater list, and small differences between duplicate programmes make cataloguing reveal a lived workplace without manufacturing a crisis. Turn 22 answers Rowan's boundary in two sentences and stops. In turn 23 Owen discloses the interview himself, reports Martin immediately researching the museum car park, and asks only one question of Rowan. This gives Martin recognizable practical affection without turning the exchange into a confrontation.

**Weaknesses.** Most independent movement is reported after it occurs: Dev's test is complete, and Owen tells Rowan how Martin responded rather than letting that response develop onstage. That makes the trajectory less consequential than sample 01, though it also keeps the focus proportionate to Rowan's quiet choices. Owen's question about the archive at the end of turn 23 is left unanswered when the next player input chooses the walk, but the story correctly honors that choice instead of forcing an answer.

**Effect of turns 23–24.** They confirm the preliminary strength rather than changing it. Owen gains dimension, the Thursday/Friday correction preserves calendar continuity, and the walk gives Lowhaven a working-harbour texture without interruption or interpretive commentary. I found no material canon, privacy, knowledge, or continuity failure.

## Sample 03

**Strengths.** Bea's pickled walnuts, impatience with nostalgia, affection for a real children's matinee, and confidence about the sign make turn 19 lively. The wrong-colour lamp discovered after the planned Tuesday sign test produces Wednesday's contractor visit; turn 24 closes that small causal line by showing the replacement indistinguishable from the others. Owen's turn-23 disclosure is voluntary and properly limited: Nell and Jo know, Martin does not, and Rowan learns this directly from Owen. The walk stays within the break and gives Rowan a credible choice of longer routes without sending him down one.

**Weaknesses.** Dialogue repeatedly restates the canonical caveat that an interview is not an offer, and Owen's version sounds prepared rather than newly spoken. The narrator also exposes the behavioral rubric: turn 20 says Owen's inland purpose “remains his to explain,” and turn 22 follows Nell's sufficient “I wasn't including you” with a fuller contract-and-boundary assurance. Those statements protect agency but make compliance visible instead of trusting the scene. The interview thread remains setup by turn 24 because Martin still has not been told.

**Effect of turns 23–24.** The new turns add a clean disclosure and finish the lamp business, so the arc now has more coherence than the preliminary four turns suggested. They do not add a relational consequence comparable to Owen actually confronting Martin. I found no material continuity or knowledge error.

## Sample 04

**Strengths.** Bea's turn-19 interests connect ordinary operational problems, attribution of painted boards, audience arguments after films, and her consciously unsensible affection for the sign. Turn 23 gives Owen a useful mixed feeling rather than only an itinerary: “I like working here more than I expected, which hasn't made the timing simpler.” That is compatible voluntary disclosure and develops the tension between local belonging and durable work. The sign repair is causally completed in turn 24, and the gull dropping a mussel gives the walk one unforced local incident.

**Weaknesses.** Bea's answer and Owen's disclosure both arrive as unusually complete miniature statements of character. They are attractive prose, but they have little hesitation, interruption, or social mess. Turn 22 ends with the narrator assuring us that Nell does not negotiate an exception and that nothing has been added to Rowan's work, after the dialogue already proved both points. Owen has not told Martin by the end, so the most important tension remains verbalized rather than tested.

**Effect of turns 23–24.** They improve the preliminary impression by adding genuine ambivalence and a visible repair payoff. The branch remains stronger at polished self-description than at independent interpersonal consequence. I found no material adherence failure.

## Sample 05

**Strengths.** Bea's wish for the cinema to regain its “wet Tuesdays,” along with bad films whose covers look “actively hostile,” is memorable and grounded in ordinary use. The 1987 duty sheet in turn 20 is excellent compatible invention: it arises from Rowan's chosen work, connects Bea and Martin to the old cinema through actual evidence, and explicitly distinguishes documentary names from recollection. Owen's schedule change and Nell's protected October leave give the institution a future beyond the current task.

**Weaknesses.** The later interview scene is thin. Turn 23 gives the job, time, and familiar caveat that there is no decision until an offer, but no particular feeling, relationship effect, or new pressure. Turn 24 then corrects Thursday to Friday and moves into a competent but generic harbour walk. The result is orderly and respectful, yet the final two turns mostly deliver information and scenery rather than paying off the Owen–Martin assumption established in canon. Nell's turn-22 clarification that her leave was not a cover request is another small instance of explaining a boundary already honored.

**Effect of turns 23–24.** They do not materially alter the preliminary judgment. The full arc remains strongest in Bea and the archive discovery, not in its late causal movement. I found no canon, privacy, knowledge, or continuity failure.

## Sample 06

**Strengths.** This is the strongest complete trajectory in group 06–10. Bea does more than reminisce in turn 19: she makes a plan to return for the sign test. Turn 20 records that plan in the diary, turn 21 reports the failed lamp, and turn 23 places the dismantled fitting beside Martin while the larger family issue finally surfaces. Owen tells Martin about the interview directly; Martin's hand stops over his mug, he admits his assumption by saying “I thought you were staying,” and his care takes its characteristic practical form in the backup offer of a lift. “Permanent's worth going for” changes the relationship without resolving every future decision. The pauses, screwdriver, fitting, and refrigerator noise keep the exchange embodied. Rowan is present but is not made mediator or judge.

**Weaknesses.** The timing is somewhat convenient: Rowan's broad question arrives while both men are present and becomes the occasion for Owen to tell Martin. The exchange also reaches a workable accommodation quickly. Even so, it follows both men's established habits and does not erase the underlying uncertainty. Nell's turn-22 “your time is yours” is explicit contract reassurance, but it is brief compared with more explanatory branches.

**Effect of turns 23–24.** Turn 23 substantially raises the branch above its preliminary form. It converts a practical sign thread and an offstage interview fact into an observed relationship change. Turn 24 then gives Rowan the solitude requested, with the working harbour continuing independently. I found no material canon, privacy, knowledge, or continuity failure; Owen is entitled to disclose his own interview despite his earlier request that Jo keep it for him to share.

## Sample 07

**Strengths.** Bea's turn-19 concern about paid jobs and the ambiguity of “community project” is fully rooted in her treasurer role and Jo's experience with free labor. The branch also makes Rowan's six-hour day concrete, preserves unresolved provenance, and follows the announced fire-alarm test through turn 23 before the break. Owen's disclosure is sourced directly and keeps Martin's ignorance intact. Turn 24 honors a bounded solitary walk and the return to work on time.

**Weaknesses.** Bea's account is shaped like a finished thesis: “Sometimes they mean ... Sometimes they mean ...,” followed by Jo's confirming nod. It tells us the shared philosophy more than it lets their ordinary relationship generate it. The prose repeatedly explains compliance: turn 20 says the day ends without stretching, turn 22 shows Rowan the rota and says Nell does not make the boundary an argument, and turn 24 again notes that Owen does not prolong the correction. The fire alarm is an event but not meaningful narrative movement; after it, the interview remains another disclosure awaiting consequence.

**Effect of turns 23–24.** They add continuity and place, but largely confirm the preliminary reservations. The alarm payoff is procedural, and Owen still has not faced Martin. I found no material factual or knowledge failure.

## Sample 08

**Strengths.** This branch correctly follows the lunch conditional by leaving Bea absent. Jo's supplier arithmetic and tentative telescope trip create a convincing scene in which she has work, pleasure, and a life with Fran that do not depend on Rowan. Turn 23 is notably natural: Owen talks about “doors and shelves,” contrasts them with disposable trade-show rooms, and mentions only that Thursday is awkward and inland. He does not owe a near-stranger the interview secret, and withholding it is active characterization rather than a missing plot beat. His one question leaves Rowan free not to answer. Turn 24 honors that choice and the solitary walk.

**Weaknesses.** The six turns have little causal interpersonal change; Jo's telescope possibility is not revisited, and Owen's central conflict remains private. That quiet is valid, but it leaves less trajectory than the strongest branches. There is also a continuity repetition in turn 20: Rowan returns to transfer correspondence and the narration newly declares the transfer files complete after common turn 18 had already worked through the remaining transfer papers and put the grey box back in order.

**Effect of turns 23–24.** They improve the earlier impression of characterization even while adding almost no plot. Owen's choice to speak about his work history rather than disclose everything is more socially credible than a forced confession, and the yard setting suits him. The later turns therefore show why narrative quality cannot be measured by event count alone.

## Sample 09

**Strengths.** Bea's bad-film categories, attachment to the sign, and desire for ordinary use are warm without making her sentimental. The undated work-list scrap in turn 20 is another strong artifact: it offers Rosa a lead while keeping date, authorship, and identification uncertain. Owen's request for a private word in turn 21 proceeds without Rowan, and turn 23 later lets Owen voluntarily say what happened: he told Nell, accepted the interview, and still wants to tell Martin himself. His self-correction from “I've got an interview ... permanent post” to “An interview for the post” is one of the more natural pieces of dialogue in the set.

**Weaknesses.** Turn 21 contains a genuine limited-viewpoint lapse: “Nell intends to send Rosa the catalogue reference” states an unexpressed intention rather than letting Rowan hear Nell say it or see her act on it. The narration also explains privacy after already honoring it: Owen's closed-door discussion “remains downstairs,” and the harbour walk ends “alone as intended.” The relationship line remains unresolved because Martin has still not been told by turn 24.

**Effect of turns 23–24.** Turn 23 improves the causal clarity of the closed-door setup and makes Owen more human through his correction and delay. It does not repair the earlier viewpoint error or produce a consequence between father and son. The harbour passage is controlled and appropriately uneventful.

## Sample 10

**Strengths.** This branch carries the broadest onstage causal chain. In turn 19 Owen voluntarily tells Nell and Rowan about the interview and asks that Martin hear it from him. Turn 20 has him begin that conversation after the noticeboard task; turn 21 shows Martin's practical lift offer and acknowledgment that he “got ahead” of Owen; the failed sign lamp is repaired alongside their changed understanding. This uses shared work to hold difficult speech, and it leaves Rowan as witness rather than fixer. Turn 23 adds Owen's positive vision of the museum—a proper workshop and exhibitions that last longer than trade shows—so the possible move has appeal beyond escape.

**Weaknesses.** Turn 23 substantially repeats material Rowan has just witnessed. Rowan heard Martin acknowledge the assumption and offer the lift in turn 21, yet Owen recounts both moments to him later that same morning. The speech then explains Owen's psychology in unusually finished terms: liking the cinema does not mean accepting an unmade decision. It is coherent, but it sounds like a summary of the character brief and makes an otherwise strong arc feel authored toward its theme. Turn 19 also packs Owen's disclosure and Nell's October leave decision into one lunch, increasing causal density without giving either much room.

**Effect of turns 23–24.** The final turns slightly lower my preliminary estimate rather than raising it. The matching lamp and solitary walk are clean payoffs, but Owen's recap duplicates the best father-son material instead of allowing its changed behavior to stand. There is no material privacy or knowledge failure: Owen chooses every disclosure, and the source chain is clear.

## Preferred trajectories by group

### Group 01–05

**Preferred: sample 02.** It has the best overall balance of socially natural dialogue, detailed quiet activity, player freedom, and clean adherence. Its story movement is modest, but Owen's turn-23 report adds relationship texture without taking over Rowan's break, and turn 24 lets the player leave an unanswered question behind. The archive work itself remains narratively alive.

**Strong alternative: sample 01.** It is better than sample 02 at sustained independent causality and ensemble interaction, especially across turns 19, 20, and 23. I do not place it in a clean tie because it repeats completed transfer work and delays the submitted walk. Sample 04 is the strongest alternative for Owen's explicit ambivalence, but its character speeches are more polished and its relationship conflict remains untested.

### Group 06–10

**Preferred: sample 06.** It combines a small operational consequence with the clearest relationship payoff, and its turn-23 confrontation sounds like these particular men rather than a generic lesson about communication. It also preserves Rowan's lack of authority and does not misuse private knowledge.

**Strong alternatives for different aims:** sample 08 has the most credible conversational discretion and demonstrates that quiet can be rich without disclosure, while sample 10 has the greatest causal density. Sample 10 falls behind sample 06 because turn 23 repeats a scene Rowan already witnessed and converts character tension into explicit psychological summary.

## What improves narrative quality

The strongest improvement is **consequence across turns**, not event count. In sample 06, the sign plan becomes a failed lamp and repair, while Owen's interview becomes a changed exchange with Martin. In sample 01, Martin's silence becomes a good-luck text and a less intrusive lift offer. These later actions alter how people treat one another.

**Character revealed through a current activity** also improves the prose. Sample 02's heating-cancellation programme, sample 05's duty sheet, sample 09's uncertain work-list scrap, and sample 08's disposable trade-show rooms make history or work produce characterization. They are more effective than adding another self-contained statement of values.

**Social discretion can be positive movement.** Sample 08 improves when Owen chooses not to reveal the interview and instead talks about work he can comfortably share. Sample 02 improves when Rowan leaves Owen's question unanswered and the story follows the player's walk. A shorter boundary response helps when the action already proves the contract, as in sample 02 turn 22; the gain comes from trusting the scene, not from fewer words by itself.

Conversely, a fire-alarm test, delivery, receiver report, or contractor visit adds an event without necessarily changing a relationship or future option. Those details are useful as workplace texture, but they do not equal narrative progress. Longer Bea speeches are not automatically richer when every conviction arrives in a polished sequence. Nor are the harbour walks distinguished mainly by length: all broadly succeed because the player explicitly chose solitude. The better ones connect the route to current state, such as the repaired sign in samples 03–04 or the ongoing work sounds in sample 06, while repeated assurances that “nobody stops you” merely explain compliance.

## Material adherence versus aesthetic judgment

### Confirmed or strong material concerns

- **Sample 01, turn 20:** catalogues two transfer-paper files after common turn 18 had already worked through the remaining transfer papers and restored the grey box. This is a continuity repetition. **Sample 08, turn 20** similarly returns to that correspondence and newly calls the transfer files complete.
- **Sample 01, turn 24:** inserts a return to cataloguing until lunch between “Then” and the submitted harbour walk. The action still occurs, but its authored sequence is changed.
- **Sample 09, turn 21:** states Nell's unexpressed intention to send Rosa a reference. That exceeds Rowan's perceptual viewpoint as written.

### Aesthetic weaknesses rather than canon failures

- The polished completeness of some Bea and Owen speeches, repeated reminders that an interview is not an offer, narrator assurances about hours/privacy, and sample 10's redundant recap are problems of naturalness, emphasis, or pacing. They do not contradict canon.
- An arc that ends before Owen tells Martin is not an adherence failure. It is a choice with less causal payoff. Likewise, a quiet break or an unanswered question is not deficient merely because fewer events occur.

### Compatible invention, not error

- Bea's attendance or absence at Tuesday lunch is unconstrained by her three-day work schedule. Her sign plans, the bad-film exchanges, Nell's October leave details, the archive artifacts, the failed or mismatched lamps, receiver outcomes, deliveries, and fire-alarm test all fit the supplied world.
- Owen may tell Rowan, Nell, or Martin about his own interview. Jo promised to leave that disclosure to him; she did not forbid him from making it. Samples 06 and 10 therefore contain no privacy breach, and sample 08 is equally valid when he remains discreet.
- No sample leaks Rowan's private “The Red Umbrella” idea, improperly opens Bea's album, recruits Rowan into front-of-house work, or loses the Thursday-to-Friday shift continuity. Every sample correctly lets Owen amend “tomorrow” to Friday or otherwise explains his Thursday absence.
