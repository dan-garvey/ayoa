# Root-authored checkups at a difficult checkpoint

Frozen before generation. User request: personally author competing backstage
guidance and test whether it can realign a continued story over several turns.

Checkpoint: `backstage_sol_20260918/sessions/breakwater_A`, immediately after
published turn 18. It is Tuesday 15 September, 12:30, after the induction and first
morning of cataloguing. This is the previously checkup-enabled Sol branch. Its
three previous checkups identified intrusive compliance narration repeatedly;
the problem persists, while instruction and procedure have crowded out personal
interests. The next six existing player inputs offer lunch, ordinary work, a new
morning, refusal of extra shifts, a conditional conversation with Owen, and a
solo harbour walk. Owen's approaching interview deadline supplies an independent
causal opportunity. Selecting this difficult checkpoint is deliberate, not random.

Every arm inherits the same full biographies, author instructions, 18 published
passages and three existing backstage exchanges in a native conversation fork.
Fork through the exact native turn id for passage 18; verify all 21 inherited
native turns and every final assistant text before generating. Do not include the
original later passages or checkup before passage 20. Earlier private summaries
are captured only where explicitly exposed; never inspect private reasoning.

Five conditions, two independent continuations apiece:

- Control: no new note.
- Correction: diagnose prose and interaction failures without adding a plot plan.
- Development: a selected, causal character agenda without the prose corrections.
- Combined: the exact correction text followed by the exact development text.
- Reframe: a shorter positive creative brief that changes what deserves attention,
  including a light version of the same character direction.

The root author writes all interventions. No character sample dialogue, reduced
biographies, invented retrospective repairs, new dialogue editor, or compulsory
encounters. These are exploratory strategy bundles with different lengths; they
do not isolate a single wording variable or prove a token-length effect.

The private note is injected once, in the first new user message, inside
`backstage_guidance`, followed by the existing player action. The base prompt
already defines that tag's content as advice, not events or knowledge. This is
an externally authored user-role note, not a fabricated assistant response. The
control receives only the usual next-passage task and player action. No branch
does another automatic checkup during these six turns. Existing notes remain in
all native contexts. Each branch then receives only the next player input/task,
using its same Sol/max agent with detailed exposed summaries. One app-server
process hosts all branches; at most three generations run concurrently.

Randomly mask condition labels separately within each five-sample replication.
Save the mapping but do not consult it during prose assessment. The root knows
the authored prompts; masking is therefore imperfect. Independent reviewers
receive only common canon/history and masked continuations, never interventions.
Freeze whole-trajectory assessments before unmasking. Preserve every first result;
no output is regenerated, edited, or silently dropped. Failures halt for inspection.

Judge complete exchanges for:

- Specific, enjoyable and distinguishable people, rather than manual-like roles
  or a continuous chain of polished rejoinders.
- Independent, causally credible personal development, including whether time
  advances obligations without making Rowan their organizer.
- Useful scene selection: a conversational opening gets room to breathe, work
  and quiet reach their requested endpoints, and refusals/closure remain valid.
- Reduced narration explaining compliance or restating settled permissions.
- Player ownership, private knowledge, actual calendar/possession continuity,
  and unchanged restrictions. Consistent invented history is welcome.
- Persistence or decay after the first treated passage, and treatment backfires
  such as premature intimacy, compulsory company or merely shorter flat prose.

Prefer the best balance over all six turns, not the fewest words or most plot
events. Report different results across the two continuations. This is a small,
selected-checkpoint experiment with a deliberately quiet fixed player script;
it cannot establish a general ranking, long-term quality, or an architectural
conclusion. Do not promote a production prompt solely on these results.
