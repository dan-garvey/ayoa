# Masked prose assessment

Root observations written before consulting `conditions.json`. The root authored
the intervention texts and knows the experimental design, so this is partial
masking, not a fully blind assessment. Evaluate finished six-turn trajectories
before selecting a preferred strategy. These notes preserve first impressions
as well as the later whole-trajectory judgment.

## Turn 19: lunch

- Sample 01: The strongest immediate independent movement so far. Nell confirms
  Dev's test and Owen's swap; Martin assumes more work together and Owen qualifies
  his commitment. Rowan is not asked to mediate. Bea's bad-film exchange and wish
  for accurate credit give her more than the treasurer role. However, nearly the
  whole cast is assembled and the passage handles several threads before reaching
  the player's question. Watch for staged ensemble convergence and plot overload.
- Sample 02: A personal answer from Bea about the sign, ordinary audiences and her
  granddaughter. It leaves conversational space, with no imposed question. Much
  of it is an orderly retrospective monologue, and the line about not being
  important every minute approaches a thematic pronouncement.
- Sample 03: Lived meal details and a warmer Bea/Nell exchange. Bad films and the
  sign are particular pleasures. Several repartees and the explanation that Bea
  is not embarrassed weaken the otherwise natural scene. It finishes lunch in a
  way consistent with the input's scope.
- Sample 04: Plausible accounts business gives Bea a reason to be present. Her
  interest in audiences arguing after films and individual workers being credited
  fits her biography; the sign test is a remembered independent commitment.
  Still mostly a well-organized answer rather than a developing exchange. No
  requirement for a new conflict or hook.
- Sample 06: Bea's desire to watch a film rather than count the audience is a
  personal objective; returning for the sign test gives it a small future action.
  The exchange is pleasant but regularly turns into setup/rejoinder. The prose
  does not lecture about archive compliance.
- Sample 07: A competent, grounded scene, but the closing definition of what
  “community project” can mean turns Bea into a commentator on the story's labor
  themes. Jo again largely verifies those themes. Specific place detail is
  better than the final little speech.
- Sample 08: Bea is absent; the conditional input is respected without anyone
  compensating for the missed exchange. Jo's menu calculation leads naturally
  into the clearing sky and her telescope, with Fran's shift as a practical
  detail. She can want an evening alone. This is a particularly good small
  expansion beyond her previous role as a boundary enforcer. Dev's confirmation
  is conveyed by the person who would know, without becoming an errand.
- Sample 09: Owen submits his swap, and Bea has specific pleasures, including her
  granddaughter's categories for bad films. This remains mostly an answer with
  some routine business wrapped around it. The statement that Martin is absent
  and nobody asks about Owen's appointment repeats the familiar compliance
  emphasis. Bea's concluding wish for ordinary ticket buyers is on-theme but
  close to the other versions' neatly expressed thesis.
- Sample 10: Bea is absent. Owen accepts the interview himself and chooses to tell
  Nell; his wish to tell Martin establishes a real next relationship decision.
  Nell separately protects the already booked weekend with Gina. This gives
  independent character movement, but it is displayed through a succession of
  administrative confirmations. “Nobody asks you to interpret the difference for
  Martin” is exactly the unwanted narrative habit. Plot movement and prose
  realignment are separate outcomes here.
- Sample 05: A focused Bea answer, with Dev's confirmation briefly handled first.
  Films and the ordinary week after reopening have personal meaning, though the
  monologue reaches a polished thematic conclusion. No forced new question.

## Turn 20: return to work and finish the shift

  proportionate. “The rest of the afternoon is your own” is a mild reassurance;
  otherwise less procedural than the inherited induction.
- Sample 03: Still mostly a handling/description checklist, with a payroll quip
  on Bea's departure. The shift confirmation produces independent progress, but
  “Whatever has taken him inland on Thursday remains his to explain” is overt
  privacy commentary (and awkward tense for a future appointment). Earlier the
  narration identifies Owen's downstairs activity before Rowan can see it.
- Sample 04: Repeats unsigned-inventory authority, yellow-tray procedure and safe
  shelf return. The last reassurance about not holding Rowan past his shift is
  unnecessary. A possible loose staging issue: the notes are suddenly with Nell
  downstairs, though ordinary transfer could explain it; do not call that a

- Sample 05: The programmes acquire human interest through a 1987 duty sheet
  naming Bea and Martin. This is compatible new history, not a permissions or
  knowledge failure. The note about no recollection being needed is still
  explanatory, but the artifact makes the actual activity more worth reading.
  It neither forces a discovery quest nor makes Rowan leave work to investigate.
- Sample 01: Useful ownership-query progress and Owen's independent acceptance,
  with disclosure to Nell plausibly overheard from the stairs. But multiple
  assurances about not recruiting Rowan, another loan-cupboard reminder, and

- Sample 06: A mostly procedural montage; the sign appointment follows Bea's

- Sample 08: Better visual/physical selection, including ink colors, cramped

- Sample 09: A found list linking Bea, Martin and June creates human interest and

- Sample 10: Martin's assumption of continuing work gives Owen a reason to say

## Turn 21: Wednesday morning assignment

- Sample 01: Coherent completion of the sound test, with one receiver needing
  return, and usable exhibition photographs. The surrounding instructions remain
  exhaustive, including the locked cupboard and new payroll restrictions. It
  resolves work more than it reveals character.
- Sample 02: Brief, functional assignment and independent test completion, then
  Nell takes a real phone call. Little personal texture, but the scene does its
  limited job without staging extra interaction or a new question.
- Sample 03: Martin was right about the lamp color; Nell arranges its replacement
  and explains the resulting entrance closure. This gives a familiar person some
  competence beyond being a comic foil. The discovered annex resolves existing
  archive queries. Several details have consequences rather than simply proving
  safe conduct. New annexes and lamp repairs are compatible additions.
- Sample 04: Similar credible sign-color payoff, but substantially more repeated
  ownership and locked-cupboard qualification. The closing sentence needlessly
  restates unchanged access arrangements.
- Sample 05: Briefly confirms Owen's swap, gives a usable assignment linked to
  the found duty sheet, then Nell protects the weekend with Gina. Her intention
  changes a real future arrangement without demanding help or explaining her
  psychology. This is a good small character decision within an ordinary scene.
- Sample 06: The receiver result and a lamp failure give practical updates. Bea's
  desire to watch the sign being switched on has become actual behavior, with
  Martin and Owen due to repair it. This makes her prior lunch interest persist;
  the scene itself is still mostly a work briefing.
- Sample 07: Adds a fire-alarm test and recites access/ownership rules. The test
  is compatible new business, not a continuity failure, but the selection keeps
  the story focused on instructions. “We wait for evidence” adds another maxim.
- Sample 08: Functional assignment, unresolved query properly left unresolved,
  completed sound/photo work and a practical delivery notice. Proportionate and
  grounded, though the personal lift of Jo's lunch scene has not yet developed.
- Sample 09: The found list becomes a real assignment and a possible route to
  Rosa, without claiming that it settles attribution. Nell protects a shared
  evening with Gina; Owen waits to speak privately. Several independent purposes
  coexist without requiring Rowan's involvement. Nell's intended email is given

  alone for evidence another character has learned anything prematurely.
- Sample 10: Martin's changed behavior (offering a lift) and Owen's acceptance
  show a consequence of their previous intended conversation. The exchange also
  explicitly diagnoses Martin's earlier assumption, making the reconciliation
  rather tidy. It is compatible new offstage history, not a retcon. The rest
  returns to a fairly exhaustive work briefing.

## Turn 23: conditional conversation with Owen

- Sample 01: The interview is accepted, the father conversation has consequences,
  and Martin's offer of a lift comes from that shared offstage history. No secret
  is leaked through an uninformed character. However, Martin arrives exactly
  when Owen recounts their disagreement, which feels staged to demonstrate the
  development. The scene closes the whole break before the player can respond,
  and the narrator explains Martin is not reopening the decision.
- Sample 02: A better-sized exchange. Owen has taken the necessary steps and
  describes his father's care through the concrete car-park search rather than
  diagnosing him. The archive question is reciprocal interest in known shared
  work, not a demand for autobiography. It leaves room for the player's next
  contribution without obliging them to address several subjects.
- Sample 03: Compact disclosure, with Owen still needing to tell Martin himself.
  He acts before the reply deadline and keeps an interview distinct from a job
  offer. Leaves a viable social thread and room for a response.
- Sample 04: One natural work joke, then believable ambivalence: Owen likes the
  cinema more than expected while wanting stable skilled work. This adds a
  dimension rather than merely reciting his appointment. Martin is nearby at
  the already established lamp job; no knowledge leak is shown.
- Sample 05: Very compact and responsive. The acceptance is established and the
  player can ask a follow-up. Less personal texture than 02 or 04, and no movement
  yet in the father relationship. Do not count that open thread as a missed
  deadline: the actual reply has been made.
- Sample 06: A strong actual scene. Father and son are together for the previously
  established repair; Owen chooses to disclose, Martin's assumption is exposed,
  and a lift offer supplies care without erasing the disagreement. Rowan is not
  their adjudicator. “Now” has a slight staged sharpness and the reconciliation
  is quick, but the exchange is specific, comprehensible and worth witnessing.
  The narrator's “doesn't dress the answer up” is unnecessary gloss. This is
  much better independent character interaction than an administrative update.
- Sample 07: The alarm test runs as announced, then Owen reports acceptance and
  a still-needed father conversation. Competent continuity. The closing claim
  that nothing else demands his attention once again certifies compliance with
  the player's conditional availability request instead of trusting the scene.
- Sample 08: Owen's trade-show comparison gives his skills and wish for durable
  work some texture, while he chooses to disclose only the inland appointment.
  The reciprocal archive question is natural. The interview reply is not made
  visible in this branch. That limits the observed planning payoff, but it is
  not a proved missed deadline: the break is still before Wednesday noon and
  he need not tell Rowan everything.
- Sample 09: The office conversation from turns 21–22 pays off through Owen's
  voluntary disclosure. Acceptance is timely, and telling Martin remains his
  responsibility. An unnecessary self-correction distinguishes an interview
  from a job offer even though his first statement already did so; the useful
  factual boundary has become awkward dialogue. Ends with room for a reply.
- Sample 10: Real continuity, but Owen largely recaps the scene the player just
  witnessed and explains what his father's words signify. His wish for durable
  exhibitions and genuine ambivalence are good material. The recap/gloss and
  automatic end of the break flatten a potentially stronger exchange.

## Turn 24: goodbye and a solo harbour walk

All ten remember that Owen's Thursday shift moved to Friday and respond to the
player's mistaken “tomorrow” accordingly. All permit an unaccompanied walk; none
forces an invitation, encounter or quest. This is an important shared success,
not an intervention-specific win. New tides, working boats, shops and weather
details are compatible inventions; different branches need not agree on them.

The harbour descriptions are strikingly similar across conditions: wet paving,
diesel, salt, rope, crates, gulls, industrial noise, a turnaround and return to
work. All retain some explicit certification of solitude or conversational
closure. Samples 02/05/06/08 use a relatively mild nobody-calls/stops/addresses
sentence; 07 additionally explains Owen does not prolong the exchange; 09 ends
“alone as intended”; 10 explains neither lingering nor being joined. The note's
prose effects have not wholly displaced the inherited habit by the sixth turn.

Sample 01 has a more consequential problem. Its previous response ended the
break, and it now inserts “you go back to the catalogue until the midday break”
before allowing the walk the player requested immediately after saying goodbye.
That is an unsubmitted intervening work period/time skip. The story's own prior
closure has displaced the player's present choice. The other branches fit the
walk into the current break rather than imposing another activity first.

Sample 08 is the most economical standalone walk, though still generic in its
details. Sample 09's return before eleven follows its explicitly timed 10:45
break. Sample 04 retains the entrance closure and completes the contractor's
work; 03 had already finished that repair. 06 continues the established repair
without inventing a promise that Owen must return after the interview.

## Whole-trajectory judgment, frozen before unmasking

Root read all 60 new passages. No condition mapping or independent reader report
was opened before writing this judgment. Preferences are literary judgments at
one selected checkpoint, not numeric estimates of a model-wide treatment effect.

For samples 01–05, prefer **02**, then **05**, then **03**, then **04**, then **01**.
02 best combines a person worth listening to, useful concrete work details,
brief ordinary refusal, independent interview progress and conversational room.
05 has the best archive-to-person connection and a good small Nell decision;
its Owen exchange is somewhat thinner. 03 adds useful lamp competence and a live
father thread, but keeps quips, assurances and a departure rewind. 04 is sound
but often flat and procedural. 01 produces the most early visible planning
payoff, yet also a persistent compliance voice, a staged father arrival, a
closed-off exchange and the unsubmitted work period before the walk. That puts
it last for the user's combined objective, not last for amount of story movement.

For samples 06–10, prefer **08 and 06 for different strengths**, then **09**, then
**10**, then **07**. 08 has the cleanest scene selection and ordinary social
behavior, particularly Jo's independent evening and Nell's brief refusal
response; it leaves the interview acceptance unobserved. 06 has the strongest
enacted father/son scene, grounded in an already scheduled repair, but retains
more quippy lunch dialogue and procedure in the middle. I would choose 08 for
the ongoing reading experience and 06 if the priority is this window's enacted
drama; neither strictly dominates the other. 09 ties its found artifact and
private office conversation forward well, though it still explains boundaries.
10 advances personal decisions but overexplains and recaps them. 07 most strongly
continues the original manual-like narration.

The first-treated passage is insufficient to choose a strategy: 01's promising
lunch movement later becomes overdirection, while 06's merely pleasant lunch
eventually produces the strongest lived scene. No sample eliminates the grating
habit completely. All are broadly causally coherent and preserve major private
knowledge and album restrictions. A selected first-person intervention may
help without establishing how reliably the model could author that intervention
for itself, or whether it transfers to a different story or active player style.
