# Assessment

The implementation works as a periodic private advisory call. It is not an
established literary improvement. These are short, unblinded smoke tests without
an author-only control; prior Covenant prose comes from the existing proxy playtest.

## Covenant

The checkup correctly spots important patterns in the supplied history: Seraphel's
unrhymed dialogue, excessive questioning, premature approval of ordinary competence,
and the need to preserve private character information. Its development suggestions
keep Ashara's practice and Dena's card game independent of the protagonist. It allows
a quiet night rather than inventing an encounter. The notes are 469 words and contain
some repetitive scene bookkeeping and a debatable minor criticism of narration
about the protagonist's focus.

The first response answers the practical question and closes without a fresh
invitation. Stone lends the volume and returns to his work. The next player input
pulls out a chair and returns to reading. Since this branch did not introduce
Seraphel, Stone takes the invitation. He then proposes an exercise and asks the
player to identify a troublesome sentence. That revives the extra-demand pattern
despite the guidance; the run is not a reliable pacing cure.

The explicit chapter-reading request is honored next. Stone lets his question go
unanswered, returns to work after the interval he announced, and the chapter ends
without another demand. Some dialogue still contains constructed jokes or maxims.
Seraphel does not speak in these new passages, so the checkup's rhyme reminder was
not behaviorally tested. The notes alone are not evidence of improved verse.

## Breakwater

Both checkups correctly separate the player's assumption of an imminent screening
from established facts: the public reopening is later. They preserve restricted
knowledge about Owen's interview and the competing auditorium plans. Both following
responses let the player join supper without making them responsible for staff work.

The initial checkup invents a source conflict. The canon says Nell *entered* a
booking on Friday 11 September for use *today*, which the opening state establishes
as Monday 14 September. It does not say the room use or Dev's receiver test occurs
on Friday. The checkup nevertheless recommends withholding the auditorium conflict
until the supposed date problem is repaired. The author does not mention Rosa's
reservation in its immediate response; this alone cannot show a causal effect.

After the generic timing-verification instruction is added, the repeat reads the
dates correctly and retains the auditorium overlap as an available development.
The author mentions Rosa's reservation without revealing Dev's unknown plan.
This is a useful counterexample to the false alarm, with only one sample of each
prompt. Neither checkup catches the opening's small elapsed-time error: Nell showed
the room at 17:45 and the opening is at 18:15, but it says twenty minutes ago.

Dialogue in both responses still stacks several polished remarks, including food
jokes and a quick exchange about the historical record. Distinct interests survive,
but the extra call has not solved the quip problem.

## Runtime evidence and limits

- Nine of nine new calls completed, comprising three checkups and six author calls.
  Only the final Breakwater repeat uses the final checkup prompt; all frozen versions
  remain available. No retries or discarded samples were used.
- Every new request reconstructs exactly from its frozen sources, active history,
  current submission and latest saved notes. Final prose matches the raw author
  response. Model and effort remain Terra/max with detailed exposed summaries.
- Notes are absent from normal chat data and published transcripts, and visible
  only in the explicitly enabled inspection surface or operator request handoff.
  Exposed summaries are not fed back as guidance.
- The real calls exercise one checkup per run and reuse of guidance across several
  turns. Repeated periodic cycles, regeneration and recovery are covered offline.
- Checkups took approximately 68–140 seconds in these proxy runs; the author then
  ran sequentially. These are observations from this environment, not API latency
  or cost estimates.

Longer controlled playtests should measure factual review precision, correction
quality, persistent effects on character development, quiet/active pacing and
whether old advice remains appropriate as the player changes direction.
