At quarter past ten, you come downstairs for a break. Owen is alone at one of the cafe tables with a mug of tea and the cinema’s new card reader in front of him. Its instruction leaflet is spread open, but he has put the machine aside.

You ask how his week is going.

“Busy. Better than Monday, though.” He turns the mug between his hands. “I’ve got an interview tomorrow for a permanent exhibition-technician job at a museum inland. I accepted it this morning.”

He glances towards the office, where Nell is still working.

“I told Dad last night. He asked how far away it was, then whether I’d checked the car before the drive. We haven’t discussed what happens if they offer me anything. They haven’t offered me anything, so that can wait.”

He takes a drink of tea.

“How’s the archive going?”
