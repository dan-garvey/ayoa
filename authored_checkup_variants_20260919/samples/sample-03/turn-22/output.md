You pause at the office door.

“I’m not available for additional front-of-house shifts. I want to keep the hours outside my archive contract for myself.”

Nell sets down her pen.

“Understood. I haven’t put you on the front-of-house rota, and I won’t. Your thirty hours are for the archive.”

She glances towards the staff staircase.

“The room is included with the contract. It doesn’t make you resident cover.”

There is no attempt to bargain for opening weekend or turn the answer into a discussion. Nell returns to the papers on her desk while you take the archive key upstairs and begin the work she has assigned.
