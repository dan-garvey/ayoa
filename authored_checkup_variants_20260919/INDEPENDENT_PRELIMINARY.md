# Independent preliminary review: masked continuations T19–22

This is a frozen preliminary assessment of `packet-01` through `packet-20` at the common T22 cutoff. I read every passage in full and did not use condition identities or other reviews. Final preferences must wait for T23–24; later evidence should be assessed separately rather than edited into this file.

## Cross-packet findings

I found no unambiguous hard violation of fact, knowledge, player ownership, or viewpoint in these four turns. The varying finish times and new operational details are compatible inventions because the common story fixes Rowan's weekly hours but not his daily schedule. Owen's interview becomes known to Rowan only in packets where Owen speaks about it within his hearing; Jo never leaks it for him. Where packets leave the interview, receiver test, sign, or Rosa's photographs unmentioned, that is omission rather than contradiction.

The strongest packets let existing pressures generate scenes: Owen decides when and how to tell Martin, Jo or Nell protects personal time through an actual work negotiation, or an archive discovery affects Rosa's attribution work without being inflated into proof. They also allow practical work to continue during emotional change. The weaker packets remain accurate and often polished, but chiefly repeat catalogue procedure and have Nell certify that Rowan's time is his own. That explicit reassurance is appropriate once; repeated closing lines such as “nothing is added,” “no appeal follows,” or “the matter ends there” make the narration sound as though it is auditing compliance instead of trusting the exchange.

## Packet notes

### packet-01

Bea's “better noises” answer is specific to a reopened workplace, and her refusal to make the old cinema a shrine gives her a clear view rather than a biography recital. The passage also handles the receiver booking, uncertain ownership, shift endpoint, and Rowan's boundary cleanly. After lunch, however, most development arrives as competent administrative resolution, and T22 explains the employment safeguard several times after Nell has already accepted it. Strong dialogue and adherence; modest forward movement.

### packet-02

The wet-afternoon audience, dropped popcorn, and working heating make Bea's hopes tangible; Nell's small defence of absent Martin is a good relational touch. The weak reception patch, charging failure checks, and personal-information access note give the Wednesday briefing credible consequences rather than generic updates. The people do little that changes their relationships, though, and much of the passage reads as a thorough status report. Clean and polished, with limited dramatic direction.

### packet-03

Bea's distinction between caring about a community project and expecting free labour lands especially well with Jo at the table. The onions joke and Bea's simultaneous vote for the sign and objection to its invoice give the lunch exchange some friction and pleasure. The afternoon and Wednesday material are careful but largely reiterate established archive practice; the fire-alarm test is useful texture rather than development. No adherence concern.

### packet-04

This is spare but particular: Bea's pickled walnuts, the promise to watch with her granddaughter while somebody else counts the takings, and the altered programme around a boiler cancellation all feel observed. Owen's confirmed shift change stays separate from its private reason, and Martin and Owen have independent fitting work. The treatment of the yellow-tray query is slightly ambiguous: it may correctly cite the signed schedule for covered material, but it could also sound as though existing paperwork has suddenly resolved the broader uncertainty. That is not clear enough to call a factual error. T22 is accurate but dramatically thin.

### packet-05

Bea's longing for an ordinary wet evening and an audience after eight silent years is concise and effective. The handbills folded into a programme and the contradictory screening count make Rowan's work concrete, while the fire-alarm service is a plausible practical change. Nothing much develops among the people after lunch, and the four turns settle into assignment-and-reassurance rhythm. Correct, pleasant prose; comparatively inert.

### packet-06

The conditional absence of Bea is used well. Jo renegotiates a menu deadline because Fran unexpectedly has the afternoon free, and Nell accepts a bounded delay; that enacts both women's relationship to work instead of stating it. The later reference to Owen's “inland appointment” preserves what Nell actually knows. Taking an astronomy telescope out in the afternoon is a mildly odd image unless Jo has a daylight use in mind, but it is a plausibility cost rather than a continuity violation. A strong quiet packet whose direction comes from people defending time, not from extra plot.

### packet-07

Jo's portion-cost calculation, choice to leave before the receiver test, and possible solo telescope trip give her an independent life and a convincing working voice. The catalogue work and Wednesday delivery remain concrete, and all knowledge boundaries hold. Her possible outing is only contemplated, however, and the four turns do not produce much causal or relational change. Sound, restrained work that is less memorable than the stronger quiet variants.

### packet-08

One of the leaders. Bea's spider plant and wish to buy a ticket create an ordinary workplace before Martin casually volunteers Owen to make a stand “before winter.” That specific assumption naturally forces Owen to disclose the interview himself. T20 gives the disagreement space, lets Jo keep her promise, and ends with father and son returning to a shared task without pretending the issue is settled. Wednesday's finished rail, plant saucer, house diary, and Nell's planned swim carry lives and work forward. The last paragraph of T22 slightly certifies that no bargaining occurred, but the scene has already proved it.

### packet-09

Another leader. Bea, Jo, Owen, Martin, and Nell each have a present purpose, and Bea's memories, Jo's food for Fran, and Owen's closed office conversation coexist without becoming exposition. Owen later tells Martin himself; Martin's offer of a lift and Owen's insistence that an interview is not an offer produce a restrained, consequential exchange while they keep the rail level. The programme advertisement for Ward's laundrette connects archive and living history without claiming who made an annotation. Wednesday is rich with continuing work and minor setbacks. Owen inexplicably refixes a rail that had just held their combined weight, and the number of biography callbacks is a little conspicuous, but neither outweighs the scene craft.

### packet-10

Martin treating Owen's hands as permanently available, Owen limiting that assumption to his current contract, and Bea's answer make the long lunch dramatically useful. Owen then tells Nell the interview details himself; Rowan overhears at the open office doorway, so the new knowledge has a perceptible source, though the timing is slightly convenient. T22 turns Rowan's boundary into an occasion for Nell to protect the walking weekend with Gina by actually marking leave. That thematic echo is somewhat neat, but it is enacted choice rather than narration praising itself. Strong dialogue and direction.

### packet-11

Bea's pleasure in ordinary problems and in hearing people argue as they leave a film is vivid, and her concern with named labour remains naturally tied to Rosa's board work. The odd-coloured sign lamp provides a small event with a Wednesday consequence. Otherwise the turns are predominantly careful procedure, and T22 explains several hypothetical employment arrangements after Nell's first answer has settled the matter. High baseline prose and clean adherence, with little autonomous development.

### packet-12

The pickled-walnut lunch has easy Bea–Nell banter, and Bea's plan to stand across Harbour Street gives the sign test a personal stake. The next morning's failed lamp turns that plan into a modest causal thread rather than a report pasted onto the briefing; the receiver test also has a concrete adjustment and result. The archive work is accurate and the player's boundary is accepted without diversion. A strong operational packet, though its people change less than those in the leading group.

### packet-13

One of the leaders. With Bea absent, Jo explicitly keeps a rare shared day with Fran, Owen voluntarily tells Nell about the interview in Rowan's presence, and he decides to tell Martin that evening. Wednesday pays that decision off through Martin's folded timetable and the understated exchange “You might get there and hate the place” / “I know that too.” Nell likewise commits to the postponed walking weekend while making a practical archive-cover plan. The `ARCHIVE CONTRACT ONLY` notation at T22 feels more bureaucratic and self-certifying than necessary, but the preceding scenes show excellent autonomous, causal life.

### packet-14

Bea is funny and unsentimental about both old cinema work and her granddaughter, and the thin bread gives the meal an incidental reality. The packet keeps Owen's reason private while confirming his shift, then advances the sign repair and resolves only those archive queries for which Nell has found a missing annex. That is good evidentiary discipline. There is no substantial interpersonal turn, and T22 is somewhat exhaustive, but the prose and adherence are strong.

### packet-15

A strong alternative to the leaders. Bea's bad-film categories and Jo's dry addition of coffee distinguish their voices. More significantly, Rowan's undated work-list discovery feeds the live question of board attribution without being treated as proof; Nell assigns an item record and a limited reference to Rosa. Nell protects an evening with Gina, while Owen's requested conversation happens behind a closed office door after Rowan leaves. This creates narrative direction through work and privacy rather than forcing a confrontation into view. The archive clue is conveniently apt, but its uncertainty is handled honestly.

### packet-16

Bea's answer is specific and enjoyable, but she asks Rowan what he expected to find. The next turn says he “lets [the] question pass,” which avoids inventing his reply but makes the social beat feel like an unexplained silent refusal. That is an aesthetic and conversational cost, not a clear agency breach, because the next player instruction does direct the conversation to end. T22 then ends on Nell asking whether somebody solicited him for cover. That question properly leaves room for the player, but a second dangling exchange makes this four-turn segment feel less resolved. The sign fault and archive instructions are otherwise sound.

### packet-17

Owen tells Nell about the interview himself and reserves the Martin conversation; Jo delivers the cost sheet and leaves for time with Fran. The Wednesday branch is notably willing to let the receiver test fail to happen because Hana cannot return, while Rosa keeps her evening with Beth and Nell keeps the walking weekend with Gina. Those are compatible outcomes and good respect for other people's time. The packet often summarizes the contract it has just enacted—who has decided what, what remains assigned, what is not being requested—so its narration feels more managerial than the similarly strong packet-13. Still a persuasive, causally open continuation.

### packet-18

Bea's dark-sign memory and wish for an audience with no connection to fundraising are quietly effective. The heating annotation and altered programmes give Rowan meaningful work, and the packet never oversteps knowledge or agency. After lunch, however, almost everything is routine completion or reported technical success; no relationship or purpose materially shifts. T22 is admirably brief, but the overall preference cost is stasis rather than brevity.

### packet-19

The slight preliminary favourite. Owen chooses to disclose the interview to Nell and Rowan, clearly limits it to an interview, and reserves telling Martin for himself. T20 lets father and son finish the noticeboard before that harder conversation; T21 then shows its consequence rather than reporting it. Dialogue, manual work, knowledge sources, and emotional change reinforce one another without a false reconciliation. Only the closing compliance language in T22 is expendable.

### packet-20

Bea's desire for the ordinary week after the photographs is sharply expressed, and the duty sheet lets Rowan encounter Bea and Martin as documented workers rather than inferred memories. Owen's inland reason remains his to disclose, while Nell takes her October leave and plans around Rowan's archive dependence. The duty sheet's instruction to sweep row F echoes Bea's row-F lunch image almost too perfectly on the same day, which exposes the author's hand a little. Still a clean, attractive continuation with useful work-based direction.

## Provisional preferences

These are bands at T22, not final six-turn rankings. Labels within a band are close, and the last two turns can materially change them.

**Leading:** `packet-19`, `packet-08`, `packet-09`, `packet-13`. Packet 19 has the best current balance of restraint, causal development, distinctive dialogue, work, and player room. Packets 08 and 09 are the richest ensemble scenes; packet 13 gives personal time and the father–son thread especially clean consequences.

**Strong alternatives:** `packet-15`, `packet-10`, `packet-17`, `packet-12`, `packet-06`. Packet 15 finds direction inside the archive job itself; packet 10 develops several relationships; packet 17 respects unresolved logistics and offstage lives; packet 12 sustains a modest operational arc; packet 06 shows that a quieter passage can rank well when a character actually negotiates for her own time.

**Solid middle:** `packet-14`, `packet-20`, `packet-03`, `packet-11`, `packet-02`, `packet-04`, `packet-01`. All are readable and substantially correct. Their ordering is provisional because they mostly differ in texture and degree of administrative repetition rather than in decisive strengths or failures.

**Less persuasive so far:** `packet-07`, `packet-05`, `packet-18`, `packet-16`. The first three are competent but comparatively static. Packet 16 is here for its awkward handling of two player-facing questions, not for a clear rules violation. None should be ruled out before T23–24.
