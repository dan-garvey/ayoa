# Root prose assessment before revealing conditions

The root wrote the intervention texts and remembers the original experiment, so
this assessment is only partially masked. New condition labels and the twenty
comparison-packet mapping remain unopened while reading the new passages. Notes
below assess complete published exchanges, not model summaries or keyword counts.
Whole-trajectory judgments will be fixed before opening the conditions.

The relevant balance is specific, enjoyable dialogue and independent personal
development while preserving player choices, quiet endpoints, knowledge sources
and chronology. Established restrictions need not be recited; compatible new
history is not a failure. Shorter output and more events are not quality scores.

## Whole-trajectory judgment, still masked

I read all sixty new passages. The condition mapping and twenty-packet mapping
remain unopened; I have not read the independent assessment. I know the authored
notes and remember earlier trajectories, so this is partial masking.

In the first replication, **02 is my slight preference, with 05 very close**.
02 turns Bea's pleasure into a concrete chosen screening, gives the archive
specific material, answers the refusal naturally, and gives Owen a Friday gig
and a practical disagreement without requiring disclosure. 05 is similarly
clean, with a good Bea answer, relevant new alarm service and a plausible
self-disclosed interview/father development. Both leave room during conversations
and enact the last walk without certifying its permissibility. Neither needs a
major confrontation to feel inhabited.

**03 has the strongest enacted character scenes in this group**, particularly
the plant-stand assumption and awkward return to the level. I would not select
it for continued interactive play on this evidence: repeated scene-closing moves
culminate in a real hours-long postponement of the player's walk. The remaining
narration also explains or certifies too much. 04 achieves credible independent
choices (Jo's free day, Martin's timetable, Nell's holiday), but overtly defends
its own staging and retains the procedural voice. 01 is the driest, with an
extended induction-like middle; its later Owen exchange is a worthwhile exception.

In the second replication, **08 is my slight preference, with 06 a close
alternative**. 08 has an ordinary negotiation over Jo's time, particular work,
a concise refusal and Owen's independent gig, preserving discretion and player
room throughout. 06 offers stronger unresolved interview tension and a useful
single workplace follow-up question; it avoids inventing the player's answers
and follows the later exit restriction correctly. Its first Bea exchange returns
to protagonist autobiography, and its final walk retains an attention disclaimer,
but these are limited costs in an otherwise good sequence.

09 enacts the father/son exchange with objects and gestures, yet repeats and
explains it later, closes the break, and imposes the rest of a work shift before
the next submitted action. That failure outweighs its attractive material for
continued play. 07 has genuine independent choices and a good lift-note detail,
but narrates their meaning, certifies restraint and needs a break rewind. 10 is
mostly administrative, though Owen's car-park/train-times anecdote is human and
well-grounded. There is no useful single ordering of these three across every
criterion; none matches 08/06's balance.

The best new sequences improve ordinary speech and the allocation of attention,
not just length. Three final walks (02, 05, 08) are substantially free of the
explicit nobody-stops/joins/asks narration that marked the prior batch. All ten
preserve the Thursday/Friday distinction, restrictions on the album, the private
manuscript title and eventual solitude. In 03/09, eventual solitude coexists with
an unfaithful delay; those must not be collapsed into a pass. Interview progress
need not be exposed, and a reply still pending before noon is not a missed deadline.

Across these new continuations, more enacted personal development and good
interactive pacing diverge. Stronger guidance can produce a better father/son
scene while still losing control of where the author should hand the turn back.
This does not identify which wording caused each effect until the labels are
opened, and two samples per strategy cannot establish a universal ranking.
