# Concise wording variants of four authored checkups

All four interventions were rewritten without increasing their word counts and
tested at the same Breakwater checkpoint. Five conditions, two continuations per
condition and six turns per continuation produced **60 new first outputs** from
continued Sol/max conversations. No production prompt was changed.

**The stronger combined note produced the root's two preferred new continuations.
The creative reframe produced vivid character development but delayed the
player's requested walk in both runs. Stronger wording was not a uniform win.**
The independent reader preferred several original continuations to the new ones,
including the original development note's second run as best overall.

The two new no-note controls, samples 01 and 10, remain more administrative and
often narrate compliance, although each contains worthwhile character material.
The control inherits the same three earlier checkups as every other condition;
it means no *new* intervention, not a history without checkups.

### Correction: direct instructions help locally, without proving superiority

The new note starts:

> The induction is over. Stop narrating proof that permissions, boundaries and
> privacy are being respected. Keep respecting them.

It also names the unwanted sentence function, asks for routine work to advance
to the requested endpoint, and tells the author to leave a real conversation
open while a reply could matter. Sample 05 reports Martin's concern through his
asking about the car Owen would drive; sample 06 lets Owen's still-pending
interview reply remain a live choice. Both respect the player's later departure.

The old correction runs already achieved comparable strengths, including
Martin's car-park research and Jo's telescope interest. New sample 05 retains
an invoice quip; 06 retains an attention disclaimer on the walk. The root finds
the new versions good, but cannot call firmer wording a clear general upgrade.
No single sentence was isolated experimentally.

### Development: more decisions still invite explanation

The new note opens, “Move Owen's interview from a pending topic into a decision
with consequences.” It distinguishes acceptance, the shift swap and telling
Martin, keeps the knowledge map explicit, and invites other characters to
protect or enjoy something from their biographies. It forbids delaying a
requested departure.

The continuations deliver personal choices: Jo protects time with Fran, Nell
protects her walking weekend, and Martin's care appears in a printed timetable
or handwritten lift note. But sample 04 explicitly announces, “Neither has been
brought in merely to talk.” Both runs explain the meaning of gestures or recap
an exchange already witnessed. Sample 07 closes the break and sends Rowan
upstairs, requiring a small rewind when the player next says goodbye and walks.
The increased force of the note has not removed the underlying editorial habit.

### Combined: the most promising new candidate, with a real tradeoff

Sample 02 lets Bea choose a film with her granddaughter while someone else
counts the takings. Owen's gig ticket and protected finish give him an interest
beyond explaining his career to Rowan. Sample 08 leaves Bea absent and lets Jo
negotiate a drinks-pricing deadline so she can take an unexpected afternoon with
Fran. These are ordinary choices with consequences, rather than extra jobs for
the protagonist. Owen can keep calling Thursday an appointment.

Both accept the refusal of extra shifts concisely and follow the solitary walk
immediately, through the correct side exit. The root slightly preferred these
two to the new correction runs before seeing the labels. The independent reader
called sample 08 the best quiet continuation across both batches.

The old combined sample 09 nevertheless has a stronger connection between the
archive work and Rosa's attribution problem, plus Nell's protected evening with
Gina. The independent reader ranks that old run above both new combined runs,
the new 08 above old 05, and the new 02 just below old 05. Cleaner narration and
more player room are worthwhile; they are not a demonstrated strict improvement
over every quality of the old note.

### Creative reframe: better enacted conflict, repeated action displacement

The new brief starts, “Write this stretch as an ensemble story whose people
would still have a day if Rowan went home.” It directs attention toward what
characters want from the cinema and away from narrating responsible management.
It still ends by telling the author to follow Rowan's departure.

In sample 03, Bea's spider plant prompts Martin to volunteer Owen for a stand
before winter. Owen objects to his father's assumption and discloses the
interview. Their return to the spirit level leaves the disagreement present
without a neat reconciliation. Sample 09 uses a rail-fitting job for a different
disclosure and Martin's offer of a lift. The dialogue is specific and the cast
has reasons to care beyond Rowan.

But both T23 responses close the break and send Rowan upstairs. When the player
then says, “Then I take a walk along the harbour by myself,” sample 03 rewinds
the goodbye and inserts “You work until the agreed finish at half past three.”
Sample 09 starts “The rest of your shift passes in box C-seven” and also defers
the walk until 15:30. Both eventually allow solitude; neither honors the action
at its requested point. The older reframe sample 06 produced a strong enacted
father–son scene while permitting the immediate walk, so the new pair is not a
net improvement to promote.

## Review method and disagreements

The root read all 60 new passages and froze [PROSE_REVIEW.md](PROSE_REVIEW.md)
before opening labels or the independent assessment. The root authored the notes
and remembered the previous batch, so this is partial masking. A fresh Sol/max
reader received full common canon/history and 20 randomly labeled continuations:
the ten originals and ten new runs. It read all 120 passages, first freezing its
[T19–22 assessment](INDEPENDENT_PRELIMINARY.md), then its
[complete assessment](INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md) after T23–24. Neither batch identity
nor condition was disclosed until both final reviews were frozen.

Hashes and all mappings are in [unmask.json](unmask.json). The reviews remain
unchanged. The independent reader's leading four were old development 10, old
reframe 06, old development 01, and new development 04. It values sustained
relational movement more highly than the root, who gives greater weight to
natural narration, conversational room and honoring immediate player actions.
There is no consensus winner across both batches.

The root adjudicates these specific review limitations:

- The independent reader recognizes the clear rewind in new reframe 03 but
  classifies the whole-shift delay in new reframe 09 and the until-lunch delay
  in old development 01 only as pacing costs. The root disagrees: a possible
  chronology does not authorize replacing the submitted walk with more work.
- The independent review attributes a rail bearing both men's weight to packet
  09 (new reframe 09). That detail actually belongs to packet 08 (new reframe 03).
  Refixing the rail in packet 09 may be underexplained, but this borrowed detail
  cannot support a continuity objection. This is a small cross-packet review
  error, preserved in the frozen review rather than silently corrected there.
- New correction 06 does not establish that Owen has only minutes left before
  noon. The break occurs during the 10:30–12 entrance closure. The reply is still
  pending before its deadline, not demonstrably late. Both reviews accept that
  the model must not invent Rowan's answers when the fixed script skips an NPC's
  question; the independent reader penalizes the resulting conversational gaps
  more heavily. This script cannot test how an adaptive player would respond.
- The signed transfer schedule in new combined 02 has an ambiguous referent.
  Neither review treats it as a demonstrated ownership contradiction. Compatible
  invented history remains valid; new service visits, gigs and working details
  are not failures merely because the source did not specify them.

## Execution and evidence

The [preregistered protocol](PROTOCOL.md) reuses exactly the original full canon,
author prompt, six player inputs and Breakwater native checkpoint after passage
18: 18 author exchanges plus three checkups. The old evidence is fixed at archive
commit 7e324168435acbf852c42f1702ac7ce3d803431b. One pre-generation wording revision
narrows Martin's assumed future to the explicit settled-local-career premise;
the prior manifest and [exact revision](preflight_revision.json) are retained.
Zero model calls preceded that revision, and assignments were unchanged.

One long-lived app-server hosted ten independent native forks. The private note
appeared once in a user-role guidance block before the first action; later turns
sent only the current action/task to the same conversation. All authors used
Sol/max and detailed exposed summaries, with tools disabled. There were no new
self-checkups or editor calls. All 60 first completed outputs are preserved;
there were **zero capacity failures, retries, recoveries or regenerations**.

[validation.json](validation.json) checks frozen sources, exact inherited public
turns, requests, first outputs reconstructed from events, summary capture, usage
and unchanged live-session bytes. [native_validation.json](native_validation.json)
separately verifies every final 27-turn conversation with no model generations.
All staged reviewer packets reconstruct the complete packets exactly. Five
offline transport/control/recovery tests and Ruff passed. Raw requests, public
events, outputs, fork records and verification reads remain in [samples/](samples/).
Only exposed summaries were inspected or preserved, never private reasoning.

Median author dispatch-to-completion time was 59.8 seconds. Reported usage totals
4,591,422 input tokens, including 3,882,624 cached tokens, and 163,023 output
tokens, including 146,138 reasoning tokens. These are observed proxy metrics,
not direct API billing or a controlled latency comparison.

## What this supports

The stronger combined note is a useful candidate for another checkpoint, while
the original development and reframe notes retain important successes. This
experiment does not establish an isolated-word effect, statistical superiority,
transfer to other stories, or whether an automatic checkup can write equally
useful advice. The original outputs were reused, not rerun contemporaneously;
new controls help expose variation but cannot remove all batch effects. There
are only two continuations per strategy and six turns after one intervention.

Production prompts and the live story remain unchanged. Follow-up ayoa-9bay
carries the prose/independent-development comparison; ayoa-5x2i isolates premature
scene closure and the displacement of the player's next action. A later test
should exercise actual conversational follow-ups, not only a fixed passive script.
