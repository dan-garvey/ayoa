# packet-07

## Turn 23

Player: If Owen is around during a break, I ask how his week is going. If he is not, I take my break without looking for him.

Midmorning, you leave the 1987–90 programme box open at a stopping point and go downstairs for your break.

Owen is in the service yard beneath the lean-to, sitting on the low step with a mug beside him. A tape measure remains clipped to his belt, but his tools are closed in their case. Through the open side door, a newly fitted shelf is visible beneath the cafe counter.

“How’s your week going?” you ask.

“All right. Mostly doors and shelves.” He rubs a smear of sawdust from his sleeve. “It’s better than trade-show work. You could spend three days building a room and watch somebody tear it down before you’d been paid for it.”

He drinks some tea.

“Tomorrow’s a bit awkward. I’ve got to be inland in the morning. Nell’s moved my shift to Friday, so that part’s sorted.”

A delivery van reverses somewhere in the lane, its warning tone sounding between the buildings. Owen moves his mug away from the edge of the step.

“How are you finding the archive?”

## Turn 24

Player: If we have been talking, I tell Owen I will see him tomorrow. Then I take a walk along the harbour on my own.

“I’ll see you tomorrow,” you tell Owen.

“Friday,” he says. “I won’t be here tomorrow.”

He picks up his mug as you leave the yard.

You go down the lane and cross Harbour Street towards the inner harbour. The rain has left the paving dark and the iron railings bright with water. Diesel, salt and wet rope sharpen the air.

The tide is high enough to lift the fishing boats close to the quay. Two crew members sort plastic crates beside a blue-hulled trawler while its engine idles. Across the basin, a grinder sends brief orange sparks from a boat raised in the yard. The sound reaches you a moment later, thin and metallic.

You follow the quay past the chandlery and the lifeboat station. Beyond it, a public-footpath sign points along the coast towards the headland. Gulls crowd the roof of an ice store, falling silent together when a forklift emerges beneath them.

Nobody stops you. You walk as far as the harbour mouth, where the water changes from the sheltered brown-green of the basin to a harder grey beyond the breakwater. Then you turn back, completing the circuit before your break ends.
