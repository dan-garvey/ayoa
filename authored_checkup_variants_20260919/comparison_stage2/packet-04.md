# packet-04

## Turn 23

Player: If Owen is around during a break, I ask how his week is going. If he is not, I take my break without looking for him.

At midmorning, the drilling stops. You come downstairs for your break and find Owen at one of the cafe tables with a mug of tea and a spirit level beside it. Two black poster frames lean against the foyer wall. Martin is in the service yard cutting narrow wooden packing strips.

“How’s your week going?” you ask.

“Busy. Tomorrow’s appointment is on, and Nell’s moved my shift to Friday. That part’s sorted.”

Owen glances towards the nearest frame.

“The wall bows out in the middle. Dad wants to pack the bottom corner; I think the top fixing needs moving. We’re trying both before either of us puts another hole in the plaster.”

He drinks some tea.

“I’ve told Nell I still finish at four on Friday. There’s a band playing that night and I’ve already got the ticket.”

He sets down the mug. “How’s it going upstairs?”

## Turn 24

Player: If we have been talking, I tell Owen I will see him tomorrow. Then I take a walk along the harbour on my own.

“I’ll see you tomorrow,” you tell Owen.

“Friday,” he says. “I’m not here tomorrow.”

You leave by the side door while Martin and Owen return to the display frame.

Harbour Street slopes towards the inner harbour. The rain has passed, leaving the paving dark and the gutters running. You walk beside the quay on your own, past coils of green rope, stacked fish crates and a row of boats tugging against their lines.

Two crewmen rinse the deck of a trawler with a hose. Farther along, a forklift carries shrink-wrapped pallets towards the packing depot. Diesel hangs in the air beneath the cleaner smell of salt and wet stone.

The route curves around the harbour office. Beyond the wall, the sea is iron-grey, with white water breaking against the outer rocks. You follow the railings as far as the end of the inner quay, then turn back.

The walk takes the rest of your break. As you return through the cinema’s service yard, the drilling begins again inside.
